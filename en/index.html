· FAQ

Straight answers.

Is matrix marketing a grey-area / black-hat tactic? +
No. Matrix is what every serious DTC brand, MCN and Web3 studio already does—Shenzhen operators have been running it for years. The only difference: Aivamax gives you the tooling to do it safely, at scale, from a single console.
Will my accounts get banned? +
Every account runs in an isolated native fingerprint environment with its own residential proxy. The Account Doctor monitors health signals and auto-adjusts cadence before any platform flags you. Customer data: 98.7% account health, quarter average.
I don't know English / industry copy. Can the AI really write for my niche? +
Yes. Give it your product spec, target market, and a few sample messages—the AI adapts tone and produces industry-specific B2B copy in 20+ languages.
How is this different from Hootsuite / Buffer? +
Those are scheduling tools—they wait for you to click. Aivamax is a digital workforce: agents think, post, reply, mine leads, and hand off to each other on events. One operator can do the work of a 50-person team.
What if I don't have overseas accounts yet? +
We can provide warmed, fingerprinted accounts, or guide you through buying them from trusted channels. Onboarding includes account warm-up and proxy binding—you go from zero to matrix in about a week.
Where are my data and accounts stored? +
Public cloud (Singapore / Frankfurt) by default. Enterprise plans support private cloud and fully on-prem deployment.
