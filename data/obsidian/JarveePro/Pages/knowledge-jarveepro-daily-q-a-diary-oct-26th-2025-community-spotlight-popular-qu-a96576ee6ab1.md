Introduction

Keeping social media accounts safe while maximizing engagement is a constant challenge. JarveePro is continuously evolving to provide smarter automation solutions across platforms like Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and Twitter. This FAQ addresses the latest updates on warmup features, multi-platform automation, and how JarveePro helps users simulate human-like behavior to protect accounts and optimize performance.

Q1: Will JarveePro add automatic warmup features for Facebook and Instagram?

We’ve received many requests for automatic warmup on Facebook and Instagram. This feature is currently under consideration for future versions to enhance account safety and automation balance.

Q2: Can TikTok get a warmup function too?

Yes, TikTok warmup automation has been requested as well. It’s being reviewed as part of our upcoming roadmap for multi-platform behavior simulation and risk control.

Q3: Does JarveePro currently support Instagram warmup?

Not yet. Instagram warmup automation is still in development planning. Users can currently optimize posting intervals and delays for safer activity simulation.
