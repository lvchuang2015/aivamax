Q1: Is the ChatGPT comment prompt or syntax wrong?

Answer:

No — the final prompt logic used is correct and well-structured.

The rules applied were solid and appropriate for Facebook automation:

Short comment (1 sentence, 12–15 words)

Calm, practical, experienced tone

No emojis, slang, hype, or sales language

Context-aware and human-sounding

Clear behavioral constraints

Result:

Once properly configured, the generated comments behaved as expected and posted correctly.

Q2: Was the user’s original prompt formatted correctly?

Yes — the user’s original version was incorrectly formatted and needed adjustment.

❌ Incorrect (Original Prompt Used by User):

This is the post content: {post content} and {post link}.

Write a short Facebook comment (1 sentence, max 12–15 words) from the perspective of a local mature adult owner of the business

The tone should be calm, practical, and confident, experienced, not directly promotional.

No emojis, no slang, no hype, no sales language. Avoid generic praise or buzzwords.

The comment should add a grounded observation, agreement, or measured positive perspective that directly fits the post’s context. It must sound human and unscripted, never automated.

return them in JSON format as {"comment content"}

❌ Issues with This Version:

{post link}

is unnecessary and adds noise

JSON output is

not required

for JarveePro AI Comment tasks

Extra formatting instructions increase instability

Output constraints conflict with natural-language generation

✅ Corrected & Recommended Prompt:

This is the post content: {Content}.

Write a short Facebook comment (1 sentence, max 12–15 words) from the perspective of a local mature adult owner of the business.

The tone should be calm, practical, confident, and experienced, not directly promotional.

No emojis, no slang, no hype, no sales language. Avoid generic praise or buzzwords.

The comment should add a grounded observation, agreement, or measured positive perspective that directly fits the post’s context. It must sound human and unscripted, never automated.

✔️ This version is

clean

stable

, and

fully compatible

with JarveePro AI Templates.

only one Facebook account

one comment per post

No duplication will occur

Multiple comments require multiple accounts

This behavior is

intentional

safe

, and

platform-compliant

Q4: What are the best practices to avoid bot-like behavior?

Answer:

Recommended settings for a

trusted, older Facebook account

Under

50 comments per day

Set

hourly limits

Use

realistic delays

Avoid aggressive interaction stacking

Keep comments natural and non-promotional

These limits help:

Maintain account health

Reduce automation detection risk

Ensure long-term stability
