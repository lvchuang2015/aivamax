## Content

Watch Video/Short

2025-06-14

Step 1: Choose "Campaigns" and YouTube, and select task.

Step 2: Select account(s) and add video/short source(s). You can import multiple video/short sources you want to watch in CVS format or add them one by one.

Step 3:

What This Page Does:

This tab is used to configure how YouTube videos are watched by each account—how many videos to watch, how often, how long, and whether to engage (like, subscribe) during or after playback.

If You Want to Simulate Watching 500 YouTube Videos (One Per Account)

Select "Number of Videos Each Account Play"

Set it: From 1 to 1

Do NOT select "Number of play each video increases"

In Select Accounts: Import 500 YouTube accounts

In Video Source: Import 500 video URLs

(Optional) Enable:

Like After X seconds

Subscribe After X seconds

In Account Use Interval(s): From 1 to 1 (can adjust to add delay between accounts)

Effect: Each of the 500 accounts will watch 1 video. Total: 500 plays.

If You Want 1 Video to Get 500 Plays (Using Multiple Accounts)

Select "Number of Play Each Video Increases"

Set it: From 500 to 500

In Video Source: Import just 1 video URL

(Optional) Use:

Limit video play time (default is 30s, can set 1–30s range)

Like or Subscribe after certain seconds (tick the box and set delay)

In Task Settings, adjust Account Use Interval(s) if needed

Effect: The same video is played by 500 accounts. Total: 500 plays.

Engagement Settings (Optional but Useful):

You can choose to perform actions after playing the video for X seconds:

Like After: e.g., after 3 seconds

Subscribe After: e.g., after 8 seconds

These options simulate real engagement to make the view more natural.

Real-World Example (Boost 1 Video with 500 Plays and Like After Watching):

Select Accounts: Import 500 accounts

Video Source: Add 1 video URL

Task Settings:

Tick “Number of Play Each Video Increases”

Set 500–500

Tick “Like After” and set it to 3 seconds

(Optional) Tick “Limit video play time” to simulate shorter watch time

Click OK → Schedule
