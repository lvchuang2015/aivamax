JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary – March 8th, 2026 | Can JarveePro Post to Facebook Groups Automatically?

2026-03-08

Every day, social media marketers ask questions about how to use

JarveePro

to manage multiple accounts and automate their campaigns. In this diary series, we share real conversations with users to help others understand how JarveePro works and how it can solve common social media automation challenges.

Today’s question focuses on

managing multiple Facebook accounts and posting automatically to Facebook groups.

Q1: Can I log in with multiple Facebook accounts in JarveePro?

Yes. JarveePro allows users to manage multiple Facebook accounts from a single dashboard.

Depending on the plan you choose, you can add a specific number of accounts.

For example:

The

Business Plan

allows up to

30 accounts

Higher-tier plans allow even more accounts

This makes JarveePro ideal for marketers, agencies, or businesses managing multiple social media profiles.

Q2: Can JarveePro automatically post to Facebook groups from a list of group links?

Yes, it can.

JarveePro allows you to create automated posting campaigns where you provide a

list of Facebook group links

, and the software will automatically publish posts to those groups using your selected accounts.

This feature is particularly useful for:

Affiliate marketers

Local business promotion

Community engagement campaigns

Traffic generation strategies

Q3: Can JarveePro find Facebook group links automatically?

Yes.

JarveePro includes an

Advanced Content Explorer

that can help discover relevant Facebook groups based on keywords or niche topics. Once you collect the group links, they can be imported into your posting campaigns.

Q4: What plan should I choose if I have 8–10 Facebook accounts?

If you manage

8–10 accounts

, the

Business Plan

is usually sufficient since it supports up to

30 accounts

This makes it a good option for:

Small marketing teams

Freelancers managing multiple clients

Affiliate marketers running several accounts

Q5: Can I test JarveePro before buying?

Yes.

Users can start with the

free version

or join the official

JarveePro Telegram channel

, where free licenses and tutorials are sometimes shared.

This allows new users to explore the software and see if it fits their workflow before committing to a paid plan.

Managing multiple Facebook accounts
