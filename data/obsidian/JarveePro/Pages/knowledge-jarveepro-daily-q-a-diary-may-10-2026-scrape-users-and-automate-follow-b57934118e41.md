Introduction

Social media automation is still one of the most debated topics in digital marketing, especially when it comes to scaling accounts safely and efficiently. In today’s JarveePro Daily Q&A session, we explore real user questions about follower automation, scraping, proxy usage, and smart account handling strategies. These are practical, real-world concerns from marketers trying to balance growth with safety in 2026.

Q 1: Is it possible to automate follows by providing a custom user list?

Answer:

Yes. With JarveePro, you can automate follows using a custom user list. This means you can upload or input targeted usernames and the system will follow those accounts automatically based on your settings.

This is especially useful for:

Targeting niche audiences

Competitor audience engagement

Building a highly relevant follower base

The key is to ensure your list is clean and relevant. Poor targeting = poor engagement, even with automation.

Q 2: Is it possible to scrape users by providing a list of usernames to scrape followers from?

Yes, JarveePro allows you to scrape users by providing a list of usernames. The tool can extract followers from those accounts, helping you build a targeted audience pool for future engagement campaigns.

This method is commonly used for:

Competitor audience research

Influencer audience targeting

Building niche-specific lead pools

However, scraping should always be used strategically to avoid low-quality audience collection.

Additional Conversation Insight (Proxy Handling Discussion)

The discussion also covered proxy usage strategies:

Using a single proxy across multiple accounts is possible

Sequential account handling can be configured

Matching actions with proxy rotation timing is supported

However, using one proxy for many accounts increases risk if not managed carefully

A more balanced setup is always recommended for long-term stability.

Summary

This Q&A highlights key automation capabilities in JarveePro, including follow automation via custom lists, user scraping for audience building, and flexible proxy configuration. While the tool offers strong automation power, success depends heavily on smart targeting and safe operational setup. In 2026, automation is less about “can you do it” and more about “how intelligently you do it.”
