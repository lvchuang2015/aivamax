JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary — Dec. 24th, 2025 | Facebook Like Post: Why I Have 112 Accounts but Only 71 Success?

2025-12-24

Introduction

Running a Facebook Like Post campaign can look confusing at first — especially when the number of accounts is higher than the number of successful likes.

Question

I ran a

Facebook Like Post

campaign with

112 accounts

The result shows

71 success

and

1 failed

Where are the other accounts? Why didn’t all 112 accounts like the post?

Answer

This behavior is

normal

and depends entirely on

how the Post URLs are set up

how likes are distributed across those posts

JarveePro does

not

force every account to like every post.

Instead, it works like this:

1. Likes Are Distributed Per Post URL

In your campaign settings, you defined:

Likes Allowed per Post:

20 → 34

This means:

Each post URL will receive

between 20 and 34 likes

Once a post reaches its allowed limit,

JarveePro stops assigning accounts to that post

If you add multiple post URLs, JarveePro spreads accounts across them —

not all accounts are used on every post

2. Total Accounts ≠ Total Likes

You had:

112 accounts

Multiple post URLs

A per-post like limit

So the math looks like this:

Some accounts are used until posts reach their limits

Remaining accounts are

not needed

, so they are skipped

Skipped accounts are

not failures

That’s why you see:

71 success

1 failed

The rest are simply

unused for this task

3. What Counts as “Failed”?

An account is marked as

Failed

only if:

It tried to like a post and couldn’t (login issue, checkpoint, restriction, etc.)

Check logs here to see why?

If an account was never assigned a post because limits were reached, it is

not counted as failed

4. What Is a “Post URL”? (Very Important)

Post URL

is the direct link to a specific Facebook post.

Correct examples:

https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=XXXXX&id=XXXXX

https://www.facebook.com/username/posts/XXXXXXXX

Incorrect examples:

Page URLs

Profile URLs

Group URLs without a specific post

Homepage links

Each

Post URL = one post that can receive likes

How to Use All Accounts

If you want

more accounts to be used

, you can:

Add

more post URLs

Increase

Likes Allowed per Post

Run multiple Like Post campaigns

JarveePro follows your limits strictly — it does not over-like posts.

Summary

JarveePro distributes likes

based on your settings

Not all accounts are required if post limits are reached

Unused accounts are

normal

, not errors

“Failed” only means an account actually tried and failed
