Q4: When I click Delete on a category that contains accounts, are the accounts removed completely? Will any caches or cookies remain on the PC?

A4:

Deleting a category does

not

instantly wipe everything.

Here’s how it works:

Accounts in that category are removed from JarveePro.

Cookies are preserved

for re-use, unless you explicitly clear them.

When you launch a new browser session, JarveePro will

auto-clear old cookies

to prevent conflicts.

This design ensures accounts can be re-used if needed, while also keeping your system clean when starting fresh.
