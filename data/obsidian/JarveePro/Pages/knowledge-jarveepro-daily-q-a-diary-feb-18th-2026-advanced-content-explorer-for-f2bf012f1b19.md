Q2: When you scrape an account’s followers using Advanced Content Explorer, can you scrape that scraped list again using keywords to get a more targeted list?

At the moment, this type of chained scraping (scrape followers → then scrape that same list again using post keywords) is not supported.

You can:

Scrape follower lists

Perform keyword-based scraping

Use different data sources independently

However, you cannot currently apply a secondary keyword-based scrape directly on a previously scraped follower list.

For more targeted results, consider starting with keyword-based scraping first, then refining based on engagement behavior or account activity.

Summary

Today’s Q&A clarified two important points:

AI comment generation tutorials are available and fully supported through Advanced Content Explorer.

Multi-layer scraping (scraping a scraped follower list with keyword filtering) is not currently available.

As JarveePro continues evolving, advanced targeting logic and workflow flexibility remain key areas of development. For now, strategic keyword-first scraping remains the most effective targeting method.
