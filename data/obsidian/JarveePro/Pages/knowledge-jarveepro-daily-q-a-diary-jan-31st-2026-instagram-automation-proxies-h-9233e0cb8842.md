Introduction

This Q&A diary captures a real pre-sale and onboarding conversation with a user looking to scale Instagram automation using 50–100 accounts while closely replicating human behavior. The discussion covers proxy usage, account safety, pricing, scraping scenarios, and manual browser access—common concerns for advanced Instagram automation users transitioning from tools like Twitterdub.

Q1: Can JarveePro be used mainly for Instagram automation?

Answer:

Yes. JarveePro fully supports Instagram automation and allows users to focus on a single platform if needed. Tasks include liking, scrolling, scraping, commenting, replying, and other engagement actions.

Q2: Can JarveePro manage 50–100 Instagram accounts simultaneously?

Yes. JarveePro allows users to manage

unlimited Instagram accounts

under a single platform license. The system is designed for large-scale account management.

Q3: Can JarveePro replicate human behavior such as scrolling and liking posts?

Yes. JarveePro supports automation that closely mimics human behavior, including scrolling feeds, liking posts, and performing actions with configurable delays and schedules to reduce risk.

Q4: Can JarveePro be used with proxy addresses?

Yes. JarveePro supports proxy usage and is fully compatible with residential, mobile, and

IPfarming proxies

. Proxies are strongly recommended when managing multiple accounts.

Q5: What is the pricing for Instagram-only usage?

Answer:

JarveePro offers a

single-platform lifetime license

for Instagram at

$500

, allowing unlimited Instagram account management.

Q6: Does JarveePro log into accounts and operate them automatically?

Yes. JarveePro logs into each account and executes tasks based on user-defined campaigns, schedules, and settings.

Q7: Is there a free trial available?

JarveePro provides a

free version with limited functionality

, primarily auto-like features. Full automation features require a paid license. Users may also access free licenses through official community groups.

Q8: Can users view all features before purchasing?

Answer:

Yes. Users can explore available features through the knowledge base, demo videos, and by testing the free version to understand the interface and capabilities.

Q9: Does JarveePro sell Instagram accounts and proxies?

Yes. Instagram accounts are available for bulk purchase. Example pricing includes

$50 for 100 Instagram accounts

. Proxy solutions such as IP farming proxies are also recommended for account management.

Q10: Is the risk of bans high when using these accounts?

Ban risk primarily depends on

usage behavior

, not the accounts themselves. Safe automation practices, realistic limits, proper proxies, and gradual scaling significantly reduce risk.

Q11: Can accounts be used for scraping data like views and likes?

Yes. Any Instagram account can be used for scraping public data such as views, likes, and engagement metrics. Dedicated scraper accounts and stable proxies are recommended for best results.

Q12: Can users open a browser session for an account without running a campaign?

Answer:

Yes. JarveePro allows users to open a

Chromium browser directly from Account Manager

, enabling manual control of the account without running any automation tasks.

Summary

This interaction highlights JarveePro’s suitability for high-scale Instagram automation, including:

Managing 50–100+ accounts

Proxy-based operation

Human-like engagement automation

Scraping public Instagram data

Manual browser access per account

JarveePro provides flexibility for both fully automated campaigns and hands-on account control, making it a strong alternative for users migrating from tools like Twitterdub.
