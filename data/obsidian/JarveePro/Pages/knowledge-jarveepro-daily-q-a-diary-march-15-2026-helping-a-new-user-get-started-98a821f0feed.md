Q3: How do I schedule 5 Instagram posts per day for 4 days in JarveePro?

Scheduling Instagram posts inside

is simple once your Instagram account is connected.

Follow these steps:

Step 1 – Add Your Instagram Account

Inside the campaign:

Go to

Accounts

Select your

Instagram account

Add it to the campaign

Once connected, your account will appear in the profile list.

Step 2 – Create a Post Campaign

Go to Campaigns

Open

Instagram

in the left panel

Click

Create Campaign

(top center)

Select

Post

This opens the

Instagram Post Campaign setup

Step 3 – Create Your Posts

Select Accounts

In

Do this:

Expand the group where your Instagram account is located

Check the box next to the account(s) you want to post from

Click

Next tab → Post Text

Important settings on the right side:

Recommended:

Use as many accounts as possible

Sequential

Posts Allowed per Account:

1 to 1

This means

each account posts one post at a time

Add Caption (Post Text Tab)

Go to:

Post Text

Add your caption.

Example:

Caption 1

Caption 2

Caption 3

Or use

Spintax

like:

{Amazing|Great|Awesome} content today 🔥

Upload Content

Select Photo/Video

Upload your

20 posts

Why 20?

5 posts per day × 4 days = 20 posts

JarveePro will post them one by one.

Step 4 – Configure Posting Schedule

To publish

5 posts per day for 4 days

, you can:

Option A – Fixed Time Schedule

JarveePro will publish posts at certain times.

Option B – Interval Posting

You can also configure intervals:

Example:

Start posting at 09:00

Interval Hours 1--means every 1 day.

Repeat this schedule for 4 days.

Extra Tip

When managing multiple accounts, it is recommended to:

Use

one proxy per account

Keep posting behavior natural
