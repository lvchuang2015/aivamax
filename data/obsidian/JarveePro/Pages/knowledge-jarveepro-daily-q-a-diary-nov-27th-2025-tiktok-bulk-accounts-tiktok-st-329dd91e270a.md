The Winning TikTok Method (Proven Across Niches)

Post 1–3 high-quality videos daily

This is the current “safe sweet spot” for both new and aged accounts.

Use different angles of the same content idea

TikTok’s algorithm rewards variation, not volume.

Always refresh your content style every 3–5 days

New hooks = more reach. Simple rule.

Keep early engagement high

Replies + likes + re-watches in the first 30–60 minutes boost ranking.

Avoid posting 10+ videos a day

That looks spammy now — TikTok tightened the filters in late 2025.
