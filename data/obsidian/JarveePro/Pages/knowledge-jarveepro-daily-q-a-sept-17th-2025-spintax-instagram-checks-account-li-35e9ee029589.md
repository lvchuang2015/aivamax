Spintax in JarveePro

Q:

How do I use Spintax for posts and comments?

A:

Use curly brackets

{}

with options separated by

. Example:

{Hello|Hi|Hey} everyone! {Check this out|Take a look|Don’t miss this}

JarveePro will randomly spin text for natural-looking posts/comments.

JarveePro Plans & Accounts

Q:

How many Facebook accounts can I add in the Individual Plan?

A:

The

Individual Plan

allows

1 account only

Does the Free Version allow unlimited accounts?

Yes, but with limited campaigns. Some actions will prompt you to upgrade.

Why does my free campaign tell me to buy?

Only specific campaigns are included for free. See

YouTube Guide

In the Individual Plan, does it run one thread at a time or only one account?

It runs

one account only

. For multiple accounts/threads, use

Enterprise or Full Version

Q:

Example: I have 60 accounts posting in 20 groups each. Which plan do I need?

A:

You’ll need at least

Enterprise

(unlimited accounts) or the

Full Version

for best performance.
