## Content

Select Post

2025-07-19

Step 1: Open JarveePro→Click Reddit→ Choose"Open Reddit"→Click "OK" to start.

Step 2: Add and select account(s). Then choose model that you want. And add information according to your 2nd step. Then choose number of post you want to select and choose type, time. Lastly, click "Start" to start search. If you want to perform task, you can click "Fast Task" to start.
