Instagram's Official Follow Limits

Total Follow Cap

Instagram enforces a hard limit of

7,500 total follows per account

Source:

Instagram Help Center – Follow Limit

JarveePro Solution:

Use the

“Unfollow Tool”

Unfollow after 3–7 days

Set limits per hour and per day to stay safe

Run unfollow tasks during off-peak hours
