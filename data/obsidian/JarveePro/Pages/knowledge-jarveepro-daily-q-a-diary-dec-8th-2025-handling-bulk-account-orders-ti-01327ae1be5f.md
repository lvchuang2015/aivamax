JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary — Dec. 8th, 2025 | Handling Bulk Account Orders + TikTok Posting Workflows

2025-12-08

Introduction

Today’s Q&A comes from a real conversation with a client requesting 1000+ fanpage-style accounts for automated posting. We break down the core questions: account creation, proxy usage, daily posting tasks, cost structure, and what’s realistically scalable. If you’re managing bulk TikTok or Instagram accounts, this will give you a super clear blueprint.

Q1: “We need 1000 accounts posting content. How should we set this up safely?”

Answer:

You can absolutely scale to 1000+ accounts — but start with a controlled test batch.

Most teams begin with

30 accounts for 30 days

, refine the workflow, then scale.

JarveePro fully supports large batches, but stable structure > fast disaster.

Q2: “Do you provide accounts and proxies?”

No — the user provides both.

JarveePro is the automation engine, not the farm factory.

Q3: “How many posts per day per account is safe?”

For TikTok:

1–3 posts/day

is the sweet spot.

Going higher increases ban risk — TikTok is aggressive.

Q4: “What’s the proxy requirement? How many accounts per proxy?”

Answer:

Best practice:

1 account per proxy

(safest).

Can test 3–5 per proxy, but for TikTok this is riskier.

Q5: “Can JarveePro run all these accounts on our VPS?”

Yes — as long as your VPS has enough CPU + RAM.

JarveePro handles all tasks; you just supply the VPS environment.

Q6: “We already have 30–90 content files. Can you automate posting?”

Yes — JarveePro can auto-post from folders, drip them, and schedule them.

You don’t need manual posting at all.

Q7: “What does the first-month test setup look like?”

Typical starter pack:

30 TikTok accounts

30 proxies

(1 per account)

Content folders connected

Daily posting automation enabled

Monitoring + fine-tuning for 30 days

Once it works smoothly → scale to 100, 300, 1000 accounts.

Q8: “What about costs?”

Answer (client scenario):

$15 for 30 TikTok accounts

$59 for 10 proxies

Additional service fee depending on workload

But TikTok content handling takes time since it’s video-based — automation helps, but manual prep is still needed.

Summary

Today’s Q&A outlined the real-world setup for scaling TikTok or Instagram posting to 1000+ accounts. The key takeaway?

Start with a clean test batch of 30 accounts, use safe proxy ratios, automate posting with JarveePro, validate the workflow — then scale confidently.
