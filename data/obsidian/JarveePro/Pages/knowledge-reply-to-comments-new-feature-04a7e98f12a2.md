Reply to Comments (New Feature)

2021-03-16

How do we reply to comments with JarveePro?

Step 1: Choose

YouTube Tasks

and then click

Add

. Name your task and choose corresponding module. Click

OK

in the end.

Step 2 Select the accounts you wanna use and finish the rest settings.

You can paste video URL here.

There are 5 ways to grab video URL. If you are not familiar with these features, you can try to use Search Users first.

Step 3 : Click

Ok

on the bottom right corner and run the task.
