JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary – Oct. 22nd, 2025(Photo Selection,Follow/Unfollow and Default vs. AI Monitor Campaigns)

2025-10-22

Today was another productive day! We received several valuable questions and suggestions from users that helped us refine JarveePro’s usability and clarify certain campaign functions. One user provided a great idea for enhancing the

Photo Selection

logic, while another asked about

Follow/Unfollow

settings — and later recorded a short video after figuring it out. I truly appreciate when users test, give feedback, and help us make JarveePro more intuitive.

Feature Focus: JarveePro Photo Selection

While reviewing campaigns today, a user shared detailed feedback about our

feature inside the

Campaign Manager

When users go to the

“Select Photo”

tab in a campaign, they can:

Browse

to upload multiple photos,

Clear

to remove previous selections, and

AI-Generated

to create smart visuals automatically.

They can also set:

Random or Sequential

posting order,

Times Allowed per Photo

and

Times Allowed per Photo by Account

, and

Photos per Post

count.

One advanced user,

Jacob

, suggested we add a

“Chance of Using Photo (%)”

control so AI replies/comments randomly decide whether to include an image. This would let AI engagement sometimes be text-only and sometimes include media, which makes interactions feel more human. I agree — this would be a useful addition for AI Monitor campaigns and have logged it for discussion with the dev team.

Follow/Unfollow – Common User Confusion and Resolution

Another user reported JarveePro wasn’t unfollowing some targets. After checking, we discovered the issue was the

input format and historical follows

The correct CSV/list format to use is:

username, link

— where the

username

is the account JarveePro should

use

to perform actions (not the target format confusion some users have).

Once the user performed a couple of follows inside JarveePro to seed the system, unfollowing worked as expected. He then recorded a short tutorial video showing the correct process — I’ll share that with the community so others don’t run into the same confusion.

Additional Q&A Highlights

Default vs. AI Monitor Campaigns

Default:

Standard posting, liking, and following automations.

AI Monitor:

Advanced auto-commenting and replying on

new

user posts (requires $2000 add-on).

Instagram Auto Engagement

Auto-follow/unfollow, auto-like posts, and auto-view stories of followers are available in the

standard version

no AI Monitor required

Reddit Access

Reddit integration is available only on

Full Version

installations.

Summary

Today’s exchanges reinforced how important hands-on user feedback is to JarveePro’s evolution — from Jacob’s suggestion for photo-inclusion probability to a user clarifying Follow/Unfollow input formats and recording a tutorial. I’ve logged the

Photo Inclusion Probability

idea for our roadmap and will circulate the user’s tutorial video internally and to the community.
