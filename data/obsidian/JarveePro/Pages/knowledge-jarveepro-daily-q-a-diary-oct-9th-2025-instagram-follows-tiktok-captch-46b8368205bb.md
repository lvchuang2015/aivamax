Q1: I want to perform follow operations on over 100 Instagram accounts. Which JarveePro plan is best for this?

Answer:

For managing and performing follow operations on more than 100 Instagram accounts, the

Unlimited or Full Version

is recommended.

The

Business Plan

supports up to around 30 accounts, while

Unlimited and Full Versions

allow you to scale without limits.

Tip:

Always verify your exact plan capacity from your JarveePro dashboard or reseller to ensure you can run multiple campaigns simultaneously.

Q2: Is the only difference between Unlimited and Full plans the supported platforms like TikTok?

Yes — the

Full Version

includes additional supported platforms such as TikTok, while the

Unlimited Version

focuses on supporting unlimited accounts.

If you need cross-platform automation (Instagram + TikTok + Facebook + LinkedIn, etc.), the

is ideal.

Q3: I have a list of Instagram users I want to follow. Can JarveePro do this automatically?

Answer:

Yes — you can upload your target user list directly to JarveePro.

Go to

Campaign Manager → Create Follow Campaign

Import your profile URLs

Set follow limits and time intervals

Start campaign

Pro Tip:

Use realistic follow delays to keep automation natural and safe.

Q4: Does JarveePro automatically handle TikTok “I’m not a robot” puzzles?

Yes — JarveePro can automatically bypass TikTok verification challenges when connected with a captcha-solving key.

If you encounter login puzzles, go to:

Settings → Captcha Key → Add SadCaptcha API Key.

This enables JarveePro to solve most login challenges automatically.

Q5: Some of my purchased TikTok accounts get suspended. How can I prevent this?

Answer:

Account suspension usually occurs when proxies or fingerprints are not properly bound.

Follow these steps to protect your TikTok accounts:

Bind

unique proxies

for each account

Set

browser fingerprints

Avoid over-automation during the first 48 hours

Maintain steady activity patterns

Policy Reminder:

Accounts can usually be replaced if suspended within the first login window (24 hours).

Q6: How often should I bind fingerprints or proxies to my accounts?

Answer:

Bind each account

only once

— unless you change the proxy or reset the device profile.

Re-binding isn’t necessary weekly. Stable bindings help ensure account consistency and prevent login flags.
