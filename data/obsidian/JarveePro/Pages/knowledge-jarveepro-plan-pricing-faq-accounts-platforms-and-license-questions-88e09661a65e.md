Q1: What does “1 account” mean in JarveePro?

In JarveePro,

1 account refers to one social media profile

For example:

1 Instagram profile = 1 account

1 Facebook profile = 1 account

1 Twitter/X profile = 1 account

1 YouTube profile = 1 account

Each individual social media login counts as one account inside the software.
