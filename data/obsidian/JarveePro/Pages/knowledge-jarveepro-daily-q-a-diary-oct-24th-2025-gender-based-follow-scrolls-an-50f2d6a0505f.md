2025-10-24

Introduction

Automation never stops evolving, and today’s JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary proves just that. From Instagram gender-targeted following requests to multi-browser synchronization discussions, today’s user feedback offered valuable insights into how marketers, agencies, and automation experts are scaling operations safely and efficiently. Whether it’s comparing survival rates against other bots or exploring premium licensing options, these real-world exchanges highlight JarveePro’s superior performance, customer-centric development, and reliability across hundreds of daily active accounts.

Q&A Conversation Highlights

1. Instagram Gender-Based Follow Requests

A user asked whether JarveePro could follow accounts based on gender filters — specifically, male users only.

Answer:

Currently, JarveePro doesn’t include gender-based following filters. However, this can be developed as a

custom feature

upon request.

Follow-up clarification confirmed that the user needed it

for Instagram only

, showing demand for niche targeting capabilities within automation workflows.

2. Manual Feed Scrolling & Browser Control

Another user asked whether it’s better to

scroll feeds manually

during early account warm-up stages and if there’s a way to scroll multiple browsers at once.

Manual scrolling during initial setup is recommended. JarveePro doesn’t yet allow synchronized scrolling across multiple browsers — but users needing this level of control can use

MarketerBrowser

, which syncs mouse and scroll actions for coordinated multi-browser activity.

3. Comparative Performance Feedback

One user shared impressive results from a 5-day trial:

“Better than Fanzella — on 100 accounts, 50 died. With JarveePro, only 1 died.”

They praised both software stability and the

quality of JarveePro’s support

, noting that human assistance plays a major role in their success with automation.

4. Corporate Licensing Inquiry

A client asked about the pricing model for the

Online Monitoring Feature

, wondering whether the $1,000 fee applies per license or covers multiple corporate accounts.

Answer:

The cost is

$2,000 per license

, currently priced individually rather than in bulk packages. The client expressed interest in purchasing once the upcoming JarveePro updates are released, showing confidence in future platform improvements.

Summary

JarveePro’s flexibility and support continue to attract advanced users and agencies.

Custom development requests (e.g., gender-specific following) highlight evolving automation needs.

Real-world feedback confirms better account survival rates compared with competing bots.

The Online Monitoring license model remains single-license based, but enterprise interest is strong.
