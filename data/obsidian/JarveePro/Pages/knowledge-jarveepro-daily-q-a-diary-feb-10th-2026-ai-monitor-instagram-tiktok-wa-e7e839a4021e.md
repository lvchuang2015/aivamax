Q3: Can I change my private key when switching devices?

Yes. If you change your device, your private key can be updated securely. Once payment and ownership are confirmed, the JarveePro license is refreshed and re-issued to ensure safe access on the new device.

Q4: What payment method is supported for license activation?

Q5: Why is Instagram warm-up necessary before automation?

Instagram warm-up is critical to prevent suspensions and restrictions. New or inactive accounts must gradually build trust through human-like activity before automation begins. JarveePro’s warm-up system simulates natural behavior patterns, helping accounts age safely before scaling.

Q6: Does JarveePro support TikTok warm-up?

Yes. JarveePro includes TikTok warm-up strategies designed to reduce bans by avoiding sudden activity spikes. Proper warm-up ensures accounts behave like real users rather than automated bots.

Q7: Is it possible to scale to 100+ Instagram and TikTok accounts?

Yes, but scaling requires proper preparation. Each account must be warmed up correctly, infrastructure must be isolated, and automation should only begin after accounts show stable behavior. Skipping these steps almost always results in bans.

Q8: Does JarveePro offer custom development solutions?

Yes. JarveePro provides custom development for advanced needs, including GSM/SIM gateway integration, private device usage, IPfarming, and one-device-per-agent configurations. These solutions are designed for enterprise-level automation and high-trust environments.

Summary

This Q&A session highlights the importance of proper monitoring, secure licensing, and structured warm-up strategies when using JarveePro.
