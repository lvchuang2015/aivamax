Q1: How can I safely update website links on multiple Twitter profiles at once?

Answer:

Within JarveePro’s Twitter Campaign--Add Website, you can bulk-edit website links across unlimited Twitter profiles without switching tabs or logging in manually.

Just import your accounts, open the

Add Website

campaign, and input your desired website link. JarveePro updates all accounts automatically, maintaining uniformity and saving hours of tedious work.

Pro Tip:

Schedule link updates during non-peak hours for smoother synchronization and lower rate-limit risks.

Q2: How can I change my Twitter profile picture, cover photo, location, and bio using JarveePro?

JarveePro gives you full creative control over your Twitter identity — at scale. Inside

Twitter Campaign

, you can upload new profile and cover photos, update your bio, and adjust location data in one action for multiple accounts. Each update is processed through secure automation parameters, ensuring your profiles remain authentic and compliant.

Keep image sizes optimized (400x400 for profile photos, 1500x500 for covers) to prevent cropping or upload errors.
