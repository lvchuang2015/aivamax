Introduction

In today’s JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary, we’re breaking down a real user conversation that hits some of the most common (and important) questions about

PVACreator Deluxe

—pricing, features, limitations, and what’s actually included vs. what’s extra.

If you’re planning to scale account creation and integrate with JarveePro, this kind of clarity can save you both time and money. Let’s get straight into it.

Q 1: Does the $551 Deluxe version include Speeder and Browser?

Answer:

Yes. The

Deluxe version ($551)

includes both the

Speeder

and the

Browser

, so you’re getting the full stack needed for efficient account creation and handling.

Q 2: Does the Deluxe version include lifetime updates?

Yes. The Deluxe version comes with

lifetime updates

, meaning you won’t need to pay again for future improvements or supported platform updates.

Q 3: Can I create unlimited accounts for the 17 supported sites?

Answer:

Yes. The “17 sites” refers to supported platforms—not a limit on account quantity.

You can create

unlimited accounts

across those 17 supported services, depending on your resources (proxies, captcha balance, etc.).

Q 4: Is captcha completion included or does it cost extra?

Captcha solving is

supported but not included for free

You’ll need to connect your own

captcha-solving service API key

(such as 2Captcha, Anti-Captcha, etc.), which will incur additional cost based on usage.

Q 5: Are proxies included?

No, proxies are

not included

You’ll need to purchase proxies separately. A recommended solution for account farming and management is IP-based proxy services like Ipfarming.

Q 6: Can I use a VPN instead of proxies?

Answer:

Yes, you can use a VPN—but it’s

not recommended for scaling

Proxies offer better control, rotation, and lower detection risk compared to VPNs, especially when creating multiple accounts.

Q 7: Can I export accounts to use in JarveePro free version?

Yes. You can

export created accounts

and use them inside the

JarveePro free version

, making PVACreator a strong companion tool for automation workflows.

Q 8: The homepage shows a 40% discount, but clicking it freezes. Why?

This could be related to the

payment method or page behavior

, especially when using certain browsers or payment types.

A follow-up question suggests checking:

Are you trying to

pay via crypto

Try a different browser or connection if the page freezes

Summary

PVACreator Deluxe

is essentially a

complete account creation system

bundled with browser and speed optimization tools, plus lifetime updates. However, like most serious automation setups, the real performance depends on

external components

—proxies, captcha services, and your setup quality.

The key takeaway?

The core software is powerful and “one-time purchase”

But scaling requires

ongoing investment in infrastructure

If you’re planning to integrate with JarveePro, this combo can be extremely effective—just don’t expect a plug-and-play miracle without proper setup.
