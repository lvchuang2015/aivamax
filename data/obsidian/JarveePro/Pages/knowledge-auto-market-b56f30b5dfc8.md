## Content

Auto Market

2025-07-19

Step 1: Open JarveePro→Click Reddit→ Choose"Open Reddit"→Click "OK" to start.

Step 2: Choose task and appear a new debug. Then choose first setting and then select account. Also, select monitoring account and set interval time. Next, import keyword file.

Step 3: Slide the page and select one of task. Then import its file. Last, click "Finish" to start.

Step 4: If we choose second one, we have to select account and import post with file. You can click"Create Model" to create model and fill in it if you don't have file.

Then repeat 3rd step.
