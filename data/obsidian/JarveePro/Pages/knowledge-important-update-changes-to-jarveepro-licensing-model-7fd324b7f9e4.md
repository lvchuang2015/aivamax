Important Update: Changes to JarveePro Licensing Model

2026-05-22

JarveePro is updating its licensing structure to better support long-term development, stability, and continuous software innovation.

At JarveePro, we’ve always prioritized delivering frequent updates, improved automation systems, and platform adaptability. After reviewing customer feedback and evaluating the sustainability of maintaining daily updates for legacy pricing models, we’ve made an important decision regarding our licensing options.

What Is Changing?

We will be

discontinuing the following license types:

Lifetime Licenses

Yearly Licenses

These options will no longer be available for new purchase moving forward.

Why this change?

This adjustment allows us to:

Maintain consistent

daily updates and platform compatibility

Support faster development of new AI and automation features

Ensure long-term sustainability of the product infrastructure

Improve service stability for all active users

Our Commitment

This decision was not made lightly.

We deeply value every user who has supported JarveePro throughout its journey. Your feedback has been instrumental in shaping this evolution, and we remain fully committed to delivering:

Continuous software improvements

Faster automation performance

Stronger anti-detection systems

Expanded AI-driven features

JarveePro is moving into a more advanced, future-ready development phase—and this change ensures we can continue building without compromise.

Limited-Time Thank You Offer

As a gesture of appreciation to our community, we are introducing a

special, limited-time discount

30% OFF – All Existing Yearly & Lifetime Licenses

This is our

largest discount ever offered

It surpasses all previous promotions, including Black Friday deals

Available for a

limited time only

Why this matters:

This offer represents a final opportunity to secure JarveePro under legacy pricing conditions before the transition is complete.

Important Note

Once this promotional period ends:

Lifetime and Yearly licenses will no longer be available

Future pricing will reflect the new subscription-based model

What’s Next?

We are entering a new chapter focused on:

Smarter AI automation systems

Faster platform adaptation

Improved scalability for agencies and power users

More frequent feature releases

JarveePro is evolving—and this is just the beginning.

Thank You

We sincerely appreciate your trust, patience, and continued support. This transition helps ensure that JarveePro remains one of the most advanced automation platforms in the industry for years to come.

— The JarveePro Team
