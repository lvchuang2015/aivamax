Instagram Campaign Guide: Follow His Followings / Follow His Followers

2026-02-23

Introduction

JarveePro 4.0 provides two powerful methods to follow users from a target Instagram account:

Direct Integrated Follow Method (Built-in Dashboard Method)

– simplest and fastest

Advanced Content Explorer Method (AI Monitor + Export Capability)

– more powerful and flexible

Both methods allow you to safely grow your Instagram account by targeting users already connected to your niche.

This guide explains both approaches and when to use each.

Method 1: Direct Integrated Follow Method (Recommended for Most Users)

This method is fully integrated into JarveePro and does not require scraping or exporting lists manually.

JarveePro automatically visits the target profile and follows users from their followings or followers.

Step 1: Create Campaign

Navigate to:

Campaigns → Instagram → Create Campaign

Select:

Follow His Followings

or

Follow His Followers

These options are built directly into JarveePro.

Step 2:Select Accounts

Go to:

Select Accounts tab

Choose the Instagram accounts that will run this campaign.

Step 3: Add Target Profile URL

Open the

Profile URLs tab

Click:

Add

Enter target Instagram profile URL:

Example:

https://www.instagram.com/jarveeproadmin/

JarveePro will automatically:

Visit the profile

Open followers or followings

Follow users based on your settings

No scraping required.

Step 4: Configure Campaign Settings

Configure important parameters:

Sequential or Random

Sequential → follows users in order

Random → follows users randomly

Recommended: Sequential

Wait Time (seconds)

Example safe settings:

10 to 30 seconds

This simulates human behavior.

Follows Allowed per Profile

Example:

100 to 100 per profile

This determines how many users JarveePro follows from each target account.

Times Allowed per Account(How many time the same profile each account will follow)

1 to 1

This controls how many times JarveePro revisits the same profile.

Step 5: Start Campaign or Use Scheduler

You can:

Start manually

or

Use Scheduler (recommended)

Scheduler ensures safe automation.

How This Built-In Method Works

JarveePro automatically:

Opens target profile

Opens followers or followings list

Selects users based on settings

Follows users safely

No manual scraping required.

This is the simplest and most efficient method.

Method 2: Using JarveePro Content Explorer (Advanced Method)

Content Explorer provides additional advanced capabilities including:

Real-time monitoring

AI Monitor integration

Exporting follower lists

Building reusable target databases

Step 1: Open Content Explorer

Navigate to:

Content Explorer → Search Tools

Select:

Followers

or

Followings

Enter target username.

Check "Save All Results"

Check Enable Real-time monitoring

Click SAVE

Step 2: Export or Send to Campaign

You can now:

Option A: Send users directly to Follow Campaign

Option B: Export users to file

This allows:

Future reuse

Backup storage

External processing

Step 3: Use with AI Monitor Campaigns

JarveePro Content Explorer supports:

AI Monitor Campaigns

This allows real-time monitoring of:

New followers

New followings

Active users

JarveePro can automatically follow new users as they appear.

This enables continuous automated growth.

When to Use Each Method

Use Built-in Method if:

You want fastest setup

You want simple automation

You do not need exported lists

Use Content Explorer if:

You want exportable user lists

You want AI Monitor automation

You want real-time follower targeting

You want advanced campaign control

Recommended Safe Follow Limits (2026 Guidelines)

New account:

10-20 follows per day

Aged account:

30-80 follows per day

Trusted aged account:

80-150 follows per day

Always combine with Warm-Up Campaign.

Best Practice Strategy (Recommended)

Phase 1: Warm Up account

Phase 2: Use Follow His Followers campaign

Phase 3: Add AI Monitor Campaign

Phase 4: Expand with Content Explorer targeting

This produces safest and most effective growth.

Summary

JarveePro 4.0 provides two powerful methods for following target users:

The built-in Follow His Followings / Followers feature is the fastest and easiest method and requires only a profile URL.

The Content Explorer method provides advanced capabilities including real-time monitoring, export functionality, and AI-powered targeting.

Both methods are fully supported and recommended depending on your automation strategy.
