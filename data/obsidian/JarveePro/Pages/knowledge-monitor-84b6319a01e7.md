## Content

Monitor

2025-07-20

Step 1: Open JarveePro→Click Limkedin→ Choose"Open Linkedin"→Click "OK" to start.

Step 2: Select account→Set interval time→Click "OK" to start.

Interval(H) 24:

Run monitoring tasks every 24 hours.

Account Intervals(S) 20:

The interval between switching between each account is 20 seconds.

Refresh Interval(H) 12:

Refresh monitored post every 12 hours.
