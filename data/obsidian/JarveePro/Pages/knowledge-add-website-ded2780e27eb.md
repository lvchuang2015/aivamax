## Content

Add website

2025-06-01

Step 1: Choose "Campaigns" and Twitter, and select task.

Step 2: Select account(s) and add website info. You can import multiple website info you want to add in your home page. Or add one by one.

Step 3: "Random use of data" and "Data is not reused" are optional tasks. But you must click "OK" to start.
