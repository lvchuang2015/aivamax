Like Comments

2025-05-15

How do we like comments with JarveePro?

Step 1: Click

"Campaigns"

, then choose

Facebook

. Select the

"Like Comment"

task, then complete setup.

Step 2: Select your account(s)

Step 3: There are two ways to add links:

adding in bulk (CVS File)

or adding

one by one.

Step 3: Complete the

task setting

and choose

like type

When all settings are completed, click Start and the task will run automatically.
