What is the loss rate for new accounts?

On average, the loss rate for new accounts is estimated at

5%–10%

under normal usage. This is considered a healthy benchmark in the industry.

What is the success rate of registering new Instagram accounts?

The success rate for registering Instagram accounts through automation tools typically falls between

40%–80%

, depending on factors like proxies, SMS verification, and account quality.

How much does it cost to register one Instagram account with PVACreator?

The cost varies based on the resources you use (proxies, SMS services, and account verification methods). JarveePro itself does not charge per account, but external services may affect your cost per registration.

If my PC breaks and I switch to a new one, do I need to buy a new license?

No, you do not need to purchase a new license. You can simply

transfer your existing license

to your new PC. Contact support if you need help deactivating it on your old device.

How many people can I @mention on Facebook?

When posting manually on Facebook, you are limited to

@mentioning up to 19 people

per post. However, with

JarveePro’s automation

, you can

go far beyond this limit

, making it possible to tag a much larger audience and boost your content’s reach significantly.
