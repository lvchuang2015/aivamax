How to Get More Instagram Followers With JarveePro

There are a number of social media automation tools designed to save you time and effort with your social media marketing. A number of those tools are available for Instagram specifically. Here is how you can grow your Instagram followers by using our JarveePro.

Auto - Find& Follow New Accounts

Though JarveePro varies in terms of the services it offers to different social media marketing, all of them are offered the ability to automate finding and following new accounts. Generally speaking, all you have to do is to set up hashtags and accounts you want to follow in JarveePro.

Then, using this information, it will start following these accounts and select random accounts from the hashtags to start following automatically. It will follow a certain number of new accounts gradually every day to make your account activity looks as natural as possible.

Auto - Unfollow

With this strategy and JarveePro, first, you will follow users who may be interested in your account. Then, you wait a few days to see if they follow you back, and if they do not, use JarveePro to unfollow them automatically. Most of the time you’ll find that 20 to 35% of the people will follow you back.

The actual percentage will vary significantly depending on how effective your strategy is for the people you start to follow. The idea is that you have to use filters to make sure you were engaging with users that are most likely to engage back with you.

Using the follow/unfollow method without JarveePro is unlikely to help you achieve those results. Manually following 1,000 users in the span of a week in an active niche compared to JarveePro you may find the manual method only achieves a 10% follow back rate which is comparatively lower. When you consider the increased time you spend with the manual method and ROI rate of both, you will find no reason not to use JarveePro.

Taking the time to unfollow users who don’t follow you back keeps you with a good follower ratio and ensures you’re not wasting time and effort on people who are not going to engage with you. High engagement rates make your account look more appealing to the Instagram algorithm for better chances of viral posts and follower growth through viral activity.

Auto-Schedule

Post

JarveePro allows users to schedule posts to social networks automatically. This makes it possible for users to prepare different content for multiple posts and share it all at once. And this also allows you to keep a consistent posting calendar, that is to say, posting at specific hours whenever your target audience is most likely to be activ

e.

manage posts manually everytime so that you can focus on other tasks that can not be automated to win online.

Auto - Comment

There are some people out there who would advise against automating comments because it could come across as spammy. However, when it is done correctly using neutral yet positive comments

you can still leave relevant comments on photos without being marked as spam.

To make sure you’re not completely wasting time, it’s recommended to comment on a few photos every day. Because JarveePro will interact with hundreds of photos every day, it’s impossible to program it in a way that will create context-relevant comments for all of the photos it comes across, So that’s why you should prepare some time to craft genuine comments manually every day

Auto - Like

Automating likes is safe to do with JarveePro. It won’t come across as spam or get flagged by Instagram. Likes are a non-intrusive way to make people happy to increase your odds of engagement. Likes or a subtle nudge that will only show up on a person’s activity feed. Many people don’t have issues with getting likes from people we don’t know and everyone benefits when their content gets a like because Instagram’s algorithm picks up on it as a signal of being popular.

Unless you are the one who has thousands of followers, chances are you’re checking your activity feed to see who liked your post and discover new Instagram users this way. Automating liking banks on the fact that you’re liking posts from people who will discover that you liked their post and hopefully will turn around and follow you.

Automating likes through JarveePro is similar to paying Instagram to have your account shown on people’s home feeds through ads. The activity creates weren’t even aware of the fact that you existed. And, because it’s not a formal ad that you are running through the Instagram platform, it’s free.

Auto - Send Direct Messages

Just like other automation, you can also automate direct messaging to Instagram users. However, because it is difficult to make contextually relevant to every single follower JarveePro encounters, you will have to control the hashtags to find the posts and keep it limited to a small number of accounts every day.

You will, of course, have to use generic phrasing, so keep a variety of positive, neutral statements such as, “Thanks for the follow!” in the repository for the bot to work with.

Focus Your Efforts on Quality Content Creation

Use the time you save with JarveePro to make sure the content you’re creating to post on Instagram is of the highest quality and is targeted to your audience.

Check on JarveePro Efforts Regularly

From time to time, you want to check and see how our JarveePro efforts are going. If you find that you are not getting a lot of engagement, you may want to adjust JarveePro to target different hashtags.

Take Time to Answer All Comments

To boost your engagement and to show your audience you care, take time to reply to any and all comments on your posts. Even if you notice generic ones that you believe were automated, taking the time to respond to them shows the rest of your audience that you were paying attention and you appreciate the time they took to comment.

JarveePro to Save Time – Not to Completely Avoid Work.

If you set up JarveePro one time, walk away, and never look at JarveePro or your Instagram account again, you’re doing it wrong. Not only are you doing it wrong, but you’re obviously doing it for the wrong reasons. Chances are, you’re looking for a quick and easy way to monetize your Instagram following and while this is a respectable desire, it won’t get you very far.

You will still need to spend a certain amount of time engaging on Instagram and creating content for your followers manually. Nothing on the internet that is of the good quality you need to develop stable customer relationships is done with 100% automation.

Yes, there’s plenty of stuff out there that is completely automated, but it’s not the kind of stuff your audience will enjoy or respond to the way you hope.

Don’t Push JarveePro to The Max

Unless you use JarveePro from the very beginning of your Instagram account, chances are you were only able to like and follow a certain number of users every day.

You want to avoid maxing JarveePro out because Instagram will notice if you go from following 50 users a day to 1,000 users a day. The key is to make the activity look as natural as possible which often means using JarveePro much less than what it is capable of actually doing.

Do Not Use More Than One Instagram Marketing Tool

It may be tempting to double down on your efforts by using other Instagram marketing tools while you are using JarveePro. However, is best if you only use one at a time. You can use one for a certain amount of time before testing on another one to see which one you like better, but using more than one at the same time creates unnatural activity and could possibly cross some wires and create potential flags on your Instagram account.

Everything you do on Instagram from content creation to following accounts and engaging with them should be done with your followers in mind. Without a dedicated group of loyal followers, you won’t be able to reap sales benefits from your social media presence.
