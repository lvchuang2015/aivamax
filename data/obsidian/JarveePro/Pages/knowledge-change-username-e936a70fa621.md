## Content

Change Username

2025-06-01

Step 1: Choose "Campaigns" and Twitter, and select task.

Step 2: Select account(s) and add username(s). You can import multiple usernames you want to change in CVS format. Or add one by one.

Step 3: Complete task settings(Optional settings) and click "OK" to start.

Random use of data:

When checked, the system will randomly select data for each operation instead of using it in order, reducing the risk of repetitive actions.
