Introduction

Most users think unfollowing in JarveePro is just about cleaning up accounts—but the

Targeted Unfollow Campaign

takes it to another level.

Instead of blindly unfollowing, this feature lets you

unfollow specific users based on collected data

, giving you full control over who stays and who goes.

If you’re running multiple accounts, scaling campaigns, or working with scraped audiences, this is the tool that keeps your account structure clean, safe, and strategic.

Q1: What is the Instagram Targeted Unfollow Campaign?

Answer:

The

allows you to unfollow users based on a

custom input list

, instead of relying on general filters like “unfollow after X days.”

This means:

You choose exactly who to unfollow

You can import scraped data

You can clean up accounts with surgical precision

Think of it as:

manual-level control, automated at scale.

Q2: What format should I use to import users?

JarveePro requires the following format:

username,profile_link

Example:

john_doe,https://www.instagram.com/john_doe

jane_smith,https://www.instagram.com/jane_smith

Key Notes:

One entry per line

Separator =

No extra spaces

Must include valid Instagram profile links

This is exactly what you saw in your setup screen.

JarveePro Advanced Content Explorer

Q5: How do I run this safely across multiple accounts?

Answer:

This is where strategy matters.

Recommended Limits:

New accounts: 50–100/day

Aged accounts: 150–300/day

Best Practices:

Add delays between actions

Mix with other activities:

Likes

Reposts

Story views

Avoid:

Mass unfollow spikes

Running only unfollow actions

Pure unfollow campaigns = suspicious behavior.

Blend it like a human would.

Q6: When should I use Targeted Unfollow instead of normal Unfollow Tool?

Answer:

Use

Targeted Unfollow

when:

You imported/scraped specific users

You want to remove low-quality or irrelevant audiences

You’re cleaning accounts after aggressive growth campaigns

Regular Unfollow Tool

You just want time-based cleanup

You don’t care who gets removed

Simple rule:

Control needed? → Targeted Unfollow

Automation only? → Regular Unfollow

Q7: Can I combine this with Follow campaigns?

Answer:

Yes—and you

should

Advanced Strategy:

Follow users from a niche

Let them engage / follow back

Extract non-engagers

Feed into Targeted Unfollow

This creates a

self-cleaning growth loop

This is how you scale accounts without turning them into spam machines.
