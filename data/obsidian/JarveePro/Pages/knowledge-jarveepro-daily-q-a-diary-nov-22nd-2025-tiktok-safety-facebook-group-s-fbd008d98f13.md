Q5: If I switch proxies on an existing account, will that trigger verification?

Answer:

It

can

, but it depends on how drastically the fingerprint changes.

Here’s what usually triggers verification:

switching from a US IP to an Asian or EU IP

switching from datacenter → residential or vice versa

switching every few hours

jumping between cities or countries

using a proxy with a known low reputation

Safe switch strategy:

Keep the same country.

Keep the same timezone.

Switch to a similar IP type (residential → residential).

Log in slowly and only perform light actions for 24 hours.

General rule:

Switching proxies is safe if the change looks like a normal ISP change — not like teleportation.
