Q3: I want to grow my groups and use comment strategies. Why isn’t tagging working?

Answer:

This is a common concern. Here’s what’s really happening:

Social platforms (especially Facebook)

limit or block automated tagging

in comments to prevent spam behavior

Tagging in replies may not work due to:

Platform API restrictions

Anti-spam detection systems

Privacy or engagement settings of users

What you can do instead:

Use

smart comment replies without tagging

(more natural, less risky)

Focus on

high-converting call-to-actions (CTA)

Engage users who already commented instead of cold tagging

AI-generated personalized replies

to increase conversion

Strong take:

Tagging is pretty much a dead tactic in 2026—platforms suppress it heavily. Natural engagement wins.
