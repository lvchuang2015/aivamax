Introduction

Welcome back to the

JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary

multi-platform posting

performance tracking

Facebook Leads Extractor

Q1: Why can’t I publish my videos to TikTok and Instagram at the same time?

Answer:

This issue typically occurs when platform tokens expire or accounts need re-verification. JarveePro’s

multi-platform publishing system

relies on secure authorization with TikTok and Instagram, which occasionally update their verification mechanisms.

Go to

Account Manager → Edit Account → Re-login/Verify

Go Back to Campaign Manager

Restart your

Posting Campaign

Q2: Can JarveePro automatically reply to comments or DMs?

Yes — the

Auto Reply & DM Module

can handle both public replies and private messages.

You can:

Use text or media templates

Apply

Spintax

for varied natural responses

Choose response delay intervals to mimic real users

Access: Go to

Campaigns → Select Platform (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)

→ Choose

Reply Comment

or

Send Message

Campaign type.

Q3: Why does my Facebook bulk posting sometimes stop unexpectedly?

Answer:

This can be caused by

temporary rate limits

unstable proxies

To fix:

Check proxy health in

Proxy Manager

Add posting intervals (30–90 seconds recommended)

Enable

“Auto Retry Failed Tasks”

Q4: What is JarveePro’s Facebook Leads Extractor and how does it work?

The

Facebook Leads Extractor

is one of JarveePro’s most requested tools. It automatically collects leads from

Facebook groups, pages, and post comments

— including names, contact info, and profile URLs — without needing manual copy-paste.

Here’s how it helps marketers and agencies:

Extracts lead data from

targeted posts or pages

in seconds

Supports

niche-based filtering

(location, keyword, interest)

Exports data to CSV for CRM integration

Works with

JarveePro’s automation campaigns

, so you can auto-message leads afterward

Use Case:

A digital agency extracted over

2,000 active business leads

from niche Facebook groups in one week using the Leads Extractor, then launched automated follow-ups through the

Messenger Outreach Campaign

— achieving a

23% reply rate

Tip:

Always apply realistic time intervals and active proxies to maintain Facebook compliance and ensure smooth extraction.
