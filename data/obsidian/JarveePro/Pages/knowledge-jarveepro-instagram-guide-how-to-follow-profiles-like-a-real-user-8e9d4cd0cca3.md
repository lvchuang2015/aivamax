JarveePro Instagram Guide: How to Follow Profiles Like a Real User

2025-08-16

This guide will show you how to:

Set up an Instagram Follow Campaign with multiple accounts.

Add profile URLs to follow.

Avoid duplicate follows across accounts.

Control wait times and follow limits to stay safe and realistic.

1. Create a New Follow Campaign

Go to the

Campaigns

tab on the left menu.

Click

Create Campaign

→ Select

Follow

A new campaign window will open where you can configure your settings.

2. Add Instagram Profile URLs

Switch to the

Profile URLs

tab.

Paste the Instagram profile links you want to follow.

Example:

https://www.instagram.com/jarveeproadmin/

https://www.instagram.com/ipfaming/

Click

Add

to confirm.

👉 You can import a list via TXT, CSV, or Excel if you have many profiles.

👉 JarveePro automatically manages duplicate profiles so the same user isn’t followed twice by different accounts.

3. Select Accounts for the Campaign

Go to the

Select Accounts

tab.

Choose which Instagram accounts should participate.

In the example,

49 accounts

are selected.

You can assign actions

sequentially

or

randomly

across accounts.

Tip: Sequential makes accounts follow one by one, while Random makes actions look more human.

4. Set Safe Follow Limits

JarveePro lets you control how many times each account follows a target profile.

This prevents spam and ensures every account follows naturally.

5. Control Wait Times (Look Real)

To avoid looking like automation, you must randomize wait times.

In the

Settings tab

Wait Time (sec):

60

180

seconds (1–3 minutes).

JarveePro will wait a random time before following the next profile.

This simulates real human behavior instead of instant actions.

6. Schedule and Run the Campaign

Once settings are ready, click

Schedule

Review the campaign under the

Campaign Manager

tab.

Hit

Start

to begin.

JarveePro will now:

Use all 49

accounts

Follow the

2 selected profiles

Randomize timing between follows.

Avoid duplicates across accounts.

Best Practices

Start slow (few follows per day) and gradually increase.

Always use realistic wait times to avoid Instagram detection.

Keep your account warm-up period in mind before running massive campaigns.

Monitor

Success / Failed logs

to fine-tune performance.

With these steps, your clients can safely run

Instagram Follow Campaigns

in JarveePro — looking just like a real user while scaling across dozens of accounts.
