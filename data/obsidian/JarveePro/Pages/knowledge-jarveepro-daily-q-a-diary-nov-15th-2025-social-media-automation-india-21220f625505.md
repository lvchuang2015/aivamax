JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary — Nov. 15th, 2025 (social media automation India, white-label,hosting setups)

2025-11-15

Today’s Q&A session came straight from a brand-new Enterprise client gearing up to launch in the Indian market — and wow, they came prepared with questions. From white-label possibilities to hosting setups, to what exactly is included in the Enterprise full version, everything landed on the table. So in today’s Daily Notes, we’re breaking down each point clearly, concisely, and without any of the usual confusion around licensing or server installations. If you’re deploying JarveePro for serious, large-scale operations, today’s answers will feel like a cheat sheet you’ll want to save.

Q1: “We bought JarveePro Full Version with unlimited accounts and users. What exactly do we get with this version?”

Answer:

With the

Enterprise Version

, users receive:

Unlimited accounts support

One License is only for one user

Full automation suite

across all supported platforms

Advanced tools

such as Impression Monitor, Audience Sync, Filters, Tasks Automation, and more

Priority updates + priority support

Q2: “Do we need to purchase add-ons? What add-ins are required?”

For most Enterprise users, everything is already included.

The only add-ons users may consider:

AI Monitor

(if your account structure requires extra modules)

Dedicated Proxy Packages

(optional, only if needed)

If your team needs clarification, support can check your deployment needs and recommend only what’s necessary — no upselling.

Q3: “Can we host it on any server?”

Answer:

Yes — JarveePro allows flexible hosting.

You can install it on:

✔ Local PC

✔ Company workstation

✔ VPS

✔ Dedicated server

As long as the device meets basic system requirements and is

exclusive for your license

, you’re good to go.

Q4: “Do you provide hosting service too?”

Yes please check our

JarveePro services page

Q5: “Our target is the Indian market. We need white-labeling. Is this possible?”

Answer:

YesWhite-labeling can be supported depending on customization needs.

If your goal is to distribute JarveePro-based services under your own brand, our team can discuss what level of white-label is available and what integrations are required.

Q6: “Should we host on cloud? Do you offer cloud hosting service?”

Cloud hosting works perfectly fine with JarveePro, and in many cases it's recommended for 24/7 operations.

However:

Consider

JarveePro VPS Version

✔ You can install it on

any

cloud server you choose

✔ Performance is typically better on stable VPS/dedicated servers

Q7: “We bought Full. Is this lifetime? And how many devices?”

Answer:

Yes

, your full version is

for lifetime

One license = one device

You can install it on

one PC OR one server

You may migrate later if needed (contact support)

This ensures security, stability, and proper usage tracking.
