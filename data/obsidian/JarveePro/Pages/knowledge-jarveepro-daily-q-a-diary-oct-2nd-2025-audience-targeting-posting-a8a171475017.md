Growing your audience requires more than just posting—it requires

precision targeting

and

smart automation

. JarveePro makes this possible by letting you choose exactly who sees your content, while handling the heavy lifting of posting across multiple platforms. If you’ve been asking how JarveePro identifies the right crowd or why the free version is important, here are the answers you need.

How does JarveePro know which type of crowd I’m targeting?

JarveePro doesn’t “guess” your audience—it gives you

control

. You can search and filter by:

Keywords

User Profile Links

Hashtags

This allows you to precisely target the audience that matches your campaign goals.

Can I create and post videos by just prompting JarveePro?

Currently, JarveePro focuses on

automation and publishing

. You can upload your videos and schedule them across multiple platforms.

JarveePro is designed for you to manage

multiple accounts

easily inside the software. If you want managed setup or workers to run campaigns, you can check our service page:

https://www.jarveepro.com/service.html

Why do I need to download the free version first?

JarveePro’s

free version

lets you test the core automation features. It helps you understand how campaigns, targeting, and posting work before upgrading to paid plans.

Why is the free version not working on my Samsung Galaxy S23?

JarveePro is a

Windows-based software

. It does not run natively on Android or iOS devices. To use it, you need a Windows PC, VPS, or server.

How do I target my audience?

You can:

Search posts by

keyword, user profile, or hashtag

Like, comment, follow/unfollow, or interact with the results

Facebook
