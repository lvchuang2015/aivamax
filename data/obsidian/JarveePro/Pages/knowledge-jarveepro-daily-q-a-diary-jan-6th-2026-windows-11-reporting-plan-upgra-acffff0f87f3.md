JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary — Jan. 6th, 2026 | Windows 11, Reporting & Plan Upgrades

2026-01-06

Introduction

Every day, our support team answers practical questions from users running JarveePro in real production environments.

This diary captures real conversations from January 6th, 2026, covering system compatibility, enterprise reporting, reposting logic, custom development, and upgrade options. The goal is clarity—so users can make informed decisions without assumptions.

Q1: Does JarveePro work on Windows 11?

Answer:

Yes. JarveePro works on

Windows 11

Although Windows 10 is listed in the system requirements, Windows 11 users can run the software without issues.

Q2: Does the Enterprise plan include reporting for Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube?

The Enterprise plan includes reporting for:

Facebook:

Yes

Twitter (X):

YouTube:

Instagram:

Not by default

Instagram reporting is not included in the standard Enterprise plan.

Q3: Can Instagram reporting be added?

Answer:

Yes. Instagram reporting can be added through

custom development

The cost for this custom development is

$600

Q4: Will repost tasks continue running until posts run out?

No.

Reposting tasks will not continue running if the post list is done.

For account safety and long-term stability, it is recommended to keep reposting

under 5 posts per day

Q5: Why is repost interval measured in seconds instead of minutes?

Repost intervals are currently measured in

seconds

Feedback has been noted that minute-based input would be clearer for longer schedules (e.g., 45 minutes instead of 2,700 seconds). This suggestion is under internal review.

Q6: If I upgrade my Individual Lifetime plan with AI, will AI transfer when upgrading to FULL later?

Answer:

Yes.

If you purchase the

AI upgrade

on the Individual plan, it will

transfer automatically

when upgrading to the FULL plan in the future.

Q7: Does the New Year sale apply to upgrades?

15% discount

is available and can be applied to eligible upgrades, including FULL plan and AI upgrades, as confirmed on January 6th, 2026.

Summary

On January 6th, 2026, common questions focused on platform compatibility, reporting capabilities, repost scheduling clarity, and upgrade flexibility. JarveePro continues to support Windows 11, offers enterprise-level reporting for most major platforms, and provides customization options where native reporting is not available. Upgrade paths remain flexible, with AI features transferable and New Year discounts applicable.
