Overview

JarveePro Advanced Content Explorer is a cross-platform search tool that allows users to extract posts, users, comments, followers, and community data from major social media platforms.

Unlike native platform search functions, Advanced Content Explorer provides structured results that can be used for:

Audience research

Competitor analysis

Lead generation

Engagement campaigns

Content inspiration

Automation targeting

This guide explains what can be searched on each supported platform.

Supported Platforms & Search Types

Facebook Search Functions

You can extract and analyze the following:

Search Posts by Keywords

Find public posts related to specific topics or phrases.

Search Posts by Users

Extract posts from a specific profile or page.

Search Group Posts

Pull posts from selected Facebook groups.

Search Comments by Post Link

Retrieve comments from a specific public post.

Search Users by Keywords

Discover users connected to specific keywords or interests.

Search Followings

View accounts followed by a specific user.

Search Followers

Retrieve followers of a specific profile.

Search Group Members

Extract members from selected Facebook groups.

Instagram Search Functions

Available search options include:

Search Posts by Keywords

Search Posts by Hashtags

Search Posts by Users

Search Comments by Post Link

Search Stories by Users

Search Reels by Users

Search Users by Keywords

Search Followings

Search Followers

These searches help identify content trends, influencers, and active audiences.

Twitter Search Functions

The following search types are supported:

Search Posts by Community

These functions are commonly used for trend tracking and targeted outreach.

YouTube Search Functions

You can search:

Search Videos by Keywords

Search Videos by Channels

Search Comments by Video Link

This is useful for comment targeting and competitor channel analysis.

Quora Search Functions

The following are supported:

Search Questions by Keywords

Search Recommended Questions

Search Answers of Question

Search Answers of User

Quora search allows users to identify high-intent discussions and active contributors.

Pinterest Search Functions

Advanced Content Explorer supports the following Pinterest searches:

Search Posts by Keywords

Discover pins related to specific topics or phrases.

Search Posts by Users

Extract pins from a specific Pinterest profile.

Search Comments by Post Link

Retrieve comments from a specific pin URL.

This is useful for product research, niche trend discovery, and audience targeting in visual-driven markets.

Reddit Search Functions

Reddit allows community-focused research and discussion monitoring:

Find discussions across subreddits based on keywords.

Extract posts created by a specific Reddit user.

Search Posts by Community

Pull posts from a specific subreddit.

Retrieve comments from a specific Reddit thread.

Reddit search is commonly used for intent discovery, market research, and identifying highly engaged niche communities.

RSS Feed Search

Advanced Content Explorer also supports RSS-based research:

Search Articles by RSS

This allows you to monitor blogs, news websites, and niche publications by importing RSS feeds and extracting article data.

Useful for content aggregation, monitoring industry updates, and content inspiration.

Financial & Market News Sources

Advanced Content Explorer includes support for financial data monitoring from:

StockTwits

– Search stock-related discussions and news.

Yahoo Finance

– Extract financial news and stock updates.

Reuters

– Monitor global financial and market news.

These sources are ideal for users operating in finance, trading communities, crypto niches, and market commentary automation.

How Advanced Content Explorer Is Used

Advanced Content Explorer is commonly used to:

Identify target audiences before running automation.

Build engagement lists.

Discover high-performing content formats.

Extract followers or group members for analysis.

Monitor niche discussions.

Important Notes

Results depend on account quality and proxy configuration.

Some searches require properly warmed-up accounts.

Platform limitations may affect data availability.

Always ensure compliance with platform terms of service.
