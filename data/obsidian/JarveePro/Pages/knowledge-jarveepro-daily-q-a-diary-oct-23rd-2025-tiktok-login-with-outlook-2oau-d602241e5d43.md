Introduction

The highlight was the clarification on how to log in TikTok accounts via

email and password using OAUTH tokens

— a crucial update since

Outlook no longer supports IMAP login

Additionally, the session shared a

productivity tip

on efficiently combining spreadsheet columns to streamline data preparation for JarveePro imports.

Q&A Conversation Summary

User: “Hello, I don’t get email verification on them.

Which one is the protocol?”

Answer:

When TikTok accounts fail to receive verification emails, the issue often stems from

Outlook’s IMAP access being disabled

TikTok accounts that log in via email using Outlook must now authenticate using

OAUTH tokens

instead of the traditional IMAP protocol.

If your previous JarveePro connection relied on

IMAP or cookies

, and those sessions have expired, you should:

Generate a new

OAUTH token

through your email provider.

Update it under

JarveePro > Account Settings

for the corresponding TikTok account.

This ensures

stable login sessions

and

uninterrupted automation

, even if cookies or IMAP sessions expire in the future.
