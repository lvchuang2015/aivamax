## Content

Upload Cover Photo

2025-05-19

Step 1: Choose "Campaigns" and Facebook, and select task.

Step 2: Upload cover photo.

Step 3: Complete task settings and click "OK" to start.

This page is for setting how cover photo are uploaded for Facebook accounts:

Change all Covers as the same

: All accounts will use the same cover.

Change all covers as different (stop it when not enough profile)

: Each account will use a different cover. If there aren’t enough covers, the task will stop.
