Introduction

Before starting Instagram automation, asking the right questions can save users from mistakes, confusion, and unnecessary support tickets. In this JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary for Dec. 22nd, 2025, we address some of the most frequently asked pre-sales and onboarding questions — including proxy requirements, Instagram photo and Reels posting, comment reply delays, and how JarveePro connects to Instagram. If you’re evaluating JarveePro or setting up your first Instagram account, this guide clarifies exactly what is supported, what is required, and how automation works behind the scenes.

Q1: Do I need a proxy for each Instagram account?

Answer:

Yes. Each Instagram account must use its own dedicated proxy. JarveePro does not include proxies in any plan, so users need to add their own.

Best practices:

Use 1 proxy per Instagram account

Avoid sharing proxies between accounts

Prefer residential or mobile proxies

Match the proxy country with the account’s registration location

Using shared or reused proxies significantly increases the risk of login challenges and temporary or permanent blocks.

Q2: Does JarveePro post directly to Instagram?

Yes. JarveePro posts directly to Instagram and does not require a Facebook Page, Meta Business Manager, or API-based connection.

This allows full control over posting, automation actions, and scheduling without Meta account dependencies.

Q3: Can JarveePro post photos to Instagram?

Yes. JarveePro fully supports direct photo posting to Instagram, including captions, hashtags, and scheduling.

Q5: Are there any music or copyright limitations when posting Reels?

JarveePro does not restrict music usage. However, Instagram may mute, limit, or remove content that violates its copyright or music policies. Users are responsible for ensuring compliance with Instagram’s rules.

Q6: Can I set delays for comment replies?

Yes. JarveePro allows custom delays for comment replies.

You can:

Set delays such as 5, 10, or 30 minutes

Apply hourly and daily rate limits

Enable randomized timing to better mimic human behavior

This helps reduce automation patterns and lowers detection risk.

Q7: What are the safest automation limits for Instagram?

Answer:

For new or private accounts, recommended starter limits are:

Follows: 20–40 per day

Likes: 50–80 per day

Unfollows: After 3–7 days

Spread actions evenly throughout the day

Avoid aggressive settings, especially during the first 7–14 days of automation.
