Feature Suggestion: News Feed Auto Scroll & Story Watch

User Suggestion:

“Daisy, this software is perfect, but in the next update, please make an option to scroll the news feed for example 10 seconds, or to watch 3–4 stories. This option will save our accounts.”

Response:

That’s a great idea! Feed scrolling and short story views help simulate authentic user activity, reducing detection risk and improving account trust.

Our development team is reviewing this feature for the upcoming

Behavior Simulation Update

, which will include:

Adjustable scrolling intervals (5s–20s)

Automatic story views (2–5 stories per session)

Randomized human-like delay behavior

These additions aim to make automation more natural and safer across Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok.

AI Agent Access Issue

User Report:

“When I click AI Agent, it says not included in your plan. I had some success with it last night — made a Twitter account post, follow, and like.”

Response:

The AI Agent module is not included in the Full License, it's a separate one which cost $2000. If it worked before, you may have temporarily accessed a test instance. Please confirm your current plan to ensure uninterrupted AI access.

Scheduled Tasks: Do They Start Automatically?

User Question (Jacob):

“If I schedule a task to a specific time and date, does it start automatically or do I have to start the task first?”

If the task is set to

Active which shows waiting

, it will trigger automatically at the defined time and date.

If

Paused or Inactive

, it will not run until you enable it manually. Always double-check the switch status for reliable scheduling.
