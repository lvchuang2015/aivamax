Q1: Can JarveePro continue posting if I keep the account open and start other actions?

A:

Yes. Even if you begin performing other actions or open another account, JarveePro will

not interrupt your posting tasks

. They will continue running until completion. Once a task is done, it closes automatically — simple and efficient multitasking!

Q2: Does JarveePro support real-time Twitter monitoring and automated actions?

Absolutely. JarveePro allows

real-time keyword monitoring

on Twitter. You can automatically

like, retweet, quote, and comment

on all new posts under selected keywords. This is perfect for supporting trending topics or promoting active campaigns.
