Introduction

TikTok is one of the fastest-growing social media platforms, and consistent engagement is key to growth. JarveePro empowers marketers, creators, and agencies to automate posting, engagement, and content distribution for multiple TikTok profiles efficiently. From liking videos to auto-following targeted users and scheduling posts, JarveePro ensures your growth strategy is smart, safe, and scalable.

This guide walks you through setting up TikTok campaigns in JarveePro, including advanced tips and best practices to maximize reach and engagement.

1. Accounts vs Campaigns: The Key Difference

In JarveePro,

TikTok accounts and campaigns are completely separate

Accounts:

Where you manage your TikTok profiles — login, credentials, proxies, and general account settings.

Campaigns:

Where you automate actions like posting, following, liking, commenting, and messaging. Campaigns

can target one or multiple accounts

at once.

Important: Adding an account does

not automatically create campaigns

, and creating a campaign does

not automatically attach it to an account

. You must select which accounts a campaign will run on.

2. Accessing TikTok Accounts & Campaigns

Step 1: Accounts

Navigate to

Accounts → TikTok

in the left menu. Add and configure your profiles with proxies, phone verification, and warm-up routines.

Step 2: Campaigns

Go to

Campaigns → TikTok

(separate from Accounts). Here you can create campaigns for posting, engagement, or messaging.

Step 3: Assign Accounts to Campaigns

When creating a campaign, you will be prompted to select one or more TikTok accounts. This allows you to run campaigns across multiple profiles efficiently.

3. TikTok Campaign Types

JarveePro offers several campaign types to automate growth and engagement:

Campaign Type

Purpose

Follow/Like Video

Automatically follow users or like videos to boost engagement.

Favorite Video

Save/favorite videos to engage with creators naturally.

Share Video

Share videos to your timeline for content distribution.

Post/Create & Schedule

Publish new TikTok posts with images, videos, links, and text on a schedule.

Auto-follow Specific Users

Target users via hashtags, locations, or competitor followers to grow organically.

Automated Messaging (DM)

Send personalized messages to specific users.

Comment

Post personalized or spintax-generated comments to increase visibility.

Watch Video / Search Video / Search People

Batch search for videos, users, or live streams and automatically watch them.

4. Automation & Targeting Options

Batch Operations:

Add multiple users, videos, or live streams at once.

Filters & Targeting:

Focus on hashtags, locations, or competitors’ followers.

Spintax Personalization:

Keep messages and comments varied and natural.

Human Behavior Simulation:

Warm up accounts and mimic real behavior to avoid TikTok flags.

5. Posting & Engagement Best Practices

Schedule Posts:

Keep consistent posting to maintain engagement.

Engagement Mix:

Combine liking, following, commenting, and messaging campaigns.

Dynamic Campaigns:

Rotate campaigns to avoid repetitive patterns.

Performance Monitoring:

Track likes, follows, comments, and shares for optimization.
