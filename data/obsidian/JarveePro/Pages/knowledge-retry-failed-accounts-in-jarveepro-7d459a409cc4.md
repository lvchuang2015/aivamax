Retry Failed Accounts in JarveePro

2025-10-30

The "Retry Failed Accounts" feature in JarveePro is designed to help users save time by not requiring them to restart a campaign from scratch when it encounters issues. This feature is especially helpful when certain accounts within a campaign fail due to errors, interruptions, or unexpected stops.

Here’s a breakdown of the options available when using "Retry Failed Accounts":

Duplicate Full Campaign

This option creates an exact copy of your campaign, including both the accounts that succeeded and those that failed. It’s essentially a fresh start for the entire campaign, ensuring that every aspect of the campaign is reset and retried, including successful accounts.

Copy Campaign Settings Only

This option allows you to create a brand-new campaign using the same settings (such as targets, filters, actions, etc.) as the original. It ignores the execution history, meaning the campaign starts as though it’s fresh. This is helpful if you just want to replicate the campaign's setup without the history.

Duplicate & Retry Failed Accounts

This is the most specific option. It allows the campaign to continue from where it left off, retrying only the accounts that failed. The accounts that have already succeeded will be skipped. This ensures that only the problematic accounts are retried without wasting time on those that already completed their tasks.

This feature is ideal for ongoing campaigns that might have interruptions or minor issues with specific accounts, allowing the campaign to continue smoothly without starting over.
