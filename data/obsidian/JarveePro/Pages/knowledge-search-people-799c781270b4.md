## Content

Search People

2025-07-20

Step 1: Open JarveePro→Click Limkedin→ Choose"Open Linkedin"→Click "OK" to start.

Step 2: Add and select account(s). Then choose model that you want. And add keyword according to your need. Then choose number of searching people and set up timeout(s). Lastly, click "Start" to start search. If you want to follow/unfollow/add people or send message, you can click "Fast Task" to start.
