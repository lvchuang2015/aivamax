JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary — Jan 12th, 2026 | Facebook Page Automation & AI Monitor Campaign Setup in JarveePro

2026-01-12

Introduction

Every day, JarveePro users raise practical, real-world questions about automation, setup, and performance. In today’s Q&A Diary, we cover two common Facebook automation topics: using personal accounts to act as business pages, and properly configuring AI Monitor campaigns so they don’t stay stuck in “waiting” mode. If you’re managing multiple pages or scaling engagement, this one’s for you.

Q1.

Facebook Account question:

I’m using a personal Facebook account in JarveePro that is the owner/admin of several business pages. Is there a video or instructions on how to make JarveePro like and comment

as the business pages

instead of my personal profile?

Answer:

Yes — this is possible, but it requires a

manual page switch inside the browser

JarveePro operates through the logged-in Facebook session. When your personal account manages multiple pages, Facebook still defaults actions to the personal profile unless you explicitly switch to a page identity.

Correct workflow:

Open the JarveePro embedded browser.

Log into Facebook with your personal account.

Manually switch to the target business page using Facebook’s page switcher.

Once switched, JarveePro will perform likes and comments as that page — not the personal profile.

This behavior is controlled by Facebook, not JarveePro. JarveePro follows the currently active identity in the browser session.

Q2.

AI Monitor Campaign question:

Is there a specific video or setup guide for configuring an AI Monitor campaign that likes and/or comments on posts from specific accounts? My manual campaigns work, but the AI Monitor campaign just stays on “waiting.”

Answer:

This is a very common issue — and usually

not a bug

AI Monitor campaigns are

condition-based

, not action-forced. If the campaign stays on “waiting,” it means the trigger conditions have not been met yet.

Key points to check:

AI Monitor only activates when

new qualifying posts

appear.

The target accounts must publish

fresh content after the campaign starts

Filters (keywords, engagement thresholds, content type) must actually match the post.

AI Monitor is not designed to retroactively act on old posts.

Why manual works but AI Monitor doesn’t:

Manual campaigns execute immediately on defined URLs or targets.

AI Monitor waits patiently for content that meets its logic rules.

Best practice:

Start AI Monitor campaigns

before

expected posting activity.

Keep filters simple during testing.

Confirm the account/page is actively posting new content.

Once conditions are met, the AI Monitor will automatically trigger likes or AI-generated comments.

Summary

JarveePro automation is powerful, but understanding how platforms behave is critical. Acting as a Facebook page requires manual identity switching, and AI Monitor campaigns rely entirely on real-time triggers. When configured correctly, both features save massive time while keeping actions natural and compliant.
