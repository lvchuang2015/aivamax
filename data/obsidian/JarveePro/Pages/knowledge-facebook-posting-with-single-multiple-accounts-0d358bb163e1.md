## Content

Facebook Posting with Single/Multiple Accounts

2025-10-19

Objective:

Manage posting across multiple Facebook accounts or posting multiple texts per account efficiently.

Setup Options:

Multiple Accounts – Single Text

Select multiple accounts in your Facebook campaign.

Posts Account Per Account--1 to 1 (Each of the 84 will make one post)

Times Allowed Per Post --1 to 1 Means no Duplicate, but make sure you added enough texts. If you only have 2 Text, it will only pull 2 accounts to post 2 different posts.

Expected Result:

2 post will be published on 2 accounts simultaneously.

If you added 84+ texts, then 84 posts will be published on 84 accounts.

Single Account – Multiple Texts

Posts per Account

→ Total posts the account will make.

Select a single account.

Assign

multiple text posts

for that account.

Configure:

Posts per Text per Account

→ How many times each text is posted by the account. 1 to 1 Means no Duplicate.

Expected Result:

The account will post 10-30 times and only push 10-30 texts from all the 99 texts.

What You Can do with JarveePro Facebook Posting Campaign:

Consistent posting according to your parameters.

Multi-account reach for a single message.

Multiple variations for content testing on a single account.

Multi-account reach for multiple different messages.
