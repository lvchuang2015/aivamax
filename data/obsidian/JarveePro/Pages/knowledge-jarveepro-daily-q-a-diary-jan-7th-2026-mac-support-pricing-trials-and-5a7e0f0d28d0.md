2026-01-07

Introduction

Every day, JarveePro users ask real, practical questions while setting up, pricing, upgrading, or scaling their automation workflows.

This Daily Q&A Diary captures the most common questions from

real conversations

and provides

clear, official answers

—no fluff, no guessing.

If you’re new to JarveePro or planning to upgrade, this one’s especially useful.

Q1. Can I use JarveePro on iOS or macOS?

Answer:

JarveePro is a

Windows-based desktop software

However, macOS users can still use JarveePro without limitations by choosing one of the following solutions:

Rent a

Windows VPS

(recommended for stability and 24/7 running)

Install

Parallels Desktop or a Windows virtual machine

on macOS

Both methods allow full access to all JarveePro features.

Q2. What is the price for Facebook and Instagram automation only?

The Facebook + Instagram automation license is priced at approximately

$1000

, covering those platforms specifically.

This option is suitable for users who only focus on Meta platforms.

Q3. What does the $2199 price include?

Answer:

The

$2199 license

is for the

JarveePro Full Version

It includes automation for

all supported platforms

, such as:

Facebook

Instagram

Twitter (X)

YouTube

TikTok

Additional supported networks

This version is recommended for agencies and multi-platform marketers.

Q4. Where can I try JarveePro or get a free license?

Answer:

Users can access

free licenses, trials, and official support

by joining the JarveePro community groups:

WhatsApp Group:

https://chat.whatsapp.com/Bd5tn2PpWs95rWXjg6VPBV

Telegram Group:

https://t.me/ExploreJarveePro

Community members receive announcements, support, and occasional free access opportunities.

Q5. Does JarveePro create social media accounts?

No. JarveePro

does not create social media accounts

JarveePro is designed for:

Automation

Engagement

Growth

Multi-account management

For account creation and verification, JarveePro is commonly used together with

PVACreator

, which handles bulk account creation.

Q6. Can my license be reset if I move JarveePro to a new machine?

Answer:

Yes.

If you upgrade your license or move JarveePro to a

new dedicated machine or VPS

, the support team can reset the serial number so you can continue using the software without interruption.
