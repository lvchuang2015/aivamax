How to Search Posts by Keywords?

Step 1

Choose LinkedIn--Choose Create--Choose Search Posts by Keywords--Input random task name--Click OK

Step 2

Select account--Input keywords--Specific settings--Click OK

Step 3

Select your task--Check the task--Click start

How to search posts by People ?

Choose LinkedIn Tasks--Unfold Search Posts--Select Search Posts by People(Double click)--Input random Task name--click ok

Select account--choose search amount- input url of user-Click ok

Select your task--check the task-and click run
