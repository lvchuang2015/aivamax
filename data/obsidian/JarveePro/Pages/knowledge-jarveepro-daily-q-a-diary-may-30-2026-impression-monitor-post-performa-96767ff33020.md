JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary – May 30, 2026 | Impression Monitor & Post Performance Tracking

2026-05-30

Introduction

In today’s JarveePro Daily Q&A session, users discussed a common issue with the

Impression Monitor / post performance tracking system

, including missing engagement data (views, likes, and other metrics) across some connected Instagram accounts. The conversation also highlighted UX improvement requests for account search and campaign filtering.

This diary entry explains how JarveePro handles post result visibility, why data may not appear for certain accounts, and what improvements are being considered.

Q1: Where can I see post results like views and likes?

Answer:

Post performance data (such as views, likes, impressions, and engagement metrics) is typically displayed inside the

Impression Monitor / post results section

, depending on campaign configuration and account support.

If a post has been successfully tracked, it will show:

View count

Likes

Engagement activity

Post-level performance stats

However, if tracking is not active or supported for a specific account or campaign type, the data may not appear even if the post was published successfully.

Q2: Why is no data showing even after monitoring is enabled?

If impression or engagement data is missing, common reasons include:

Account-level tracking limitations or missing permissions

Temporary system sync delay after posting

Q3: Why do some accounts show data while others don’t?

Answer:

This usually happens when:

Accounts are in different tracking states

Instagram API or session differences affect data retrieval

Some accounts were added before monitoring updates were fully enabled

Even if all accounts appear “normal,” tracking eligibility may differ per account internally.

Q4: Can we improve account search and filtering in JarveePro?

Yes — two strong feature requests were raised:

1. Account Search by Name

Instead of scrolling through large account lists, users want:

Direct search input for account names

Faster navigation across large account pools

2. Tag-Based Filtering for Campaigns

Users also requested:

Filtering campaigns by tags

Better grouping and organization of large automation setups

These improvements are aligned with improving usability for users managing 50+ accounts.

Summary

Today’s discussion around the JarveePro Impression Monitor highlighted two key themes:

Some users are experiencing missing post performance data due to tracking sync and system updates.

There is strong demand for better usability features like account search and campaign filtering.

The Impression Monitor system is actively being improved, and future updates aim to make tracking more consistent, faster, and easier to navigate across large-scale account setups.
