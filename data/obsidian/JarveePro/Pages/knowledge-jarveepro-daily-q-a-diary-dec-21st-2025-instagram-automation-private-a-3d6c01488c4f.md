2025-12-21

Introduction

Instagram automation sounds simple on the surface — add an account, turn on follow, watch the numbers grow. In reality, most problems users face with Instagram automation in 2025 are not software issues, but configuration mistakes, skipped steps, or unrealistic expectations. This Daily Q&A Diary addresses the most common questions we receive about connecting Instagram accounts to JarveePro, running automation on private profiles, setting safe daily limits, and understanding why actions sometimes don’t start. If you want stable growth without blocks, this guide is required reading.

Q1: I logged in, added my Instagram account, but I don’t know if it’s really connected. Did it work?

Answer:

Adding an account is

not the same as starting it

After adding your Instagram account:

Go to Accounts

→ Instagram

Select the account (checkbox on the left)

Click

Start

If the status changes from

Unknown

to

Running

, the account is connected correctly.

If you don’t click

, nothing will run.

JarveePro will not auto-start accounts for safety reasons.

Q2: How do I connect my Instagram account to JarveePro properly?

Answer:

To connect safely and avoid blocks:

Use a

dedicated proxy

(1 proxy = 1 IG account)

Add your account under

Accounts

→ Instagram

Click

Start

to initialize login

If Instagram challenges the login, JarveePro will show it clearly in the account status.

Q3: My Instagram account is private. Can I still grow followers with JarveePro?

Short answer:

Yes — but

with limits

What works on private accounts:

Follow users (they receive follow requests)

Like posts (from public profiles)

Warm-up actions

What does NOT magically change:

Private accounts grow

slower

Users must manually approve follow requests

Expect lower acceptance rates than public accounts

This is an Instagram limitation, not a JarveePro limitation.

Q4: I want to follow users daily, like their posts, then unfollow later. How do I do this safely?

Answer:

This is already built into JarveePro — and yes, the format is shown directly in the UI.

Best-practice setup:

Enable

Follow

Unfollow after X days

Set

daily limits conservatively

Do

NOT

Follow too fast

Unfollow immediately

Stack too many actions on a fresh account

JarveePro gives you the tools. Safety comes from

how you configure them

Q5: What are the safest limits to avoid Instagram blocks?

Recommended starter limits (new or private account):

Follows: 20–40/day

Likes: 50–80/day

Unfollows: after 3–7 days

Actions spread across the day (no bursts)

🚫 Do not run automation immediately after login

🚫 Do not copy aggressive settings from old YouTube videos

Instagram in 2025 ≠ Instagram in 2019.

Check how to set your accounts rate limits here:

https://blog.jarveepro.com/knowledge/JarveePro-3-Update/JarveePro-Rate-Limits-Avoid-Bans-with-Safe-Daily-Activity/5521

Q6: I followed the steps, but nothing is happening

Checklist before opening a ticket:

Account is

Started

Status is

Running

Actions are enabled

Daily limits are set

Targets are added

No error message shown

If any of the above is missing — JarveePro will not act.

Automation does not guess your intent.
