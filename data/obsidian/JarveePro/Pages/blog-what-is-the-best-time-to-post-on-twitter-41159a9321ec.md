What Is The Best Time To Post On Twitter

Share

2021-10-29

Twitter is a fast-paced social media site that requires you to consider more than just what you’re posting. Timing, in particular, is an important metric.

Twitter seems to be more popular at school, work and in transport. The "9 to 5" crowd often use their commute to and from work to interact with their mobile devices, especially social media. Public transport users in particular have between one and three hours a day to kill, making Twitter a major source of important information. Although people tend to be busier during the working day, social media traffic is also higher. People want to be distracted from the hustle and bustle of everyday life and they often turn to their mobile phones.

Of course, the best advice is to check your own analytics to track the best days and times to post tweets and interact with your specific audience. This is also true of other social media networks you may be using such as Facebook insight

One option is to access Twitter's native analytics platform, which is available to all accounts. You can view past tweet performance and look for trends that correlate posting times with higher levels of activity. This takes a lot of work and may not always be the most accurate, but over time it can provide powerful insights - and it's free.

We found that there is no best universal time to post on Twitter either.

Different businesses have different audiences, and so are their preferences and content posting time. Still, you can leverage the general guidelines to test and find the best time that works for your business.

Check out these stats from popular social media marketing companies suggesting when brands should post on Twitter.

Test out different posts timings using a Twitter scheduling tool

Unlike Facebook insight, Twitter doesn't directly tell you the best time to post. But, it still gives you enough insight to experiment to find out the best time. Simply break down your experiments over a period of time. Post your tweets in the morning, noon, and evening at specific times.
