In this article, I will share you some tips to reach new audiences on your LinkedIn page.

1. Post Status Updates

It is a quick and easy way for you to connect with your LinkedIn audience by sharing status updates.Most LinkedIn influencers choose to post longer updates about career journeys, the obstacles faced and the lessons learned along the way.Therefore,it is also a good option for you.

2.Publish and Share Articles on LinkedIn

LinkedIn prioritizes content published on its built in “Publishing Platform,so it’s really a good idea to use this platform to share long-form articles that showcase your thought leadership.If your connections like your article enough to share it, it can open doors to new audiences!

3. Post Video Content

It is reported that video posts are one of the most successful types of content on LinkedIn for users to share over other types of posts.Videos can bring to life your ideas and show your personality in a richer, more engaging way.

4.Post Consistently

One of the best LinkedIn tips for beginners is to be consistent. Not only with how often you post, but with your tone,brand marketing, messages, and the way you engage with your audience.There's always something to be gained from sticking to it.

5.Foster Community

One of the best ways to get involved in the LinkedIn community is by commenting and reacting to other people’s posts.Not only does post engagement help you build valuable connections by showing them you’re an active LinkedIn member, it can also help you demonstrate your industry knowledge to new audiences.
