JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary — Nov. 16th, 2025 ( Powerful Free Version Updates & Upcoming Report System)

2025-11-16

Introduction

Today’s Q&A hit a fun mix: users testing the limits of the Free Version, marketers asking about platform-specific licensing, and a lot of curiosity around Facebook scraping and the upcoming Report System. And honestly? I love seeing people shocked at how powerful JarveePro Free Version actually is. If someone thought Free meant “limited,” today proved the opposite — it’s absolutely capable of running full operations without a paywall.

Q1: “If I add Twitter + YouTube licenses, how much will be added to my plan? If I only need YouTube, is it possible?”

Answer:

Yes, both options are possible — JarveePro’s licensing system is fully modular.

Adding

Twitter + YouTube

simply adds the price of both licenses on top of your current plan.

If you only want

YouTube

, you can enable just that.

No forced bundles, no unnecessary upsells — take only what you need.

Q2: “I’m only using the Free Version. Can I still run multiple campaigns at once?”

Yes — and this surprises a lot of people.

JarveePro Free Version is extremely powerful

, not a trial or a demo. You can:

Run

multiple accounts simultaneously

Add

unlimited accounts

Manage platforms like Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and Twitter

Automate engagement

Use privacy and safety features like

Fingerprint Browser

Cookie Isolation

, and

Built-In Proxy Tools

The Free Version is designed to be

real

, not restricted.

Q3: “Why do I get different scraping results compared to my friend when scraping Facebook Pages?”

Answer:

Different results usually come from differences in:

Account history & trust score

Location and IP

Previous engagement history

Interest-based feed personalization

Different filters or keywords

JarveePro always pulls

the maximum data your unique account can access

, which varies from user to user. No two accounts see the exact same Pages data on Facebook — that’s by design.

Q4: “Is the new Impression Monitor update rolling out for everyone?”

Yes — the rollout is global.

The latest version includes:

Smarter, more accurate impression tracking

Better hourly refresh intervals

Improved performance for users with large accounts

Clearer visual patterns and analytics

And yes — the upcoming

Report System

is almost ready. The team is finalizing formatting, layout styles, and export quality before releasing it to all users.

Nov. 16th brought a spotlight on something many overlook: just how strong JarveePro’s Free Version truly is. From multiple campaigns to unlimited accounts and built-in safety tools, the Free Version isn’t a teaser — it’s a full automation engine.

More insights tomorrow — and as always, JarveePro keeps evolving faster than your competition can keep up.
