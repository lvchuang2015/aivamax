JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary – Feb 14th, 2026 | Free License, Email Scraping, Facebook Group Campaigns & Setup Questions

2026-02-14

Introduction

Today’s Q&A covers common questions from new and existing users regarding:

Free license access

Email scraping capabilities

Facebook group auto-posting

Campaign setup and group targeting

Coupon and payment clarification

These are practical onboarding questions many first-time users ask before and after purchasing JarveePro.

Q1: How do I get the free license? I registered but didn’t receive a link.

Answer:

JarveePro free licenses are distributed through official community channels.

If you registered but did not receive an email, you may need to:

Join the official WhatsApp group

Join the official Telegram group

Follow the pinned instructions inside those communities

Free licenses are often distributed through controlled channels to prevent abuse.

Q2: Can JarveePro scrape emails from the internet or Google?

No. The standard paid version of JarveePro does

not scrape emails from the internet or Google

JarveePro is primarily a

social media automation and campaign management platform

, not a bulk email harvesting tool.

For highly specific data extraction requirements, custom development may be required.

Q3: Can I use JarveePro to auto-post in Facebook groups?

Answer:

Yes. JarveePro supports Facebook group posting campaigns.

You can:

Create automated posting campaigns

Target specific groups

Schedule posts

Apply limits and pacing controls

To add groups to a campaign, you typically:

Add group URLs manually

Or use built-in search functions to discover eligible groups

Manual review is recommended to ensure groups allow posting and that content aligns with group rules.

Q4: How do I add existing Facebook groups I’ve already joined into a campaign?

Groups can be added to campaigns in two main ways:

Manually adding group URLs

Using the built-in group search functionality in JarveePro Advancced Content Explorer

Users should verify:

The account has posting permissions

Admin restrictions are respected

JarveePro does not provide tools to bypass administrator restrictions or platform safeguards. If a group blocks posting, the safest approach is to respect the restriction.

Q5: I used a coupon code that wasn’t intended for JarveePro. What happens?

Answer:

Coupon codes are product-specific.

If a code intended for another product is used accidentally, the pricing difference must be adjusted to match the correct JarveePro plan.

Users are encouraged to confirm:

Plan type

License duration

Discount eligibility

before completing payment.

Summary

Today’s discussion highlights important onboarding realities:

Free licenses require joining official communities

JarveePro does not scrape public internet emails

Facebook group automation is supported

Admin restrictions cannot be bypassed

Coupon codes must match the correct product

JarveePro is built for structured automation — not shortcuts or policy violations.

Clear setup and correct expectations lead to long-term success.
