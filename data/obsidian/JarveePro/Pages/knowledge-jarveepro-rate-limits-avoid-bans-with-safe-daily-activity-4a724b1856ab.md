JarveePro Rate Limits: Avoid Bans with Safe Daily Activity

2025-12-15

JarveePro’s

Rate Limit feature

is designed to keep your accounts safe while still allowing automation at scale. The goal is

human-like activity

, not maximum speed. Following these guidelines reduces the risk of bans or soft-bans.

General Principles

Start Slow:

Every new account needs a warm-up period to mimic natural activity.

Incremental Growth:

Increase posts, likes, follows, and messages gradually.

Respect Platform Limits:

Each social network has its own thresholds; exceeding them is the fastest way to trigger a ban.

Use Rate Limits:

Always configure JarveePro’s per-day and per-hour limits. Never disable them.

Q: After the warm-up phase, what are the limits in terms of posts, retweets, replies, etc. per day for Twitter?

A:

After the warm-up phase, safe daily activity limits on Twitter depend on account age, trust, and past activity. The goal is to mimic human behavior to reduce the risk of bans or soft-bans.

Twitter-Specific Warm-Up Phase

For Twitter, the warm-up period is crucial:

Days 1–3:

1–3 tweets, 5–10 likes, 2–5 follows, 1–2 replies

Days 4–7:

3–5 tweets, 20–50 likes, 10–20 follows, 5–10 replies

After Week 1:

Gradually increase to the general safe limits table above

Tips:

Retweets are counted separately from original tweets. Spread activity throughout the day.

Slow and steady wins the safety game

. Rapid spikes = high risk of suspension.

Additional Tips for Safety

Use different actions per account:

Don’t do maximum posts, likes, follows, and messages all at once.

Randomize timing:

JarveePro’s scheduling tools can randomize intervals to mimic human behavior.

Monitor notifications:

If the platform flags an account, scale down immediately.

Check the Rate Limit logs:

JarveePro logs allow you to review hourly/daily activity to stay within safe limits.

Summary

Account limits are

not fixed

— they depend on:

Account age and trust

Platform type and activity history

Warm-up progression and scaling speed

JarveePro Rate Limits

let you configure all of this safely. Start conservative, monitor activity, and gradually scale to maximize automation without risking bans.
