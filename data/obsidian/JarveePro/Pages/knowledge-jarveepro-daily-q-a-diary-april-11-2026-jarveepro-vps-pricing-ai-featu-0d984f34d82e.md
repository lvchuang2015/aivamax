Introduction

Understanding how JarveePro is deployed, licensed, and scaled is essential before getting started. Many users—especially beginners—get confused by terms like VPS Mode, licensing options, and AI add-ons due to unclear explanations or mixed pricing information.

This Q&A guide provides clear, structured answers to the most common questions, helping you make informed decisions and avoid unnecessary costs or setup mistakes.

Q1: What is VPS Mode?

Answer:

VPS Mode refers to running JarveePro on a

Virtual Private Server (VPS)

instead of your local computer.

A VPS is a remote machine that operates independently from your personal device. When using VPS Mode, JarveePro runs 24/7 without relying on your computer being turned on.

Key Benefits:

Continuous operation (no downtime when your PC is off)

Improved stability for long automation sessions

Better account management at scale

Reduced risk when combined with proper proxy setup

In short, VPS Mode is recommended for users managing multiple accounts or running long-term automation campaigns.

Q2: What is “10 VPS Lifetime”?

“10 VPS Lifetime” is a

one-time license

that allows you to run JarveePro on up to

10 VPS machines simultaneously

Important Clarification:

This license covers

software usage only

It does

not include the VPS servers themselves

Users must still obtain VPS hosting separately, either:

From JarveePro’s own service

Or from third-party VPS providers

This option is designed for advanced users or agencies scaling across multiple environments.

Q3: Do I need to purchase a VPS from JarveePro?

Answer:

No, purchasing a VPS from JarveePro is

optional

You have two choices:

Use JarveePro’s VPS service (simpler setup)

Use an external VPS provider (more flexibility and often lower cost)

Both options are fully supported, and the choice depends on your technical preference and budget.

Q4: How much does JarveePro cost monthly?

JarveePro offers a

monthly subscription plan priced at $397/month

This plan provides access to the core automation features and supports multi-platform usage, although users can choose to focus on a single platform (e.g., Facebook) initially.

Q5: What are AI Monitor and AI Agent? Are they monthly?

AI Monitor and AI Agent are

optional add-ons

and are

not billed monthly

AI Monitor: $2000 (one-time fee)

AI Agent: $2000 (one-time fee)

These are advanced tools designed to enhance automation efficiency and safety.

Q6: What is the difference between AI Monitor and AI Agent?

Answer:

AI Monitor:

Monitor Trends

Monitor Specific Accounts

Monitor Keywords

Monitor Competitors

Monitor Industry

AI Agent:

Actively manages and optimizes campaigns

Adjusts automation behavior dynamically

Automates decision-making for scaling and performance

Summary of Difference:

AI Monitor = monitor and scraping trends

AI Agent = execution and optimization

Q7: Can I start with only Facebook and upgrade later?

Answer:

Yes, you can start by focusing on

Facebook only

and expand to other platforms later.

JarveePro supports multi-platform automation, but users are not required to use all features immediately. Starting with one platform is often recommended to test strategies and optimize performance before scaling.

Summary

JarveePro is a powerful automation platform, but understanding its structure is key to using it effectively.

VPS Mode ensures stable, always-on automation

“10 VPS Lifetime” is a software license, not hosting

Monthly pricing starts at $397

AI tools are optional, one-time investments

Users can begin with a single platform and scale gradually

By starting with the core features and expanding strategically, users can maximize efficiency while minimizing unnecessary costs.
