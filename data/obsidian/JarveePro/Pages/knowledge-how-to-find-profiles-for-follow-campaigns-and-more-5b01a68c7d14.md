Overview

JarveePro’s

Advanced Content Explorer

is a powerful tool that lets you

discover social media profiles, pages, groups, and posts

relevant to your niche. By using it, you can quickly generate lists of URLs that can be used to:

Start

Follow Campaigns

Add friends

Send

direct messages (DMs)

Join groups

Interact with posts efficiently

This article will guide you through using the Content Explorer to find

target profiles

safely and effectively.

Step 1: Open the Advanced Content Explorer

In JarveePro, navigate to the

Content Explorer

tab.

Select

Advanced Search

to access the full range of filters and options.

Choose the platform you want to explore (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, etc.).

Step 2: Configure Your Search Filters

Advanced Content Explorer allows you to refine your search to

target the right audience

Keywords / Hashtags

– Search for specific topics or niches.

Account Type

– Pages, profiles, groups, or public posts.

Tip: Use multiple filters together to get

high-quality results

instead of generic profiles.

Step 3: Load Profiles from Content Explorer

Once you’ve found relevant profiles,

select them

in the explorer.

Click

Export to CSV

or

Load to JarveePro

directly.

The list of profile URLs can now be used for:

Follow Campaigns

– Automatically follow your target users.

Friend Requests

– Send friend requests safely.

Direct Messages (DMs)

– Automate messaging campaigns.

Group Engagement

– Join groups or interact with posts.

Tip: Always review your list before starting campaigns to ensure URLs are valid and active.

Step 4: Best Practices for Using Content Explorer Lists

Segment your list

– Group profiles by niche, location, or engagement level for smarter campaigns.

Respect platform rules

– Avoid adding too many friends or following too quickly to prevent account restrictions.

Regularly refresh your list

– Profiles may become inactive; keep your targets updated.

Step 5: Combining with Other Campaigns

Follow Campaigns:

Use your scraped list to automatically follow relevant accounts.

Auto DM Campaigns:

Send personalized messages to your target audience.

Add Friends Campaigns:

Send friend requests in batches using valid profile URLs.

Group Campaigns:

Use the list to identify and join groups that fit your niche.

Tip: Using Content Explorer ensures your campaigns are

targeted, relevant, and more likely to succeed

Summary

The JarveePro Advanced Content Explorer is your

launchpad for targeted campaigns

. By gathering high-quality profile URLs, you can:

Grow followers

Expand your network

Send DMs

Join relevant groups

Increase engagement across platforms

Always use these features responsibly to

avoid restrictions or penalties

on social media platforms.
