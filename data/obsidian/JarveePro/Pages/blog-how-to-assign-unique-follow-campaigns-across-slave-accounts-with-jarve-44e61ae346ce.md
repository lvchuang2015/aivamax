How to Assign Unique Follow Campaigns Across Slave Accounts with JarveePro

Share

2025-08-05

If you’re using the follow/unfollow method with mother/slave accounts, chances are you’ve faced this common issue: every slave account ends up following the same users from your scraped list. Not only is this inefficient, but it’s also a red flag to Instagram’s algorithm. In this post, we’ll walk you through how to use JarveePro to distribute unique follow campaigns across your slave accounts — maximizing efficiency and minimizing risk.

The Problem with Overlapping Follow Campaigns

Let’s say you’ve scraped 1,000 Instagram usernames. You assign your slave accounts to follow from this list. Here’s what usually happens:

Slave #1 follows users #1–100

Slave #2 follows users #1–100

Slave #3 follows users #1–100

Result? All your accounts are following the same people, making your strategy look spammy and potentially triggering bans.

What You Want Instead

A more efficient approach:

Slave #2 follows users #101–200

Slave #3 follows users #201–300

Each slave follows a unique segment of the same scraped list. This way, you cover more ground, avoid overlap, and keep your activity looking organic.

How to Do This in JarveePro

JarveePro makes it easy to assign segmented follow tasks. Here’s how:

Step 1: Scrape Your User List

Use JarveePro’s Search tools or import a .txt/.csv file of usernames. Make sure the list is complete and clean.

Step 2: Split the List Manually or Automatically

Manual Method: Divide the full list into smaller parts using Excel/Notepad.

Automatic Method: Use JarveePro’s list-splitting feature to distribute evenly.

Step 3: Assign Lists to Accounts

Slave #1 gets List A (users #1–100)

Slave #2 gets List B (users #101–200)

And so on.

Step 4: Create a Follow Campaign

Create a new Follow Campaign.

Select multiple slave accounts for the campaign.

Upload the user list.

Set the daily limits, delays, and filters.

Why This Works

Avoids account duplication

Increases reach and engagement

Looks more organic to Instagram

Reduces chance of mass bans

Pro Tips for Large Campaigns

Use high-quality proxies (1:1 or 1:2 max)

Set random delays to avoid patterns

Monitor task logs regularly

Rotate lists weekly for freshness

Real Results from the Field

One of our users shared this issue:

“Our slaves were all following the same top 10 scraped users. We wanted each slave to follow a different segment. JarveePro made this possible and now we’re scaling 10x more efficiently.”

Conclusion--

Smart Automation Wins

Following the same users across 50 slave accounts is a quick way to get shadowbanned. But using JarveePro’s Advanced Follow features, you can run unique, scalable follow campaigns that grow your network — not destroy it.
