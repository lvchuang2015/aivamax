Q1: Can I use JarveePro's Auto DM feature without spamming users?

Absolutely — and that’s one of the most common questions we receive.

The

Auto DM feature

in JarveePro is designed for

personalized outreach

, not mass spam. You can set message limits, random delays, and spintax variations to sound natural while maintaining compliance with platform limits.

Here’s a simple best-practice example:

“{Hey|Hi|Hello}! 👋 I’m using JarveePro’s auto DM feature to connect with people in my niche.

If this message isn’t relevant, please ignore — I totally understand. 🙏”

By combining

custom triggers

and

smart filtering

, you can reach your target audience effectively without crossing platform boundaries.

Q2:

Can I manage multiple Auto DM campaigns from one dashboard?

Yes.

JarveePro’s

Multi-Account Management

module allows users to create and schedule Auto DM campaigns across all connected platforms — Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and more — all from a single interface.

You can:

Assign unique message templates per account.

Set randomized schedules to avoid pattern detection.

Track response rates with

AI Monitor

, which evaluates engagement and conversation outcomes.

For agencies managing 10+ clients, this feature alone saves hours daily.

Q3: Can I test Auto DM before upgrading to the full version?

Yes. You can access the

Auto DM feature

in

Any Paid Version.

To unlock unlimited messaging with TIkTok, you’ll need the

Full Version

The Free plan is perfect for testing how your accounts perform before committing to a full license — ideal for small-scale creators and testers.
