Search Comment Users of Twitter Posts with JarveePro Advanced Content Explorer

2026-03-19

Introduction

Finding the right audience is no longer about guessing hashtags or scraping random users — it’s about targeting people who are already engaged.

With

JarveePro Advanced Content Explorer

, you can now

extract users who commented on specific posts

, giving you direct access to highly relevant, high-intent audiences.

This feature allows you to move beyond traditional automation and step into

precision targeting at scale

What Is “Search Comment Users of Posts”?

This feature lets you:

👉 Input a post URL (e.g., from X / Twitter)

👉 Extract users who commented on that post

👉 Build a targeted user list

Use JarveePro to engage, follow, or message them

Instead of targeting cold audiences, you’re now reaching:

People actively engaging in your niche

Users already interested in similar topics

High-probability prospects for outreach

Why This Matters (The Real Advantage)

Most automation tools still rely on:

Keyword search

Hashtags

Followers of large accounts

The problem?

These audiences are broad and often low-intent

With comment-based targeting, you get:

Higher relevance

→ they already care

Better response rates

→ they’re active

Stronger conversions

→ warmer audience

This is the difference between:

Spraying messages

vs

Starting conversations with the right people

How to Use It (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Find a Relevant Post

Choose a post that:

Is in your niche

Has strong engagement

Contains your target audience

Example:

A viral post about AI, eCommerce, or automation.

Step 2: Copy the Post URL

Grab the direct link of the post (e.g., from X).

Step 3: Open Advanced Content Explorer

Inside JarveePro:

Navigate to

Advanced Content Explorer

Select

Search Comment Users of Posts

Step 4: Paste the URL

Insert the post link

Start the extraction process

JarveePro will automatically:

Collect users who commented

Build a usable list for automation

Step 5: Export or Send to Campaigns

You can now:

Add users to Follow tools

Send Direct Messages

Engage with their content

Build long-term outreach campaigns

Best Use Cases

1. Competitor Audience Mining

Find posts from competitors → extract their engaged audience → connect with them.

2. Viral Post Targeting

Jump on trending posts → capture attention while interest is high.

3. Niche Community Building

Identify active users in specific discussions → build relationships over time.

4. Lead Generation

Target users already discussing problems your product solves.

Pro Tips (This Is Where Results Change)

Don’t just blast messages —

start conversations

Reference the post when possible (feels natural)

Segment users based on intent (builders vs casuals)

Combine with content automation for full funnel coverage

From Data to System

This feature isn’t just about scraping users.

It’s about building a system:

Discover → Extract → Engage → Convert

When combined with JarveePro automation, you can create:

Continuous audience sourcing

Scalable outreach workflows

Consistent growth loops

Summary

The

Search Comment Users of Posts

feature transforms how you find and connect with your audience.

Instead of cold targeting, you get:

Warmer leads

Better engagement

More efficient growth

In a landscape where attention is everything, the real advantage is talking to people who are already paying attention.
