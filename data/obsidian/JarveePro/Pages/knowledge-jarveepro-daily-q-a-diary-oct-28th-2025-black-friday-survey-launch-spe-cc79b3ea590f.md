Today marks a big day for the JarveePro community — the official launch of our

Black Friday 2025 Community Survey!

Your voice matters. Every click, every vote, and every suggestion helps determine how we design our limited-time Black Friday deals.

It’s a community-driven voting campaign launched on

Twitter (X)

that allows users to choose what kind of rewards, bonuses, or price cuts they’d love to see during our

Black Friday event

From discounts on automation tools to bundle offers and lifetime plans — you have the power to decide what’s coming.

Vote Now

Q2: Why did JarveePro launch a community poll this year?

Because our users are not just customers — they’re collaborators.

We’ve always believed in

building with our community

, not just for it. The Black Friday Poll is a way to say thank you and ensure our offers truly match what marketers, agencies, and automation experts want most.

Q3: What can voters expect after voting?

Once the poll ends, we’ll analyze all community votes and

reveal the final list of offers

in the coming weeks.

Expect surprises like:

Massive percentage discounts

Free upgrade options

Bonus campaign addons

Access to new AI features

And yes, those who vote early might even get an

exclusive early-access link

before the deals go public.
