JarveePro AI Monitor is your always-on social radar. It keeps track of everything that matters in your niche — from user activity to trending hashtags to breaking keywords.

You can monitor:

Facebook users or groups

→ Auto-like or comment the moment they post.

Instagram hashtags

→ Engage instantly when a new post appears.

Twitter users or keywords

→ Auto-reply or retweet when they tweet about your target topic.

YouTube keywords

→ React to new videos as soon as they go live.

RSS, Yahoo Finance, Reuters, and Stocktwits

→ Auto-share or comment on breaking news and trends.

While most tools just notify you, JarveePro takes action —

commenting, reposting, or DMing

automatically.

5.

How to Set It Up (Correct Flow: Advanced Content Explorer → AI Monitor)

Total time:

~5–15 minutes depending on how many queries you add.

Open JarveePro → Advanced Content Explorer

This is where you create and refine the sources you want the AI Monitor to watch: platform queries (Facebook pages, Instagram hashtags, Twitter keywords), RSS feeds, Yahoo Finance tickers, Reuters, Stocktwits, or custom URLs.

Build a single

collection

for each monitoring objective (e.g., “Brand Mentions”, “Crypto Tickers”, “Top Hashtags”).

Create & Test Your Queries

For each collection, add target users, hashtags, keywords, or feed URLs.

Use the Explorer’s preview to validate results (sample items, relevance, noise level). Tune keywords, negative keywords, or filters until results are clean.

Save the collection with a clear name (this is what you’ll select later in AI Monitor).

Optional: Group & Tag Results

Tag or categorize collections (e.g., “finance”, “influencers”, “support leads”) so you can map different actions and accounts later.

Go to JarveePro → AI Monitor

Create a new Monitor campaign. Instead of writing new content or post urls here,

choose the saved results

from Advanced Content Explorer as the Monitor source. This ensures the Monitor only receives pre-filtered, validated content.

Choose Campaign(s)

Pick one or multiple

Campaign

to run when the Monitor detects a match:

AI Comment

Auto Like

Auto Reply / AI Reply

Auto DM

Auto Post / Broadcast

, etc.

Configure the

settings (use spintax for comments, choose AI tone settings if available).

Select Target Accounts / Account Group

Choose which JarveePro accounts (or account group/category) will perform the actions. Map proxies or VPS per account if needed.

Set Frequency, Limits & Filters

Define rate limits, per-account caps, time windows, and content filters (e.g., minimum likes, language filters, exclude words). This prevents spammy behavior and keeps activity natural.

Test in Staging / Small Scale

Start with a small account group (2–5 accounts) and observe behavior. Review comments for relevance and adjust templates/filters as needed.

Activate & Scale

Once tests are clean, activate the Monitor across your full account groups. Monitor performance via logs and JarveePro reports. Use Content Explorer to periodically refine collections.
