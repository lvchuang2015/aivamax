Why People Buy Instagram Followers and Likes

Social Proof

When people see that an account has thousands of followers and plenty of likes on posts, they're more likely to trust it, follow it, and engage. Buying followers inflates your count but doesn't guarantee engagement. Often, these followers never like or comment on your posts.
