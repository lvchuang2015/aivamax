What Happens When I Stop or Pause a Task in JarveePro?

2025-10-17

When you pause or stop a campaign before it’s finished,

JarveePro automatically saves the task’s progress

This prevents data loss and lets you decide how to resume later.

When you restart the campaign, a confirmation window appears:

Dialog Explanation

Message:

“A previous task was not completed.

Do you want to continue from where you left off or restart the task?”

You’ll be given

two options

Continue

→ Resume the task from the last saved checkpoint.

Recommended if you want to pick up right where the campaign stopped.

Restart

→ Begin the entire task again from the start.

Best if you’ve changed the campaign setup, content, or accounts.

Why This Feature Matters

This smart checkpoint system is especially useful for

long-running or large multi-account campaigns

, where system restarts, schedule pauses, or internet issues can interrupt execution.

JarveePro ensures that when you resume, your workflow remains

efficient, consistent, and duplication-free

— helping your automation continue seamlessly.
