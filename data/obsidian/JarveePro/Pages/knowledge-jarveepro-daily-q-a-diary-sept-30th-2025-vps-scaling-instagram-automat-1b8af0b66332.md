Q3:

“I’m running 500 accounts simultaneously. How can I ensure they won’t be banned for using the same IP?”

You cannot safely run hundreds of accounts on the same IP.

To protect them:

Use

proxy-per-account

(residential or mobile).

Keep

sticky sessions

for IP consistency.

Separate accounts with

profile isolation

(JarveePro Fingerprint-Binding).

Apply

gradual warm-up schedules

and randomized actions.
