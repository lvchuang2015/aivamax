How to Export Accounts in JarveePro (3 Different Methods)

2025-10-23

JarveePro offers several flexible ways to export account data, allowing marketers and automation users to back up, migrate, or share their setup safely. You can export usernames, passwords, proxies, cookies, and even browser cache to ensure a seamless transition between systems or VPS environments.

Below are the

three main export methods

available inside the JarveePro Accounts section.

Method 1: Export Accounts via Toolbar (Full Account Data)

This is the most detailed and customizable method to export all your account information.

Steps:

Go to the

Accounts

section.

Click the

Export

button in the top toolbar.

In the export window, select which

columns

to include:

Platform

– Platform name (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.).

Category

– Group or project label.

Status

– Account status (Normal, Failed, etc.).

Account (Username/Email)

– Account login name.

Password

– Encrypted account password.

Proxy

– Proxy address assigned to the account.

Cookie

– Browser cookies for session restoration.

2FA

– Two-factor authentication code.

Token

– API or session token.

Choose file type:

.csv

– Ideal for spreadsheets or database imports.

.xlsx

– For Excel compatibility.

.txt

– For quick backups or scripts.

Adjust optional settings (Row/Column separators, include headers).

Click

Export

to save the file.

Use Case:

Export usernames, passwords, proxies, and cookies for account migration or backup.

Method 2: Export to ZIP (Includes Browser Cache)

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kNus016LHk8

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtIWreUgSTo

This method creates a complete backup that includes each account’s

browser cache

, making it ideal when transferring accounts between devices or VPSs without needing to re-login.

Steps:

Go to

Accounts → More (⋯)

on the toolbar.

Select

“Export to zip (includes browser cache)”

Choose where to save the ZIP file.

The exported ZIP will include:

Account credentials

Session cookies

Browser fingerprints and cache

Use Case:

Transfer accounts to another computer or VPS and continue automation without verification issues.

Method 3: Export Browser Cache Only (For JarveePro VPS Version)

This method is designed for the

JarveePro VPS version

, allowing users to

send the browser cache

directly to the VPS that the accounts are bound to. The accounts themselves are still

managed through the main JarveePro dashboard

, but their cache and sessions are maintained on the VPS for stability and security.

Steps:

Navigate to

Accounts → More (⋯)

in the toolbar.

Click

“Export browser cache to zip.”

Send or map the exported browser cache to the corresponding

VPS

where the account is hosted.

Once mapped, accounts continue to run and be managed through the

main JarveePro dashboard

, while the VPS handles login sessions and automation tasks.

Use Case:

For VPS-based setups where browser cache and session management are performed remotely, ensuring seamless operation without exposing account credentials.

Summary

JarveePro makes it easy to export your accounts in three different ways:

Full Account Export (.CSV/.XLSX/.TXT)

– export all details including username, password, proxy, and token.

Export to ZIP (with Browser Cache)

– create a complete backup for quick account migration.

Export Browser Cache Only

– Sends only the browser cache to the VPS; accounts still managed on the main dashboard.

By using the right export method, you can ensure your accounts, sessions, and proxies are safely preserved and easily restored when needed.
