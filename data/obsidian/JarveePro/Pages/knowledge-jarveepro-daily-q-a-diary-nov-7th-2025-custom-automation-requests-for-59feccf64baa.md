JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary – Nov. 7th, 2025(Custom Automation Requests for Quora & Telegram)

2025-11-07

Every day brings new insights and collaborations to the JarveePro ecosystem. On

November 7th, 2025

, two long-term premium clients reached out with requests for

custom platform integrations

—one for

Quora automation

and another for

Telegram automation

Both clients, representing well-established marketing groups, recognized JarveePro’s flexibility and reliability as their preferred automation framework. Their feedback sparked discussions that could lead to two major feature rollouts benefiting the entire user base.

The Quora integration proposal aims to help users analyze, post, and engage automatically across question threads, while the Telegram integration focuses on message sending, group management, and audience scraping—similar to the TG Hunter 2.0 system, but more seamlessly built into JarveePro’s all-in-one interface.

Development Insights:

Quora Integration Proposal:

Estimated cost of

$5000

for full implementation, including smart post scheduling, comment monitoring, and content analytics.

This feature would allow marketers to automate engagement and gather insights from one of the internet’s most knowledge-driven communities.

Telegram Integration Proposal:

Custom automation priced at

$2000

, covering message broadcasting, group invitations, and user parsing.

Once developed, users could run Telegram campaigns directly within JarveePro, combining automation precision with multi-platform control.

These requests highlight how advanced users are shaping JarveePro’s direction—bridging social engagement, content growth, and communication automation under a unified tool.

Why Clients Prefer Integration with JarveePro:

At the center of these new requests is

the JarveePro dashboard

— a data-driven powerhouse that already visualizes growth, engagement, and campaign performance across Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, and TikTok.

Instead of juggling separate tools, clients see the value in extending this environment to include

Quora and Telegram

. With JarveePro’s multi-platform charts, rankings, and real-time analytics, they can automate tasks

and

measure results from one place. That’s efficiency — not just automation.

Future Impact:

Once implemented, these integrations will enable all JarveePro users to reach audiences on even more networks, from community discussions on Quora to direct communication via Telegram. This expansion keeps JarveePro ahead of the curve as the most complete automation suite on the market.

Summary:

The

November 7th Q&A Diary

showcases JarveePro’s evolution driven by client vision. New automation ideas emerge daily, but what keeps users loyal is the

clarity and control

of its analytics system.

By adding Quora and Telegram into this ecosystem, JarveePro reinforces its promise — not just to automate, but to

monitor, optimize, and grow smarter

across every social channel.
