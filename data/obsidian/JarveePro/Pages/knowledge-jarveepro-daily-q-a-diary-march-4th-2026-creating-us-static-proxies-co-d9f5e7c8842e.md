Introduction

Automation setup can feel overwhelming — especially when you’ve just invested money and nothing seems to work.

On March 4th, 2026, a user who topped up $50 on IPFarming struggled with creating US static proxies, connecting them to JarveePro, and resolving repeated Instagram login errors.

This diary entry breaks down the exact causes of those issues and provides clear, step-by-step guidance for beginners who are setting up proxies and automation for the first time.

If you are new to proxy management, this guide will help you avoid common mistakes that can lead to login failures or account bans.

Q1: I topped up $50 on IPFarming. How do I create 6 US static proxies and connect them to JarveePro? Is there a tutorial?

Answer:

After topping up your balance on IPFarming, you must manually create the proxies inside your IPFarming dashboard. Topping up does not automatically generate proxies.

Step 1: Create US Static Proxies

Log in to your IPFarming account.

Navigate to the proxy creation section.

Select:

Country: United States

Proxy type: Static

Quantity: 6

Confirm and generate.

Once created, you will receive:

IP address

Port

Username

Password

Step 2: Connect Proxies to JarveePro

Open JarveePro.

Go to Accounts.

Select the account you want to bind.

Open Proxy Settings.

Enter:

Proxy IP

Test the proxy.

Save.

Each account should use a separate static proxy. Never share one proxy across multiple accounts unless you understand the risk.

Q2: Why does every Instagram account say “login information is incorrect” inside JarveePro, but works on other devices?

Answer:

This is usually not a password issue. The most common causes are:

1. Cookie Conflict

Multiple accounts were using the same imported cookie file.

When one cookie session is bound to multiple accounts:

Instagram detects unusual session behavior.

Accounts may trigger login errors.

You increase the risk of account bans.

Each account must use:

Its own unique cookie session

Or no imported cookies at all

If importing cookies, make sure they are generated individually per account.

2. Proxy & Account Mismatch

If you:

Logged in previously from a different IP

Suddenly switch to a new US static proxy

Instagram may challenge or block login.

Best practice:

Log into the account using the proxy first in a normal browser.

Confirm it works.

Then log in via JarveePro browser.

3. Missing Account Details (Twitter Case)

For Twitter accounts, email information was not added.

Some platforms require:

Email

Phone verification

Additional authentication

Always complete full account details before automation.

Q3: What was done to fix some accounts?

From the session:

Cookie conflicts were identified.

Some accounts were tested in local Chrome first.

Only accounts that worked in normal browser were used inside JarveePro.

Incorrect or shared cookie sessions were flagged as risky.

If an account cannot log in even in Chrome with the proxy enabled, the issue is not JarveePro — it’s the account or IP trust level.

Summary

Creating proxies on IPFarming requires manual generation after topping up. Each JarveePro account must be connected to a unique static proxy.

Login errors inside JarveePro are commonly caused by:

Shared or duplicated cookies

Proxy/account mismatch

Incomplete account information

Before assuming software issues, always test accounts in a normal browser using the assigned proxy.

Proper proxy hygiene and cookie management are critical for safe automation.
