JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary — Jan. 8th, 2026 | How to Automate Facebook Group Sharing for Leads (Real Case)

2026-01-08

Introduction

Today’s Q&A comes from a real estate broker in Egypt who relies heavily on

Facebook group sharing

to generate rental leads.

The user wasn’t looking for content ideas or marketing strategy—only

reliable, fully automated behavior

that works 24/7 without manual effort.

This diary focuses on

exact automation logic

, pricing clarity, and where JarveePro fits versus custom development.

Q1. Can JarveePro automate sharing a Facebook post from a personal account to multiple groups on a fixed schedule?

Answer:

Yes.

JarveePro can automate the following workflow using

Facebook personal accounts

Publish a post on the account’s timeline

Automatically share that

same original post

to Facebook groups where the account is already a member

No Page access required

No admin or moderator permissions required

This is

browser-based automation

, not Facebook API–based.

Q2. Can sharing be done every 4 hours and rotated across different groups?

Answer:

Yes.

JarveePro supports:

Timed sharing (e.g. every

4 hours

Sharing to a fixed number of groups per session (e.g.

10 groups

Group rotation to avoid repeating the same groups within the same day

This setup is designed to respect Facebook’s behavioral limits and reduce risk.

Q3. Is the process fully automatic or does it require manual confirmation?

It is

fully automatic

Once configured:

No mobile confirmations

No manual clicks

No copy-paste

No login/logout cycles

The automation runs

24/7

, even when:

You are offline

You are sleeping

Q4. Can JarveePro handle multiple Facebook accounts from one dashboard?

Answer:

Yes.

JarveePro supports:

Unlimited Facebook accounts

(license-dependent)

Centralized control from a single dashboard

Independent schedules and group lists per account

This is ideal for brokers, agencies, and teams managing multiple profiles.

Q5. Do accounts need to be admins of the Facebook groups?

No.

JarveePro works with:

Accounts that are

regular group members only

No admin or moderator privileges required

Q6. Are proxies or VPS required?

Answer:

Yes, for safety and stability.

Best practice includes:

Assigning

one proxy per Facebook account

Running JarveePro on a

Windows VPS

for 24/7 uptime

This setup significantly improves reliability and reduces account risk.

Q7. How many groups should be shared per session to stay safe long-term?

While limits vary by account history, a

safe long-term structure

is:

8–12 groups per session

Every 3–4 hours

Daily rotation of groups

No repeated sharing to the same group within the same day

JarveePro allows full control over these parameters.

Q8. Does JarveePro include WhatsApp automation like auto-replies and menus?

Answer:

No.

JarveePro does

not

include advanced WhatsApp automation such as:

Interactive menus

Auto-replies based on user selection

Media galleries with pricing logic

That type of system requires

custom WhatsApp development

, which is a

separate product and service

Q9. Why is there a separate cost for setup or training?

Answer:

JarveePro is a

self-service software

, and many users activate it themselves using the knowledge base.

Optional paid services cover:

One-on-one setup

VPS + proxy configuration

Business-specific automation logic

Live guidance during activation

These services are

optional

, not mandatory.
