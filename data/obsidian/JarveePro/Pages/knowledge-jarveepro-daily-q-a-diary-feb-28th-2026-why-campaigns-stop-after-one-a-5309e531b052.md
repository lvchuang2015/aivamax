2026-02-28

Introduction

JarveePro uses a precision-controlled automation engine designed to execute exactly what the user configures—nothing more and nothing less. When campaigns stop after a single action, it is not a system failure but a configuration safeguard.

This is one of the most frequently asked questions from users automating TikTok and Instagram campaigns. Most users experiencing this issue have simply set limits too low or misunderstood shared campaign settings.

Understanding how campaign limits, account limits, and source availability work together is critical for continuous automation.

Q1: Why does my JarveePro campaign stop after watching or liking only one video?

Answer:

This behavior happens when the campaign has reached its configured execution limits. JarveePro follows your exact instructions. If limits are set too low, the campaign will stop after completing the allowed number of actions.

The most common causes are:

Daily limits set too low

– For example, if "Watch Video" is set to 1 per day, JarveePro will stop after watching one video.

Campaign source list too small

– If only one profile or URL is provided, JarveePro has no additional targets.

Account limit reached

– Each account has its own safety limits.

No loop or repetition configured

– The campaign completes once and does not repeat automatically.

To fix this, increase your limits and ensure sufficient sources are added.

Example proper configuration:

Watch video limit: 100–300 per day

Source profiles: 20–50 minimum

Loop enabled

Campaign schedule active

JarveePro is working correctly—it simply follows your defined limits.

Q2: How do I make JarveePro continue watching videos automatically instead of stopping?

Answer:

You need to configure campaign limits, sources, and repetition properly.

Follow these steps:

Step 1: Increase Account Limits

Open Campaign Settings

Set Watch Video limit to desired number (example: 10-20/account)

Step 2: Add Multiple Sources

Add

Multiple

profile URLs as target lists

The more sources you add, the longer the campaign runs

Step 3: Verify Account Limits

Go to Settings

Ensure account daily limits support your campaign targets

Step 4: Enable Continuous Execution

Make sure campaign is active

Ensure no schedule restrictions prevent execution

Step 5: Review Shared Campaign Settings

Many users skip this step, which causes campaigns to stop early.

Summary

JarveePro does not stop randomly. It stops because it completed the task based on your configured limits.

To ensure continuous execution:

Increase campaign daily limits

Add more sources

Verify account limits

Enable proper campaign scheduling

Review Shared Campaign Settings

Most importantly, read the official knowledge base. The system works exactly as designed when configured properly.

Once configured correctly, JarveePro can run continuously and scale automation efficiently without interruption.
