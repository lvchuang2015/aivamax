Q3: How does WeChat or crypto payment confirmation work?

After completing your payment:

You need to provide your

email address

This email becomes your

purchase identifier

Once verified:

Payment is confirmed

License (serial key) is issued

Your account is linked to that email

Simple rule:

No email = no activation.

Q4: How do I receive my JarveePro license?

Answer:

After payment confirmation:

serial key (license)

is sent to your email

This email serves as your official purchase record

Make sure your email is:

Valid

Accessible

Entered correctly

Most delays happen because of incorrect or missing email info.

Q5: How long does activation take?

In most cases:

immediate after confirmation

Once you receive your serial key:

Apply it

Your account upgrades instantly

No waiting queues, no delayed unlocks.

Q6: When does the license become active?

Answer:

Immediately upon applying the serial key.

There is:

No cooldown

No delayed activation

You can start using JarveePro right away.

Sometimes—but not always.

Extra discounts are not guaranteed
