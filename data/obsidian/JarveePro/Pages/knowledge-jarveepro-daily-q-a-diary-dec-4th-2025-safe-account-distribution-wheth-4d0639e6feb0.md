2025-12-04

Introduction

Today’s Q&A session brought a flood of VPS questions — seriously, it felt like someone opened the fire hydrant. The user wanted clarity on FB accounts, VPS limits, proxies, Business version pricing, and how JarveePro automates joining groups and posting.

So here’s the full breakdown, sorted, cleaned, and organized for the knowledge base.

Q1. What’s the difference between JarveePro Free and Business versions?

Answer:

The Business version includes advanced automation features, higher limits, better performance, and enterprise-grade capabilities. The customer can buy it at

$139/month or $379/year

Q2. Can I re-authenticate purchased FB accounts with 2FA inside JarveePro?

Yes.

You can authenticate and re-authenticate accounts with 2FA without issues.

Q3. Can JarveePro automatically join groups I choose? For example, can 30 FB accounts join 500 groups?

JarveePro can automatically join groups you select. The number of groups depends on rate limits and your warm-up strategy.

Q4. Can I post content to unlimited Facebook groups per account?

Nope — there is a rate limit.

JarveePro respects safe posting rules to avoid bans.

Q5. Do I need 1 VPS per Facebook account?

No.

VPS is like a PC — you can run 10, 20, 100 accounts depending on the specs.

Q6. How much are JarveePro VPS options?

Answer:

JarveePro’s 10 VPS Version is

$3979

(software included, but

not

the VPS itself).

We also provide configuration guidance via video tutorials.

Q7. Is it possible to buy only US-based FB accounts and US-based VPS?

Yes.

Both options are available.

Q8. How many FB accounts can safely run on 1 VPS?

Technically? Hundreds.

Practically and safely?

It depends entirely on proxies.

If all FB accounts log in from the

same VPS IP

, Facebook will connect them — and they can get banned.

→ That’s why:

1 proxy = 1 Facebook account

(recommended best practice)

OR

1 VPS = multiple accounts only if each account has its own dedicated proxy.

Q9. Can I run 30 FB accounts on 1 VPS if each has its own proxy?

Answer:

Yes.

In fact, we run 80 accounts on one PC

without

proxies internally — but that’s our own internal environment.

For customers:

Use 1 dedicated proxy per account

for maximum safety.

Q10. How many groups can 1 Facebook account post to in 1 day?

There is

no unlimited setting

Group posting depends on warm-up, trust score, and daily rate limits.

Take a look at

this article

Summary

Today’s Q&A revolved around VPS safety, proxy setup, and Facebook automation best practices. The core rule still holds:

You can run unlimited accounts on powerful hardware, but each Facebook account should have its own dedicated identity (proxy) to avoid bans.

JarveePro supports group-joining automation, posting, 2FA re-authentication, and works seamlessly with US-based accounts and VPS setups.

A highly practical day of clarifications for scaling Facebook operations safely and efficiently.
