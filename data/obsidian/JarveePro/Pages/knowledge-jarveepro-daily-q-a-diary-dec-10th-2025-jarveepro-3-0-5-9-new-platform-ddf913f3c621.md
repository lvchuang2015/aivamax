Today’s diary brings a wave of upgrade-related questions and scale-builder issues from users exploring the newly released

JarveePro 3.0.5.9

. With new platforms added, smarter thread handling, and deeper anti-bot realism, users are asking:

How does this change my workflow?

This entry breaks everything down with simple, practical explanations.

Q1: “What exactly changed in version 3.0.5.9?”

A:

This update introduced the following improvements:

Optimized thread management

→ Faster, more stable automation under heavy loads.

Optimized Twitter Reply

→ Better deliverability + lower fail rate.

Browser version upgraded to 142

→ Higher compatibility with TikTok, Instagram, Twitter.

New platform: Quora

→ Automate post scheduling, answering, questionning.

Instagram Like Posts now uses REAL mouse paths

→ Drastically improves anti-bot trust signals.

This is the most “anti-bot aligned” update so far.

Q2: “Why add real-mouse movement for Instagram Likes?”

A:

Because IG is getting extremely strict.

Static or robotic click patterns trigger shadow actions.

Realistic mouse paths improve:

Trust score

Action rate

Avoiding blocks

KPI consistency for mass-like campaigns

Users who tested it reported

27–42% fewer temporary action blocks

Q3: “How do I use the new Quora module?”

Once the update installs:

Add your Quora account

Choose tasks (Answer, Post, Upvote, Follow)

Schedule with your preferred timing

Add content or connect AI templates from the Content Explorer

Quora is extremely underrated for SEO — especially long-tail traffic. Agencies are already using this for client acquisition funnels.

More websites load correctly

Fewer login security checks

More human-like browser fingerprints

Higher success rate for TikTok + Instagram sessions

Yes — both platforms are resource-heavy.
