Q1: How can I import multiple Facebook accounts into JarveePro?

Answer:

You can import multiple Facebook accounts into

using the bulk account import feature. When preparing your account list, it’s important to keep the correct formatting.

Each account entry should use the

pipe symbol

as the separator

, not commas. For example:

username|password|email|cookies|token

Do not replace

with commas or other characters, or the system may fail to recognize the account fields during import.

To see the complete step-by-step process, watch the official tutorial:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1TnpYBUSDXo

Q2: I exported accounts but only see Facebook and Instagram. Why can’t I see Twitter/X?

Answer:

If your export file only shows Facebook and Instagram accounts, it usually means that only those platforms were selected during the export process.

In

JarveePro

, each platform is managed separately. To export accounts from other networks like Twitter/X, you must first select those accounts in the account manager before exporting.

Check the export settings again and ensure the correct platform accounts are selected.

Q4: I have many Facebook accounts. Can JarveePro manage them safely?

Answer:

Yes,

JarveePro

is designed to manage multiple social media accounts from one dashboard. However, account safety depends on proper setup.

To reduce the risk of accounts getting restricted, follow these best practices:

Use

dedicated proxies for each account

Set

realistic activity limits

Avoid aggressive automation

Allow new accounts to

warm up gradually

Following these guidelines helps maintain account stability when managing accounts at scale.
