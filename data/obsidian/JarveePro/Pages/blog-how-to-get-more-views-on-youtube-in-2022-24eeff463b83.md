How to get more views on YouTube in 2022

Share

2022-02-09

YouTube provides an endless source of useful information. You can obtain the latest and greatest music videos, trends and channels from YouTube. As a YouTuber, it's natural that you want more views for your channel. Before we know how to gain more views, we need to know what counts a view and what doesn't on YouTube first.

Each time a viewer intentionally initiates the playing of a video on their device and watches for at least 30 seconds, that counts as a view. While when a single user or bot refreshes a video over and over again, or if a website auto-plays a video, these views are

not

counted towards your total view numbers.

Then, what if you want to gain more real and countable views on YouTube, what should you do? Here we have 5 tips for you.

1.Increase your views with thumbnails

Video thumbnails let viewers see a quick snapshot of your video as they're browsing YouTube. After your video is finished uploading, you can choose a thumbnail from the three options YouTube automatically provided or upload your own if your account is verified. Be careful to choose it, cause thumbnail is a major part whether audience decide to watch your video while skimming through tons of videos.

An effective thumbnail is clear and accurate about what the video is describing. The thumbnail works in tandem with the video’s title. You need to find some ways to make your thumbnail stands out. Remember, thumbnails are one of the best ways to catch the attention of people browsing YouTube, so make sure that yours are effective and compelling.

2.Improve your video’s search ranking

YouTube is a social platform as well as a search engine. And one of the top strategies for getting more views is YouTube SEO (search engine optimization

. In other words, when your ideal viewer types in your chosen keywords, you want your video ranking near the top of YouTube’s results list. That means you need to know what your audience is looking for—tutorials, inspiration, or entertainment.

In order to improve your videos’ search ranking on YouTube, you need to do some research first. Find inspiration for your next video based on what people are already looking for. For example, you can take a look at search patterns and see what keywords have a lot of search queries. Take those relevant keywords and use them in your metadata (i.e., your video title, tags, video description text, subtitles)

3.Make brand new videos beyond how-to videos

You'll find out that there are a lot of how-to videos when you’re researching your target keywords. This is because there’s a lot of search volume for how-to content. But if you want to attract new eyes, you should make how-to videos as well as some unique videos to let people always focus on your channel. It is hard to find out and it may take more time to create.

In all, you should always keep in mind that on YouTube, your brand’s value-added features come in the form of content that is meaningful to people who are already your fans. Always present new content to the audience to maintain audience attention.

4. Maintain engagement with your viewers

Maintaining active engagement is a surefire way to get more views on YouTube. Like other social networks, you need to use this function of YouTube as well. In other words, it isn’t enough to just regularly share videos, you also need to actively engage with your audience. "Audience engagement" is necessary for you to gain more views.

But how? The best way is to simply respond to comments — especially if someone asks a direct question. Because other viewers will be more likely to comment themselves if they know they’ll receive a response. It only takes a second, and you can do it while you’re browsing your comments for new video ideas.

5. Promote your videos in various ways

YouTube isn’t a standalone social channel, so make sure to cross-promote your videos through your free marketing channels. If you have followers on other social channels, let them know each time you upload a new video to YouTube.
