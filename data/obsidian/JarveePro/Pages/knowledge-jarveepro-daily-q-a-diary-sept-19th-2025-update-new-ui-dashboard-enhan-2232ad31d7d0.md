JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary – Sept 19th, 2025(Update – New UI Dashboard & Enhanced Campaign Insights)

2025-09-19

JarveePro just launched a

brand-new UI Dashboard

designed to give marketers a

clear, comprehensive view of their accounts and campaigns

. From tracking followers and engagement to monitoring campaign performance across multiple platforms, this update makes managing social media automation more intuitive, transparent, and actionable. We just launched a

brand-new UI

designed to give users a clearer and more powerful overview of their operations:

Accounts Dashboard

: Instantly see how many accounts you have connected on each social media platform.

Campaign Manager

: Manage and monitor all your active campaigns in one place with full transparency.

Audience Reach

: Track the total number of

followers, friends, and subscribers

across all platforms.

Growth Tracker

: See how many

new followers, friends, or subscribers

you gained in any chosen time frame.

Top Performing Platforms

: Platforms are ranked so you can quickly identify which one performs best.

Activity Summary

: A clear overview of

account actions and growth trends

Engagement Insights

: Detailed breakdown of

likes, comments, follows, and interactions

, with time-based filters (daily, weekly, monthly).

This update makes JarveePro more

transparent, user-friendly, and powerful

, giving marketers a

360° view

of both campaigns and account-level performance.

Client Q&A

Client 1: DJ – Follow/Unfollow & Account Setup

Q1: I need a short-term solution for follow and unfollow. What’s the cost for 200 accounts, 1 month?

Pricing is

$397/month

for the

full version

, which can manage

unlimited accounts

. Covers setups with

200 accounts

Q2: Does JarveePro work with AdsPower on VPS?

We provide our own

built-in anti-detect browser

, which is faster and more stable.

Q3: How long would it take to import all 200 accounts?

Import is

fast and bulk-ready

using CSV or direct input.

Client 2: Japanese User – Facebook Likes Limit

Q1: I want to like posts from 150 friends, but it stops at 75. Why?

This is due to

hourly action limits

built in for account safety.

Q2: Can I change this limit?

It’s

default for safety

, but you can

adjust hourly/daily actions

in the campaign settings.

Q3: When can actions resume after 75 likes?

Use the

scheduler

to spread actions across time: e.g., 75 likes per hour. This keeps your account safe while reaching all 150 friends.

Client 3: Facebook Live Viewer Simulation

Q1: Can Facebook accounts simulate viewers on a live stream (e.g., 1,926 people)?

Yes,

auto join live

is possible, but requires

custom development

Q2: Cost for this feature?

$2000

for development.
