2026-04-16

Introduction

As more users move from simply using automation tools to building full-scale systems, questions naturally shift from “how to use” to “how to scale and monetize.”

In this JarveePro Daily Q&A, we cover key topics including:

reseller and affiliate opportunities

commission structure

VPS requirements

proxy usage

and system capabilities

These are real questions from users already operating at an advanced level — looking to expand beyond basic usage into agency workflows and scalable setups.

Q1: Can I resell JarveePro and earn commission?

Answer:

Yes, JarveePro offers both

reselling and affiliate opportunities

There are two main ways to earn:

Affiliate Model:

You receive a unique referral link. Any purchase made through your link earns you a commission.

Direct Reselling:

You can sell directly to clients and work alongside the official system.

Commission Structure:

25% commission

on standard software sales

Up to 10% commission

on custom solutions (subject to confirmation)

This allows users — especially agencies — to turn their usage into an additional revenue stream.

Q2: Do I need to buy JarveePro before promoting or reselling it?

Answer:

No, you do not need to purchase the software first.

You can start with an

affiliate link

, and earn commissions whenever someone signs up through your referral.

This lowers the barrier to entry for:

content creators

marketers

agencies testing new revenue channels

Q3: Can I earn commission on my own purchases?

No, commissions are only applied to

new customer referrals

, not your own purchases.

The system is designed to reward:

new user acquisition

external promotion

Q4: What about custom development — does that include commission?

Answer:

Custom development may include commission (typically around 10%), but this can vary depending on the project and internal policy.

It’s best to confirm case-by-case, especially for:

large custom builds

agency-level integrations

Q5: Does JarveePro include VPS or hosting?

No, JarveePro does

not include hardware or VPS servers

Instead, it provides:

software capabilities

automation infrastructure

Users are responsible for:

setting up their own VPS

configuring their environment

Q6: What is the minimum VPS requirement?

Answer:

The minimum setup starts at

10 VPS instances

This reflects the reality of scaling:

multi-account management

distributed environments

reduced risk through separation

Pricing for VPS-related setups can vary depending on configuration, but large-scale environments are expected for serious operations.

Q7: Does JarveePro support SMM panel API integration?

Yes.

JarveePro can integrate with

SMM panel APIs

, allowing:

automatic order processing

workflow automation

reduced manual intervention

This is especially useful for:

agencies

resellers

automated service providers

Q8: Can JarveePro mimic real user behavior to avoid detection?

Answer:

Yes, this is a core focus.

The system is designed to:

simulate human-like behavior

avoid repetitive patterns

operate within platform limits

However, results still depend on:

proxy quality

account condition

overall setup

Automation is a system — not just a tool.

Q9: Are proxies required? Can I use external providers?

Yes, proxies are required for stable and scalable operation.

You can use

external proxy providers

, and this is actually recommended for flexibility and performance.

Summary

This Q&A highlights an important shift:

Users are no longer just asking how JarveePro works —

they’re asking how to

build systems around it

Key takeaways:

JarveePro offers real monetization through affiliate and reseller models

It operates as a software layer, not infrastructure (VPS required separately)

Scaling requires proper setup: proxies, VPS, and workflow design

Advanced features like API integration support agency-level automation

Ultimately, JarveePro is not just a tool —

it’s part of a larger system for those looking to scale, automate, and even build businesses around social media operations.
