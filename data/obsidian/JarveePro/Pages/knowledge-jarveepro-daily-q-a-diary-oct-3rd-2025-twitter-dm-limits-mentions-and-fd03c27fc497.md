Q&A Section

1) How many DMs can I send daily with JarveePro on free Twitter accounts?

JarveePro follows Twitter’s technical limit of

500 DMs/day

, but the practical safe range is lower.

With JarveePro, you can configure warm-up settings (5–30/day when starting) and gradually increase to a stable volume (50–300/day) depending on the account’s age, trust, and behavior.

JarveePro’s automation lets you randomize send times and use spintax so your campaigns look natural while scaling safely.

2) Can I use @mentions inside Twitter DMs through JarveePro?

Yes — JarveePro allows you to include

@handles

inside your direct messages. This is often used for promotions like “Follow my main account @JarveeproGuru.” However, Twitter flags repetitive promotional messages as spam.

With JarveePro’s spintax and personalization, you can vary your text to reduce risk and keep your accounts safe while still pointing users to other profiles.

3) How do I connect Twitter accounts to JarveePro with cookies (including Android cookies)?

JarveePro supports

cookie import

for connecting accounts without needing to re-login manually.

You can:

Export cookies from browsers or Android sessions.

Convert them into JSON/Netscape format JarveePro accepts.

Import cookies into JarveePro’s Accounts section.

Verify accounts via the embedded browser to confirm they’re live.

For Android cookies, format conversion may be required, but once imported, JarveePro keeps the session alive and allows you to automate tasks immediately. This is especially useful when migrating large batches of accounts from other tools.
