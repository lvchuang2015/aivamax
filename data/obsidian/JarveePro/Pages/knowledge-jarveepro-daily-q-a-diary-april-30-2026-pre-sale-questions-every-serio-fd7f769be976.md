JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary – April 30, 2026 | Pre-Sale Questions Every Serious Automation Buyer Asks

2026-04-30

Introduction

Today’s Q&A came from an e-commerce entrepreneur building an automated social media engine for a women’s sunglasses brand — exactly the kind of serious buyer asking the right questions before scaling.

What stood out wasn’t just the questions themselves, but what they reveal about today’s automation market: users no longer want “tools,” they want infrastructure.

Here were the four questions asked before purchase.

Q1. Is Pinterest supported in the Full Plan?

Answer:

Yes — Pinterest is included in the JarveePro Full Plan as one of the 8 supported platforms.

This matters more than many realize. For visual-first e-commerce brands like fashion, accessories, beauty, and home products, Pinterest can become both a traffic engine and a long-tail sales channel.

For many advanced users, Pinterest is not an add-on — it’s part of the growth stack.

Q2. What is the exact pricing for the Full Plan?

Current Full Plan pricing is:

$397/month

$879/year (discounted annual option)

Unlimited profiles included

For agencies, sellers, and growth-focused operators running multiple accounts, unlimited profile support often makes the economics very attractive compared to stacking multiple separate tools.

Q3. Does JarveePro support full publishing for Instagram Stories, Reels and TikTok?

Yes — JarveePro supports full publishing and scheduling workflows across major social platforms.

That includes support for:

Instagram posts

Reels

Stories

TikTok publishing workflows

And importantly, this goes beyond draft preparation — users are looking for execution, not just content staging.

That shift says a lot about where the market is going.

Q4. Can JarveePro run on a Linux VPS or require Windows?

Answer:

JarveePro is Windows-based software, so self-hosting requires a Windows environment (local machine or Windows VPS).

For many serious users, infrastructure convenience matters almost as much as automation features.

Summary

What makes this Q&A interesting is how representative it is of today’s smarter automation buyers.

They ask about:

Channel coverage

Pricing efficiency

Publishing depth

Infrastructure flexibility

Not “Can it post for me?”

But “Can this become my operating system?”

That’s a different kind of buyer.

And increasingly, that’s the JarveePro audience.
