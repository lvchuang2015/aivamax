JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary — Dec. 15th, 2025 | JarveePro Instagram Repost Feature – Custom Development, Safety & Pricing Explained

2025-12-15

Introduction

Today’s JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary covers a practical, real-world question from the community:

custom development for an Instagram repost feature

This discussion dives into how JarveePro-style reposting works, what safety considerations matter (especially metadata handling), and how custom development is typically scoped and priced. It’s a great snapshot of how community feedback directly turns into product improvements.

Q1: What is the Instagram repost feature, and why is it needed?

Answer:

The Instagram repost feature allows users to automatically repost images from a specified source account to their own Instagram accounts, with built-in delays and posting limits.

This functionality was common in traditional Jarvee and is still highly requested because it enables:

Content scaling without manual reposting

Controlled repost frequency (e.g., 1 post per day)

Campaign-style reposting from curated source accounts

Q2: What core functions are included in this repost feature?

The requested repost feature includes three key components:

A) Generate a new caption

Each repost can use a freshly generated caption to reduce duplication and improve engagement.

B) Modify or remove original post metadata (for safety)

Metadata from the original image is modified or stripped to reduce platform detection risks.

C) Smart repost limits

Reposting can be configured to:

Post

1 image per day

(or another defined frequency)

Stop automatically after a set number of posts

End when no eligible source posts remain

Pause or stop when the campaign is manually stopped

Q3: How is original post metadata modified or removed for safety?

Answer:

In most cases, metadata handling is done by:

Removing EXIF data from images before upload

Re-encoding images to generate a fresh file signature

Avoiding reuse of original post identifiers

Q4: What is the estimated cost for custom development of this feature?

For this specific Instagram repost feature, the agreed custom development cost is:

💲 $600 USD

This covers:

Repost logic and scheduling

Caption regeneration

Metadata safety handling

Campaign controls and stop conditions

Q5: Will this feature be shared with the community?

Answer:

Yes. After confirmation, the feature is approved to be shared with the wider community, making it easier for users to profile accounts and automate repost workflows efficiently.

Q6: Is JarveePro developing related features?

Yes. In parallel, the team is actively developing a

new report feature focused on impression monitoring

, further enhancing analytics and performance tracking for automated campaigns.

Summary

This Q&A highlights how JarveePro continues to evolve through direct user conversations—turning real operational needs into actionable features. Custom development isn’t just about adding tools; it’s about making automation smarter, safer, and easier to scale.
