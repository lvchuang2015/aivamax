## Content

JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary – Oct. 29th, 2025（scalability for VPS version and Retry Failed Accounts)

2025-10-29

Today’s JarveePro Daily Q&A focuses on one of the most common questions among power users — scalability and efficiency. Many users are moving to VPS versions to manage large numbers of accounts efficiently. But when a user runs hundreds or even thousands of accounts, questions about retries, VPS performance, and lifetime licensing naturally arise.

Let’s break it down through a real user scenario.

Q1: If I want to upgrade to the VPS version, how many accounts can I run in each VPS?

The number of accounts you can run on each VPS depends mainly on your VPS resources (RAM, CPU cores, bandwidth) and the type of automation tasks you’re performing.

As a general guideline:

Light tasks (posting, scraping, liking):

100–150 accounts per 8GB RAM VPS

Moderate tasks (messaging, joining groups, commenting):

50–80 accounts per 8GB RAM VPS

Heavy tasks (multi-platform automation + AI comments):

30–50 accounts per 8GB RAM VPS

For users managing

tens of thousands of accounts (e.g., 47k)

, it’s recommended to distribute them across multiple VPS setups with consistent proxy management. JarveePro supports simultaneous multi-VPS deployments for stability and safety.

Q2: If I run a task with 200 accounts, 100 succeed and 100 fail — can I rerun only the failed ones?

Yes, you can. JarveePro allows you to

retry or continue only unfinished/failed accounts

without repeating successful ones.

When you re-run a campaign using the “Retry / Continue Unfinished” option, the system automatically skips accounts that have already completed their tasks successfully. This saves time, prevents duplication, and keeps your campaign data clean.

Check details here:

https://blog.jarveepro.com/knowledge/JarveePro-Campaign-Settings/Retry-Failed-Accounts-in-JarveePro/5463

Pro Tip:

Before retrying, identify why the previous 100 failed — it could be due to proxy, login, or temporary block issues. Fixing these ensures your next retry has a much higher success rate.

Q3: How much is the one-time payment for a Facebook automation tool with unlimited accounts?

JarveePro offers flexible licensing options, including subscription-based plans and full lifetime versions.

Recent examples (for reference):

Lifetime License (Unlimited Accounts):

around

$1,697–$2,199

depending on the package and included platforms.

Full Version One-Time Payment:

2,199

(check the official JarveePro pricing page for the latest rates).

If you manage thousands of accounts or run agency-level operations, the

lifetime or unlimited license

is the most cost-efficient choice in the long term — you pay once and manage as many accounts as you need.

Summary:

Managing massive account networks efficiently requires both system stability and strategic configuration.

JarveePro’s VPS support lets you scale your operations safely, while the retry system ensures no wasted effort on already successful tasks.

And for those managing enterprise-level automation — the lifetime “unlimited account” plan provides the perfect long-term solution for ultimate scalability and cost control.
