How to search post by user?

Step 1

Choose Instagram Tasks-Search posts by user(double click)--click ok

Step2

Select account-Input url-Click ok(You can also add some custom feature in this page)

Step 3

Select your task-check the task--click run

Success

(No debug window here)

How to Search Posts by Tag?

Choose Instagram--Choose Create--Choose Search Posts by Tag--Input random task name--Click OK

Step 2

Select account--Input tags content--Specific settings--Click OK

Select your task--Check the task--Click start

How to Search Posts by Map?

Step 1

Choose Instagram--Choose Create--Choose Search Posts by Map--Input random task name--Click OK
