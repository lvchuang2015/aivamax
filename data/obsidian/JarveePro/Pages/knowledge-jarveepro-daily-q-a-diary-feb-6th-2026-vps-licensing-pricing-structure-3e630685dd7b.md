JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary – Feb 6th, 2026 | VPS Licensing, Pricing Structure & Task Setup Explained

2026-02-06

Introduction

Today’s JarveePro Daily Q&A comes from a long-time marketer with valid concerns about VPS pricing, licensing flexibility, trust, and operational clarity. These are common questions among experienced users evaluating long-term automation tools. This article documents the discussion clearly and transparently, so others can understand how JarveePro’s VPS model works and why certain decisions exist.

Q1: Is there a monthly VPS plan for JarveePro?

Answer:

No. JarveePro does

not

offer a monthly VPS (multi-device) plan.

What

is

available:

Monthly

Full Version

license ($397)

Runs on

one PC or one VPS

Suitable for testing JarveePro features, workflows, and stability

What is

Monthly 5 VPS or 10 VPS plans

The

VPS version (10 VPS)

lifetime only

Q2: Can the monthly fee be deducted when upgrading to the VPS lifetime plan?

Answer:

Yes. Monthly Full Version payments

can

be deducted from the

10 VPS lifetime plan

Q3: Why does using 5 VPS cost the same as using 10 VPS?

JarveePro VPS licensing is

cluster-based

, not per VPS.

Whether you use:

3 VPS

5 VPS

or 10 VPS

The backend logic, coordination effort, and support workload remain the same. Because of this, pricing does not scale by VPS count.

Q4: Why does one monthly license only work on one computer or VPS?

Answer:

JarveePro licenses are

per-device

, not per-account.

This design:

Prevents account overload

Reduces automation risk

Enforces safer automation density

Protects account longevity

It is a safety-first decision, not a limitation.

Q5: Why doesn’t JarveePro offer a yearly or monthly VPS plan?

Running multi-VPS automation requires ongoing manual effort:

Individual VPS checks

Machine-level debugging

Higher support and developer costs

Non-linear scaling of maintenance effort

Monthly pricing does

not

realistically cover these costs.

For this reason, the VPS version is lifetime-only to ensure service sustainability and support quality.

Q6: What is the Task Setup Service and how does it work?

Answer:

The Task Setup Service is a

one-time paid service

Each task represents

one automation objective

, for example:

500 slave accounts following target accounts → 1 task

Liking posts → 1 task

Commenting or reposting → 1 task

This service helps:

Avoid incorrect configurations

Reduce onboarding mistakes

Speed up deployment for advanced setups

Summary

JarveePro’s VPS model prioritizes stability, support quality, and long-term automation safety over flexible but risky pricing structures. While monthly VPS plans may seem appealing, they do not align with the real operational costs of multi-device automation. JarveePro is designed for users who value disciplined growth, predictable behavior, and sustainable systems.
