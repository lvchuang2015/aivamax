What is JarveePro Account Manager ?

2026-05-21

The Account Manager inside JarveePro is the control center for all your connected social accounts. Think of it like your mission dashboard — if your accounts aren’t properly added and organized here, everything else downstream (campaigns, automation, posting) becomes unstable fast.

This module is not just a “login storage” feature. It’s where identity, proxy behavior, and platform trust all get tied together.

What the Account Manager does

The Account Manager is responsible for:

Storing and organizing all social media accounts

Assigning proxies to individual accounts

Managing account health status

Handling login sessions securely

Allowing account grouping for campaigns

Tracking basic account activity state (active, warning, blocked, etc.)

In simple terms:

If campaigns are the engine, Account Manager is the ignition system.

Supported account types

Depending on your setup, you can manage accounts from platforms like:

Facebook profiles & pages

Instagram accounts

TikTok accounts

YouTube channels

X (Twitter) accounts

Quora Accounts

StocktWits Accounts

Threads Accounts

Other supported social platforms (varies by updates)

Each account behaves differently, so treating them all the same is a fast track to trouble.

How to add an account (basic flow)

Go to

Account Manager

Select the platform (Facebook, Instagram, etc.)

Click

Add to Add a Category

Add

Account

Enter login credentials

Assign a proxy (strongly recommended)

Verify login session if required

Save and categorize the account (Move Accounts to Different Category)

Proxy assignment (this is where most users mess up)

Every account should ideally have its own dedicated proxy.

Best practice:

1 account = 1 proxy

Match proxy region with account history when possible

Avoid switching proxies frequently after setup

Bad setup = repeated logins = trust issues = action blocks.

Account status indicators

Inside Account Manager, you’ll typically see statuses like:

Normal

→ Account is working normally

Failed

→ Suspicious behavior detected or login instability

Restricted

→ Too much behaviour, rate limited. (Canbe likes, follow or dm)

Locked / Disabled

→ Account restricted by platform

Each status is a signal, not just a label. It tells you whether to slow down activity or fix configuration.

Common mistakes users make

Let’s be honest — most problems come from setup, not the tool.

1. Reusing the same proxy across multiple accounts

Platforms detect patterns fast. This is one of the biggest triggers for flags.

2. Adding accounts without warming them

Fresh accounts + instant automation = instant suspicion.

3. Ignoring account warnings

A “warning” status is basically your last polite notice before restrictions.

4. Logging in from multiple locations rapidly

This breaks session consistency and triggers security checks.

Best practices for stable account management

Keep account-to-proxy mapping consistent

Warm up accounts before running heavy automation

Space out logins and actions naturally

Monitor account health daily

Avoid sudden behavioral spikes (posting 0 → 50 actions overnight)

When to revisit Account Manager

You should regularly check Account Manager when:

Campaigns suddenly stop delivering

Accounts show warning or checkpoint status

You’ve added new proxies

You’re scaling up account volume

You notice inconsistent engagement patterns

Conclusion

The Account Manager isn’t just a setup screen — it’s your “trust foundation.” If this layer is messy, everything built on top of it becomes unstable, no matter how good your campaigns are.

Treat it like infrastructure, not storage.
