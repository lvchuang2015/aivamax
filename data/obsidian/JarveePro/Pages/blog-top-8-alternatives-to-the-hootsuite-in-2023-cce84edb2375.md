Top 8 Alternatives to Hootsuite in 2023

#1 – JarveePro

JarveePro

is one of the most powerful social media management software programs that helps individuals and businesses automate their social media activities and grow their online presence. It provides a comprehensive set of features designed to streamline and enhance your social media marketing efforts.

What can be done with JarveePro,?

You can

manage multiple social media accounts

on popular platforms, such as Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, and more, all from a single dashboard.

The software allows you to

schedule and automate posts

engage with your audience

follow or unfollow users

like or comment on posts

, and perform other actions to increase your social media visibility.

One of the key strengths of JarveePro is its automation tool. It enables you to set up automated tasks and campaigns, saving you time and effort. For example, you can schedule posts to be published at certain times, automatically follow users based on certain criteria, or engage with relevant hashtags and trending topics.

JarveePro also provides

analytics and reporting features

, allowing you to track the performance of your social media campaigns, monitor follower growth, engagement metrics, and more. This data helps you gain insights into what's working and make informed decisions to optimize your social media strategy.

Overall, JarveePro offers a comprehensive solution for managing and growing your social media presence. It simplifies the process of managing multiple accounts, automates repetitive and mundane tasks, and provides valuable insights to help you achieve your social media marketing goals.

#2 – Hootsuite

Hootsuite

is another popular social media management platform with some industry-leading features. It’s a great choice for brands and marketing agencies.

Hootsuite has been around for a long time. It was one of the first SMM tools to hit the market, and it’s still one of the best.

It covers the full range of social media marketing with tools to help with planning, scheduling, publishing, message/comment management, advertising, analytics, brand mention monitoring, and collaboration.

Like Jarvee, it also has powerful automation capabilities, with features like automated alerts, sophisticated approval workflows, and the ability to send out targeted messages automatically at the best time.

Another cool thing about Hootsuite is that it has a pretty extensive app library, where you can find and install third-party add-ons that extend its functionality. This makes it a very flexible solution.

#3 – Agorapulse

Agorapulse

is a great Jarvee alternative with tons of useful features for making social media management quick and efficient. It is a true ‘all-in-one’ social media management tool.

Like with Jarvee, you can reschedule older posts to make the most of your evergreen content. You can also use the publishing tool to schedule new posts to all your major social platforms, including Instagram!

What I particularly like about the platform is how good the user interface is. And the revamped social scheduler & calendar is one of the best we’ve seen.

Agorapulse also calculates the best time to publish your posts based on past post-performance.

In addition to publishing, Agorapulse also has a social media inbox, which allows you and your team to manage all your social media interactions in one place so that you never miss a message.

Another great thing about Agorapulse is the social listening tool that can help you keep your finger on the pulse when it comes to online conversations relating to your brand or your competitors.

Paid plans are a bit more expensive than other tools on this list which is why we’ve ranked it a bit lower than some other tools. But if you can justify the cost, you will not be disappointed. We’ve used this tool for several years and our experience has been excellent.

All in all, it’s a great all-in-one solution for brands and small businesses that want to improve their social media management.

#5– SocialBee

The tool features an intuitive content creation dashboard that can be used to create new content for different platforms, refresh evergreen content, and curate posts from articles on the web.

In addition to this, SocialBee has a powerful scheduling tool that allows you to save posts to categories, which can be paused, re-arranged, and used to create automated evergreen posting sequences. SocialBee also includes a helpful analytical feature that you can use to gain insight into your audience and post performance.

Also, if you are struggling to manage your social media accounts alone, then SocialBee also offers a concierge service that can help you with social posting, article creation, and more.

SocialBee is perfect for businesses, as it supports most major social media platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Google My Business, and TikTok. It also integrates with tons of useful social media tools like Canva.

#6 – Sendible

Sendible

is a social media management tool that can help you effectively manage large-scale social media operations. And despite its functionality, it’s surprisingly affordable.

It’s a perfect solution for agencies and has tons of features that can help you collaborate with your team, and make your social media management more efficient.

The visual scheduling tool allows you to plan posts in advance, and save your posts into campaigns, which you can use to queue posts to make the most of evergreen content.

In addition to publishing, Sendible has a social media listening tool that will allow you to automatically track conversations about your brand or your competitors. You can also respond to any social media comments directly from the Sendible dashboard.

Sendible is a tool that is aimed at agencies and teams, and it have some really useful collaboration features that can help you to automate your workflows.

You can assign certain conversations to selected team members, and set up an approval workflow for posts to be checked before they go live. Not only this, but Sendible also offers insightful social media analytics and has a useful reporting feature.

#7 – Pallyy

Pallyy

is a relatively new and extremely affordable social media scheduler. While it’s not as feature-rich as some of the more expensive alternatives like Jarvee, it’s got all the tools you need to manage social media on a small scale.

With Pallyy, you pay $15 per social set per month. One social set includes Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Google My Business, Pinterest, and TikTok.

It comes complete with an easy-to-use, visual content calendar that is perfect for planning content for visual platforms like Instagram and TikTok. It also has a useful hashtag tool that you can use to save hashtag clusters that you regularly use to make scheduling your posts a bit quicker.

Pallyy also offers in-depth analytics that can help you learn more about your post performance, audience demographics, and even the best time to post. There is also a social inbox that allows you to manage all your social media interactions in one place, and a bio link tool.

For such an affordable tool, it has all the necessary tools to help you streamline your social media management.

#8 – Publer

Publer

is a feature rich Jarvee that is perfect for businesses. Unlike many tools, Publer really does support all major social media platforms.

You can use it to schedule for lesser known platforms like Telegram and you can even schedule shorts for YouTube.

Like Jarvee, Publer has a post-recycling feature, which allows you to automatically schedule and repost your best-performing social media content at the optimal time. Publer also has features for bulk scheduling, post-curation, and even a link-in-bio tool.

When it comes to publishing. Publer offers an easy-to-use visual scheduler that integrates with popular social media creation tools like Canva.

Not only that, but it comes complete with a unique Twitter scheduling tool, that allows you to pre-schedule Twitter threads of up to 15 tweets long. Overall, it’s a useful all-in-one SMM tool at an extremely affordable price point.
