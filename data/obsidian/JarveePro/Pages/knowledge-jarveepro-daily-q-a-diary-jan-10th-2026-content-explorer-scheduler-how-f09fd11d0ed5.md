Introduction

In this JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary for January 10th, 2026, we address a common issue faced by advanced users: duplicate Facebook comments caused by scheduler settings. We also clarify the role of AI-generated comments, explain how task records work, and outline when upgrading to AI Monitor is the most cost-efficient choice for keyword-based Facebook group automation.

Q1: Is my setup correct when using Content Explorer with Facebook comment campaigns?

Answer:

Yes, the overall workflow is correct:

Content Explorer searches and generates post URLs

Facebook comment campaign pulls Post URLs and Comment Text from collections

Scheduler runs the campaign automatically every hour

This is the

intended automation flow

for scalable Facebook engagement.

Q2: Why does JarveePro comment multiple times on the same Facebook post when using a 1-hour scheduler?

This happens because the scheduler option

“Clean up completed task records when starting a task”

is enabled. When the scheduler restarts every hour, JarveePro clears its history and no longer remembers which post URLs were already processed.

As a result, previously commented posts are treated as new targets.

Q3: How can I ensure one comment per Facebook post URL only?

Answer:

To enforce one comment per post URL:

Open the scheduler settings

Uncheck “Clean up completed task records when starting a task”

Keep the scheduler running in loop mode

This allows JarveePro to retain task history and prevents duplicate comments on the same post.

Q4: Does AI-generated content cause duplicate comments?

No. AI-generated comments can be different or identical, but they do

not control deduplication

. Deduplication is handled entirely by task records and scheduler logic.

AI only affects

comment content

, not

posting behavior

Q5: When should I use AI Monitor instead of Content Explorer?

Answer:

AI Monitor is recommended when you need:

Real-time monitoring of

new Facebook group posts

Keyword-based detection without manual refreshing

Faster reaction to newly published content

For service businesses targeting local Facebook groups, AI Monitor is the most efficient and scalable solution.

Q6: Is AI Monitor a lifetime upgrade and is it worth it for single businesses (not agencies)?

Yes. AI Monitor is a

lifetime license

For single businesses that rely on Facebook group monitoring and lead engagement, it often replaces multiple tools and manual workflows, making it cost-efficient long-term.

Summary

Duplicate Facebook comments in JarveePro are not caused by AI-generated content but by scheduler settings that reset completed task records. Disabling the cleanup option ensures proper deduplication. For users who need real-time Facebook group monitoring based on keywords, AI Monitor is the recommended long-term solution.
