## Content

Instagram Search Followings and Unfollow

2025-10-19

Objective:

Extract a full list of Instagram followings and manage unfollow campaigns efficiently.

Step 1: Scrape Following List

Open

JarveePro → Content Explorer

Navigate to

Search → Instagram → Search Followings

Scrape the following list.

Export the data in

Username: Profile URL

format for review.

Step 2: Execute Targeted Unfollow

JarveePro → Instagram Campaigns → Targeted Unfollow Campaign

Import or reference the scraped following list.

Configure your unfollow parameters as desired.

Start the campaign.

Expected Result:

You will have a complete list of all accounts you follow before initiating unfollows.

Unfollow actions will be executed based on your filtered or reviewed data.

Your account engagement remains clean and under control.

Tip:

Always review your scraped data before running unfollow campaigns to avoid accidental unfollows of important accounts.
