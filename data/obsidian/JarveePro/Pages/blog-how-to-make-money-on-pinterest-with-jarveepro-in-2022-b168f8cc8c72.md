Do you want to make money on Pinterest? You’ve come to the right place. Pinterest isn’t just about discovering content, creating boards, or finding inspiration — it’s also a great place for brands to drive traffic and grow business.

Before we start, let's learn some fun facts about Pinterest:

In fact, Pinterest is a powerful platform for driving sales, which is not known by many people. According to a survey by Cowen and Company, 47% of social media users saw Pinterest as the primary platform for discovering products and shopping — more than 3 times the percentage of respondents who cited Facebook or Instagram.

After seeing this result, are you ready to start making money on Pinterest? Here I'd like to share you 5 useful ways:

Use Pinterest’s Main Shopping Features

Pins are the most straightforward way to sell on Pinterest because they take Pinners directly to your website to make a purchase. And it is easier than ever to discover your Product Pins thanks to the introduction of the Pinterest Shop tab— users now have a dedicated space to browse and shop in-stock products.

Besides, Pinterest has made updating Product Pins super easy for business owners thanks to Catalogs. Once set up, your Pinterest Product Pins will automatically update every time you upload or modify products on your e-commerce site.

Get Discovered with Rich Pins

Rich Pins are a type of organic Pin that automatically sync with information from your website. There are a few different kinds of Rich Pins, including Product, Recipe, Article, and App Pins.

Setting up Rich Pins is a great way to drive sales on Pinterest. They provide a direct route to your website and are valuable SEO boosters for a search-first platform. To make a Rich Pin, you need to add relevant, top-trending keywords to your Pin title, as well as the first 50 words of your description.

Offer Pinterest-exclusive Promotions to Would-be Buyers

Another great way to make money on Pinterest is to offer a unique promo code to your followers and users who stumble across your Pin in their Home Feeds.

Special promotions are highly effective and perform great year-round. Plus, offering a limited-time code exclusive to Pinterest creates a sense of urgency, which can give your account a boost in clicks and engagement.

Promote Your Products with an Ad Campaign

On Pinterest, Pinterest Ads are one of the most effective ways for businesses to drive traffic, boost awareness, and make money. You can use demographics like age and location, as well as categories and interests, to better-target Pinterest Ads and reach the right shopping audience for your business or product.

Once an ad is set up in Pinterest Ads Manager, it will appear in users’ Home feeds in their relevant search results — and it will look almost identical to a regular Pin.

Catch Your Audience’s Attention with Video Pins

Video Pins are also a great way to engage an audience, tell a story, show your products or services in action, and ultimately make sales on Pinterest. They automatically start playing on desktop, which makes them a great way to grab viewers’ attention.

If you’re already posting video content on platforms like Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube, consider using video on Pinterest too.

All of the above concludes 5 tips for making sales on Pinterest. In fact, Pinterest is a platform that has a wide range of tools and features. Therefore, there are more great ways to make sales. Stay tuned to our blog and you'll find out more.
