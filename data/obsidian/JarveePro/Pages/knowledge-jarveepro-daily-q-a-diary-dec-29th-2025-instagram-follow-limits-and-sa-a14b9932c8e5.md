Introduction

Today’s Q&A session focused on Instagram follow limits and safe automation practices. Users asked about following large numbers of accounts, using single-profile URLs, and avoiding bans with JarveePro Instagram automation.

We always emphasized the importance of respecting Instagram’s daily limits, distributing follow operations across multiple accounts, and pacing actions to ensure secure and effective automation. This session highlights how JarveePro’s campaigns, AI Monitor, and multi-account management empower marketers to scale safely and efficiently.

Q1: I can’t follow more than “Suggested accounts.” Why is this happening?

Answer:

This occurs because your campaign is set to follow only one account or one profile URL. Instagram limits the number of follows per account per day, and using a single account for hundreds of follows can trigger bans or restrictions.

Tip:

Use multiple accounts to distribute follow actions.

Follow only a limited number of accounts per day per profile to stay within Instagram’s safe thresholds.

Check JarveePro’s

Instagram Follow Limits Knowledge Base

for official safe practices.

Q2: How many accounts should I use for following 500 profiles?

You should spread follow operations across

several accounts

instead of using just one. The exact number depends on your account’s age, trust level, and activity history.

Start by following a small number of accounts daily on each profile.

Gradually increase volume as accounts demonstrate stability.

Avoid overloading a single profile; doing so can result in temporary blocks or permanent bans.

Q3: Can I follow hundreds of users with a single profile URL?

Answer:

No — following hundreds of users from a single profile URL is unsafe. Instagram monitors follow patterns closely, and excessive activity from one account may trigger restrictions or a ban.

Tip:

Use multiple profiles to distribute the workload.

Pace follow actions realistically to mimic natural human behavior.

Use JarveePro campaigns to automate safely while staying within limits.

Q4: What is the safest way to scale follow campaigns using JarveePro?

Scaling safely requires distributing actions, monitoring performance, and respecting Instagram limits.

Steps:

Add multiple accounts to your campaign.

Set realistic daily follow limits per account.

Schedule actions over several days rather than all at once.

Monitor account health and adjust volume accordingly.

Pro Tip:

Automating gradually ensures all accounts remain healthy and avoids triggering Instagram’s anti-spam algorithms.

Answer:
