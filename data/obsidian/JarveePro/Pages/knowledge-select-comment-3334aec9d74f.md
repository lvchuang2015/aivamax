Select Comment

2025-07-19

Step 1: Open JarveePro→Click Reddit→ Choose"Open Reddit"→Click "OK" to start.

Step 2: Add and select account(s). Then choose search model and add information according to your 3rd step. Then choose number of searching commentand choose type. Lastly, click "Start" to start search. After selecting comment, if you want to perform task, you can click "Fast Task" to start.
