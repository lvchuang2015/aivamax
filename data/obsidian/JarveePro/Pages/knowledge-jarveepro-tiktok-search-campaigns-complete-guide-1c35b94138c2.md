2026-01-26

Introduction

TikTok search campaigns in JarveePro let you

find and engage with users, videos, and live streams efficiently

. Unlike posting campaigns, search campaigns focus entirely on discovery and targeted engagement. They can be run on

one or multiple accounts at once

, giving you scale without linking campaigns directly to an account.

This guide breaks down

all TikTok search campaign types

and how to use them effectively.

1. Search People Campaigns

Campaign Type

Purpose

Best Use Case

Search People by Keywords

Find TikTok users based on usernames, profile descriptions, or keywords.

Identify influencers, competitors’ audiences, or niche-specific accounts.

Search Followers

Find the followers of a specific user or competitor.

Target users already interested in similar content.

Search Followings

Find accounts followed by a specific user or competitor.

Discover active accounts your audience is already following.

Actions After Search:

Follow users automatically

Send DMs (spintax enabled)

Comment on their videos

Add to internal lead list

Tips:

Use filters like follower count, engagement rate, or location for precise targeting.

Rotate search lists to mimic natural discovery behavior.

2. Search Video Campaigns

Campaign Type

Purpose

Best Use Case

Search Video by Keywords

Search TikTok videos by captions, hashtags, or keywords.

Engage with trending content or competitor posts.

Search Video Publisher

Target videos posted by specific users or accounts.

Focus engagement on high-value accounts or influencers.

Search User Videos

Fetch all videos from a specific user.

Monitor competitor or influencer content and engage strategically.

Actions After Search:

Watch videos for X seconds

Like videos automatically

Comment (with spintax)

Share videos to your own account

Tips:

Combine keyword search with engagement actions for growth.

Prioritize recent or trending content for higher visibility.

3. Search Live Campaigns

Campaign Type

Purpose

Best Use Case

Search Live

Find live TikTok sessions by keywords or hashtags.

Engage with live content in your niche.

Search Live Streamer

Target live streamers directly by username.

Interact with specific creators or influencers in real-time.

Actions After Search:

Auto-watch live streams

Comment during live sessions

Send engagement messages to live streamers

Tips:

Live engagement often boosts visibility faster than pre-recorded content.

Rotate search streams to mimic natural browsing behavior.

4. Key Settings Across All Search Campaigns

Filters & Targeting:

Keywords

Behavior Simulation:

Warm up accounts, randomize intervals, rotate accounts

Batch Operations:

Add multiple keywords, users, or hashtags in bulk

Action Automation:

Watch, follow, like, comment, share, message

5. Best Practices

Mix Search Types:

Combine People, Video, and Live campaigns for full-spectrum discovery.

Dynamic Campaigns:

Rotate search queries and target accounts to avoid repetitive patterns.

Monitor Performance:

Track which search types and actions drive the most engagement.

Use Spintax & Personalization:

Make comments and messages appear human and varied.

Focus on Relevance:

Search campaigns work best when keywords, hashtags, and targets match your audience.

Summary

JarveePro TikTok search campaigns are

designed for discovery, engagement, and growth at scale

. By understanding the differences between People, Video, and Live search types, and using automation safely, marketers can

find and interact with the right audience across multiple accounts

efficiently.

Key takeaway:

Accounts and campaigns are separate — you assign accounts to search campaigns, but campaigns themselves are independent. This lets you scale safely without mixing account credentials or behavior.
