Q1: Why am I receiving incorrect verification codes when trying to log into Instagram?

Answer:

This issue usually happens due to sync delays or mismatches between Instagram’s security system and your environment setup. If you're using automation tools like JarveePro, make sure:

Your IP matches your usual login location (avoid sudden geo-changes)

Your device/session is stable (don’t switch proxies mid-login)

SMS delays aren’t causing expired codes

A simple retry often fixes it, as seen in this case—the user successfully logged in shortly after retrying. If the issue persists, switching to email verification or confirming account security settings inside Instagram can help stabilize the process.

Q2: After logging in, how do I start setting up daily Instagram automations?

Once your account is successfully logged into JarveePro, you can begin configuring daily actions like:

Story viewing

Follow/unfollow cycles

Content engagement (likes, comments)

Story interactions

The key is to start

slow

. Think of it like warming up an engine—you don’t go from 0 to 100 instantly. Gradually increase activity limits to mimic human behavior and avoid triggering Instagram restrictions.

Q3: How do I get Instagram Story post URLs?

Answer:

Here’s the reality—Instagram does

not provide direct URLs for Stories

like it does for posts. So trying to scrape “story URLs” directly is a dead end.

Instead, JarveePro takes a smarter route:

It uses

profile URLs

, then accesses Stories from those profiles.

From your setup (like in the screenshot), you’re already in the right place:

Save → Run Task

Q4: If I scrape users in JarveePro, how do I get profile links for Story viewing?

Answer:

This is where JarveePro shines. When you scrape users (from hashtags, followers, or locations), the tool automatically gathers:

Usernames

Profile URLs (linked to each scraped account)

You don’t need to manually extract anything extra. Just:

Run your scraper

Export or directly use the scraped users

Feed them into the Story Viewer or engagement tools

JarveePro will handle accessing their Stories through their profiles—no separate URL needed.
