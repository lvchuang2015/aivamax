## Content

Verify Proxy Validity Directly in Accounts Manager

2026-01-13

Introduction

Managing multiple accounts in JarveePro just got smarter and faster. With the latest update, users can now

verify proxy validity directly within the Accounts Manager

, eliminating the need to navigate to the Proxy Manager. This streamlined approach saves time, reduces steps, and ensures your accounts are always running on valid, reliable proxies. Whether you manage dozens or hundreds of accounts, this feature simplifies proxy oversight and enhances campaign efficiency.

Step-by-Step Guide

Open

Accounts Manager

in JarveePro.

Select the accounts you want to verify.

Click

Proxy --Verify Proxy Availability

The system will check each account’s proxy and display its validity status:

Available

Verifying

, or

Unknown

Adjust or reassign proxies as needed without leaving the Accounts Manager.

Benefits of This Update

Time-Saving:

No need to switch to Proxy Manager for verification.

Better Workflow:

Keep all account-related campaigns centralized.

Increased Reliability:

Quickly identify invalid or problematic proxies to avoid campaign interruptions.

Scalable Management:

Efficiently monitor multiple accounts at once.

Summary

JarveePro’s Accounts Manager now allows direct proxy verification, offering users a faster, more reliable way to ensure all accounts are operating with valid proxies. This update improves efficiency, reduces errors, and makes managing large numbers of accounts smoother than ever.
