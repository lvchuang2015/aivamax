Twitter New Feature: Smart Warmup System

2025-10-26

The new

Twitter Warmup

feature in JarveePro 3.0.3.7 helps accounts behave more naturally before and after posting. It simulates human activity—scrolling, liking, and retweeting—to reduce detection risks and boost account trust.

Key Functions:

Warmup Before Activity:

Automatically performs page scrolls, likes, and retweets before posting.

Warmup After Activity:

Keeps the account active briefly after posting to simulate natural engagement.

Custom Controls:

Adjust warmup time, scroll frequency, and engagement chances per action.

Benefit:

This update improves account safety during scaling and posting campaigns, especially across multiple accounts and GEO regions. It’s ideal for marketers who want to balance automation with human-like behavior.
