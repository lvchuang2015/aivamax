Introduction

This Q&A diary documents a real advanced-user journey covering AI Monitor campaigns, post freshness control, multi-account tracking behavior, YouTube discovery workflows, and infrastructure questions around VPS versus proxies. These are common challenges for users scaling automation across Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube inside JarveePro.

Q1: Why are my accounts liking posts from a year ago? Can I limit likes to new posts only?

Answer:

Yes. To avoid loading and interacting with historical content, you must configure your

AI Monitor Campaign

to monitor

new posts only

. When set correctly, JarveePro will not load or process historical data and will only act on newly detected posts.

Reference:

https://blog.jarveepro.com/knowledge/Daily-Notes/JarveePro-Daily-QA-Diary-Jan-25th,-2026-How-to-Enable-Continuous-Twitter-Monitoring-in-JarveePro/5602

Q2: Where do I set “new posts” versus “all posts”?

This setting is controlled within the

AI Monitor configuration

. When monitoring is enabled for new posts only, the system begins tracking from the moment the campaign starts and ignores past content.

Q3

: Do I need to add YouTube accounts to search for channels or videos?

Answer:

No. YouTube searches in JarveePro do

not

require adding YouTube accounts. Searching for channels or videos can be done directly through the search functionality.

Q4: Why don’t I see YouTube inside Content Explorer?

YouTube discovery does not require YouTube accounts, so you don't see the option to add YouTube accounts in Content Explorer--Account. Instead, YouTube searches are handled directly through search tools.

Q5: Is there a video guide for YouTube search and setup?

Yes. An official walkthrough video is available demonstrating the correct YouTube search workflow and setup process inside JarveePro.

Q6: Do I need a VPS to run YouTube automation?

No. A VPS is

not mandatory

for YouTube automation. Many users successfully operate using proxies alone, depending on their scale and performance needs.

Q7: Is VPS included in the FULL version of JarveePro?

Answer:

No. VPS functionality is not included in the standard FULL version. It is a separate offering.

Q8: What is the pricing for the VPS version?

The 10 VPS version is available at

$3,979

Q9: Are proxies enough for most YouTube use cases?

Yes. For many users, high-quality proxies are sufficient for YouTube automation. VPS is typically considered for higher stability, isolation, or very large-scale operations.

Summary

This case highlights several important operational clarifications:

AI Monitor can be limited to

new posts only

Multi-account tracking behavior may depend on configuration and system updates

YouTube discovery does

not

require YouTube accounts

VPS is optional and not bundled with the FULL version

Proxies alone are often sufficient for YouTube automation

Understanding these distinctions helps users scale automation efficiently without unnecessary infrastructure costs.
