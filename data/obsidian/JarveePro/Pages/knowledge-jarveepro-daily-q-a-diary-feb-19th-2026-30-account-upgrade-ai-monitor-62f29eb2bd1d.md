JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary – Feb 19th, 2026 | 30 Account Upgrade, AI Monitor Pricing & Payment Setup

2026-02-19

Introduction

Today’s Q&A focused on subscription upgrades, payment options, AI Monitor access, keyword-based automation, invoicing, and post-payment setup.

This conversation highlights common questions agencies have when scaling to higher account plans and activating advanced features.

Q1: We want to subscribe to the 30 accounts plan, but there is no card payment option. Can you help?

Answer:

Yes. A direct payment link can be provided to complete the subscription.

If the standard checkout does not show a card option, a manual crypto address can be issued to process the subscription successfully.

Q2: Does the 30 accounts subscription include AI Monitor campaigns?

No.

AI Monitor is a separate feature and requires an additional fee of $2000. It is not included in the standard 30 accounts subscription plan.

Q3: Can we use a new email for the subscription even if we didn’t register for the free license first?

Yes.

You can use the new email address during payment. That email will be used to receive the license and access credentials. A free license registration is not required beforehand.

Q4: Can we run automation without a specific post URL? Can we use keywords instead?

Yes, but the workflow is different.

You must first search and collect posts using keywords inside

Advanced Content Explorer

Keyword input is not inserted in the automation action itself. It is configured inside the Content Explorer module, where you generate and filter target posts before applying automation.

Q5: If we upgrade to 30 accounts, can we use the platform fully?

Answer:

Upgrading to the 30 accounts plan allows you to operate and manage up to 30 accounts under the platform infrastructure.

Q6: Can invoices be sent automatically to email?

Invoices can be automatically delivered to the registered subscription email.

Q7: Payment was successful. What’s the next step?

After successful payment:

Confirm the email address to receive the license.

The license is issued and sent to that email.

Log in and begin account setup inside the dashboard.

In this case, the license was successfully sent and received.
