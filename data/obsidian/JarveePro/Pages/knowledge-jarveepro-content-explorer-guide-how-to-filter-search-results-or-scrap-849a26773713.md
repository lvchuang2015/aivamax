JarveePro Content Explorer Guide – How to Filter Search Results or Scraped Data

2026-03-16

Introduction

When collecting posts, users, or engagement data with

JarveePro Content Explorer

, the raw search results can sometimes be too broad. For example, a keyword search may return hundreds of posts with different engagement levels, languages, or content types.

To help marketers focus on

relevant, high-quality targets

, JarveePro includes a powerful

Filter Options

system inside the Content Explorer. These filters allow users to refine scraped data based on keywords, engagement metrics, language, date, and more.

This guide explains how to use the

Filter Options panel

to narrow down results and keep only the content that fits your campaign.

Q1: Where can I find the Filter Options in JarveePro Content Explorer?

Answer:

Inside the

Content Explorer task creation window

, there is a

Filter Options → Settings

button.

After clicking it, a filtering panel appears where you can define conditions such as:

Keywords

Date range

Likes

Shares

Comments

Word count

Language

These filters help remove irrelevant results and keep only the posts or users you want.

Q2: How does the Filter Keywords option work?

Answer:

The

Filter Keywords

field allows you to filter results based on specific words appearing in the content.

How to use it:

Enter keywords separated by commas.

Example:

discount, giveaway, crypto, marketing

After entering keywords, you can choose:

Keep matched content

– Only results containing these keywords will remain.

Discard matched content

– Results containing these keywords will be removed.

This is useful for excluding spam or focusing on specific niches.

Q3: What does the Filter Date option do?

Answer:

The

Filter Date

option allows you to keep content published within a certain time period.

This helps users focus on

recent or trending posts

rather than older content.

Typical use cases include:

Monitoring current marketing trends

Finding fresh engagement opportunities

Tracking new posts from competitors

Q4: Can I filter posts by engagement metrics like likes, shares, or comments?

Yes. JarveePro Content Explorer supports filtering based on engagement metrics.

Available filters include:

Filter Likes

Filter Shares

Filter Comments

For example, you can configure filters such as:

Likes

greater than 500

Comments

greater than 20

Shares

greater than 50

These filters help identify

high-performing posts

or remove low-engagement content.

Q5: What is the Filter Word Count option used for?

Answer:

The

Word Count filter

allows you to control the length of the content you collect.

Examples:

Filter posts with

more than 50 words

Exclude short posts or spam

Focus on long-form or detailed posts

This is useful when analyzing

quality content or longer discussions

Q6: How does the Language Filter work?

Language Filter

allows you to select specific languages for the content results.

After selecting a language, you can choose:

Keep matched content

– Only content in the selected language will be kept.

Discard matched content

– Content in that language will be removed.

This feature is particularly useful when running campaigns in

specific geographic markets

Q7: What happens after configuring filters?

Answer:

After setting your filters, click

Save

to apply them.

JarveePro will then:

Process the search results

Apply the filter conditions

Remove unwanted results

Keep only the data matching your criteria

This ensures your automation campaigns use

clean, targeted data

Summary

The

Content Explorer Filter Options

in JarveePro provide powerful ways to refine scraped data and search results.

With filters such as keywords, engagement metrics, language, and word count, users can quickly remove irrelevant results and focus on the most valuable targets.

Key benefits include:

Cleaner datasets

More precise targeting

Improved automation campaign performance

Better content monitoring and research

Using these filters effectively can significantly improve the efficiency of social media automation workflows.
