Watch Videos by Link

2025-05-23

Step 1: Choose "Campaigns" and Facebook, and select task.

Step 2: Select account(s) and add multiple video links you want to watch. You can import multiple links in CSV format or add them one by one.

Step 3:Comments shared while watching the video.

Step 4: Complete task settings and click "OK" to start.

What This Page Does:

This tab is to configure how many Facebook videos each account watches, how many views each video receives, and whether the account likes, follows, or shares the video after a certain watch time.

If You Want to Get 1 View on a Video from Each Account:

Select “Number of Views per Video”

Set it: From 1 to 1 — this means each video will receive one view from each selected account.

✅ Enabled in current settings.

Set “Number of Videos to Watch per Account”

Set it: From 1 to 1 — each account will only watch one video.

✅ This ensures focused engagement per account.

Optional Actions After Watching:

✅ Like

Check the “Like” option if you want accounts to like the video.

Set “Like Video at”: 30 seconds — the like will be triggered after the video has been playing for 30 seconds.

Choose Like Type: Currently set to 👍 (Like).

Other options include ❤️ (Love), 😆 (Haha), 😯 (Wow), 😢 (Sad), 😡 (Angry), or Random.

✅ Follow

Check the “Follow” option if you want accounts to follow the video creator.

Set “Follow Video Owner At”: 0 Hours 10 Minutes 15 Seconds — follow action will occur 10 minutes and 15 seconds after the video begins playing.

Helps simulate genuine follower behavior with delayed action.

✅ Share

Check the “Share” option if you want accounts to share the video.

Set “Share Video After”: 60 seconds playing — accounts will share the video after watching for one minute.

Real-World Example Setup for New Users:

Scenario: Simulate Full Engagement on One Facebook Video

In Select Accounts Tab: Choose the number of Facebook accounts you want to use (e.g., 100 accounts).

In Video URLs Tab: Add the link(s) of the video(s) you want to promote.

In Task Settings Tab:

Set Number of Views per Video: From 1 to 1

Set Number of Videos to Watch per Account: From 1 to 1

✅ Enable Like at 30 seconds with 👍 Like type

✅ Enable Follow video owner at 10 minutes and 15 seconds

✅ Enable Share after 60 seconds of watching

This setup ensures each video gets one full view per account, followed by a Like, Follow, and Share to simulate organic interaction patterns.
