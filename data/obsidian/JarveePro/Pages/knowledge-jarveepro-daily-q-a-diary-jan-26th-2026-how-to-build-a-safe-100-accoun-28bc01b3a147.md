Q5. Meta Accounts Center Linking (Chain Ban Risk)

Official recommendation for farms:

DO NOT link accounts in Meta Accounts Center

Why:

Meta treats Accounts Center as

explicit ownership confirmation

If one account is flagged → linked accounts are reviewed together

Brand setups ≠ farm setups

Best practice:

Isolate all accounts

No Accounts Center linking

Even within same proxy group

Especially dangerous for niche or disposable accounts

Verdict:

For farms →

leave phone field empty

If accounts share a phone number:

devices must stay unique
