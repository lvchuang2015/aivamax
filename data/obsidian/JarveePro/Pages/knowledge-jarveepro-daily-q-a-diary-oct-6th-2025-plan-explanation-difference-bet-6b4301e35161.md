JarveePro Daily Q&A Diary – Oct. 6th, 2025(Plan Explanation & Difference Between PVA Creator and JarveePro)

2025-10-06

Introduction

Every day, we receive a wide range of user questions about how to maximize automation performance with JarveePro. Today’s topics cover account allocation, the meaning of “1 account with 4 sites,” and the difference between

PVA Creator

and

JarveePro

. If you’re managing multiple platforms like Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube, this Q&A will help you understand how to integrate and optimize your workflow effectively.

Q1: What does “1 account with 4 sites” mean in JarveePro (Individual Lifetime Version)?

Answer:

This refers to one

user license

that supports

automation across four different social media sites

. For example, one JarveePro account can manage automation for:

Facebook

Instagram

Twitter (X)

YouTube

Each site can have multiple accounts under the same license, allowing you to control posting, scheduling, engagement, and messaging across multiple platforms—all from one unified dashboard.

Q2: What’s the difference between PVA Creator and JarveePro?

Feature

PVA Creator

JarveePro

Purpose

Creates and verifies new accounts automatically (PVA = Phone Verified Account)

Manages, automates, and scales marketing across social media platforms

Functionality

Focused on account registration and verification

Full social automation: posting, commenting, following, DMs, tracking

Usage Stage

Used

before

automation (account creation stage)

after

account creation (operation and management stage)

Platform Support

Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, TikTok, Gmail, and more

All major platforms plus integration with custom API modules

License Type

One-time

Monthly, Yearly or lifetime vailable

Integration

Exports ready-to-use verified accounts

Imports and manages those accounts for marketing actions

In short:

Use PVA Creator

to

generate and verify accounts

safely.

Use JarveePro

automate posting, grow audiences, and manage engagement

across those accounts.

Quick Example

You can first use

PVA Creator

to generate 10 verified Instagram accounts.

Then, import those accounts into

JarveePro

to schedule daily posts, follow users, like content, and reply to DMs automatically.

Summary

“1 account with 4 sites” = one license for automation on Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube.

PVA Creator is for

creating

accounts.

JarveePro is for

running

and

automating

them.

Together, they form a powerful toolkit for digital marketers, SMM panel operators, and agencies managing hundreds of accounts.
