Repost（New Features）

2021-03-08

How do we repost on Instagram with JarveePro?

Step 1: Choose

Facebook Tasks

and then click

Add

. Name your task and choose corresponding module. Click

OK

in the end.

Step 2 Select the accounts you wanna use and finish the rest settings.

You can paste or import post URLs here.

There are 3 ways to grab post URL. If you are not familiar with these features, you can try to use

Search Posts

first.

Step 3 : Click

Ok

on the bottom right corner and run the task.
