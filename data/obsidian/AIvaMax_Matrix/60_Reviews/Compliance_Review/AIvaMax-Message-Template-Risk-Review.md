# AIvaMax Message Template Risk Review

## Review Checklist

- The message follows a real interaction or explicit user interest.
- The first line refers to the user's context, not a generic pitch.
- The CTA is soft and optional.
- Claims are specific and verifiable.
- No fake scarcity, impersonation, scraped personal data, or platform-limit evasion.
- The next step can be handled by a human reviewer.

## Public Course Policy

Public lessons may show principles, safe examples, and review rubrics. Internal message libraries stay internal until reviewed.
