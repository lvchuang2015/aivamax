# SOP-AIVAMAX-Lead-Generation-Risk-Rewrite

## Rule

Comment and message templates are not public course copy until they are rewritten into a value-first, context-aware, human-reviewed flow.

## Rewrite Standard

| Source pattern | Public course rewrite |
| --- | --- |
| Bulk comment template | Teach comment diagnosis and value-response principles |
| Automatic direct message | Teach opt-in follow-up after meaningful interaction |
| Competitor audience capture | Teach market listening and ethical audience research |
| Multi-account scaling | Teach account roles, content separation, review, and pause rules |

## Approved Public Framing

- Start with useful context.
- Mention the next step only after the user shows interest.
- Keep claims modest and verifiable.
- Move sensitive replies to human review.
- Log every conversion path for weekly review.
