Module 01: Social Automation Growth Map

Module 01: Social Automation Growth Map

Teaching Goal

Help students understand the complete AIvaMax growth system: project input, account matrix, platform weighting, content system, engagement workflow, review, and delivery pack.

Learning Outcomes

  • Explain the module decision model in plain language.
  • Complete the matching AIvaMax worksheet.
  • Identify what is public-course content, internal SOP content, and client-delivery content.
  • Apply the review gate before using the output in a real project.

Production Output

`AIvaMax Growth System Map`

Lesson Plan - Module 01: Social Automation Growth Map

Teaching Goal

Help students understand the complete AIvaMax growth system: project input, account matrix, platform weighting, content system, engagement workflow, review, and delivery pack.

Lesson Flow

| Segment | Teaching point | Instructor action |

| --- | --- | --- |

| 0-5 min | Explain the growth system as a workflow, not a tool trick | Draw the flow from intake to review |

| 5-15 min | Separate strategy, content, execution, and review | Use one sample product and mark each layer |

| 15-25 min | Show why account, platform, and content decisions must be made together | Compare weak plan vs structured plan |

| 25-35 min | Introduce review gates and public/private boundaries | Mark which details are public course vs internal SOP |

| 35-45 min | Convert the full system into a project checklist | Have students complete the checklist |

Instructor Demo

Use one realistic project brief and show how this module changes the operating decision. Keep the demo focused on method, worksheet completion, and review gates.

Instructor Guide - Module 01: Social Automation Growth Map

Facilitation Notes

  • Start from the project brief, not from the tool or task list.
  • Keep students focused on the decision model and final output.
  • Ask students to explain why their output is appropriate for the audience, channel, and risk level.
  • Require a review owner when the output affects client communication, public claims, or follow-up messaging.

Discussion Prompts
