# PB-AIVAMAX-Goal-Lead-Generation-30D

## Positioning

Use this playbook when the main goal is qualified inquiry, demo booking, free-trial signup, or partner conversation.

## 30-Day Rhythm

| Phase | Days | Goal | Core work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | 1-3 | Prepare accounts, assets, keywords | Intake, account roles, content topics |
| Content launch | 4-10 | Build baseline visibility | Publish reviewed posts and videos |
| Interaction test | 11-20 | Find responsive audiences | Comment, monitor, research, record |
| Conversion path | 21-27 | Guide interested users | Human-reviewed follow-up and landing path |
| Review | 28-30 | Decide scale or revise | Stop weak tasks, double down on working angles |

## Required Outputs

- Account matrix
- Platform weight table
- Weekly content calendar
- Lead qualification table
- Risk review notes
