# AIvaMax Content System And Calendar

## Purpose

This media plan turns positioning, offers, and platform weights into a repeatable weekly content calendar.

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | Job | Example format | Review focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem education | Make the audience feel understood | Short post, carousel, article | Specific pain and credible framing |
| Method explanation | Show the system behind the result | Walkthrough, checklist, mini lesson | Clear steps and no exaggerated promise |
| Proof and cases | Build trust with observable evidence | Case note, before/after, metric recap | Evidence source and claim strength |
| Offer bridge | Move attention to a next step | CTA post, lead magnet, consultation prompt | Low-pressure and platform-appropriate |
| Community response | Turn comments and replies into insight | FAQ, reply post, objection response | Human tone and risk review |

## Calendar Rules

- Every content item must map to one audience stage: cold, warm, interested, or customer.
- Every CTA must have a destination and a manual review owner.
- Content that references results must include evidence strength: observed, estimated, or illustrative.
- Conversation templates are stored in lead generation SOPs and reviewed before public use.
