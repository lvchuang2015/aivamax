# Lesson Plan - Module 09: Campaign Naming And Structure

## Teaching Goal

Teach students to make naming and campaign structure searchable, auditable, and reusable.

## Lesson Flow

| Segment | Teaching point | Instructor action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 min | Bad names destroy review and reuse | Show ambiguous names and their failure modes |
| 5-15 min | Explain the AIvaMax naming pattern | Break down client, platform, goal, stage, week, owner |
| 15-25 min | Map names to campaign, account group, task, and calendar | Build the hierarchy |
| 25-35 min | Add version and review status | Show draft, approved, active, paused, archived |
| 35-45 min | Rename a messy campaign set | Students apply the naming standard |

## Instructor Demo

Use one realistic project brief and show how this module changes the operating decision. Keep the demo focused on method, worksheet completion, and review gates.
