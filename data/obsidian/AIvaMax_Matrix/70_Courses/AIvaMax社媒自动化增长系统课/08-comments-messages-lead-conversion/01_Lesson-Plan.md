# Lesson Plan - Module 08: Comments, Messages, And Lead Conversion

## Teaching Goal

Teach students to turn interactions into qualified next steps without using aggressive or context-free communication.

## Lesson Flow

| Segment | Teaching point | Instructor action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 min | Lead conversion starts with context | Define qualified interaction |
| 5-15 min | Comment replies should add value before asking | Rewrite a generic reply into a helpful reply |
| 15-25 min | Messages require consent, relevance, and human review | Show approval criteria |
| 25-35 min | Build a lead signal table | Score interest, fit, urgency, and next step |
| 35-45 min | Route leads to a human-owned follow-up | Define owner and response SLA |
