# Lesson Plan - Module 10: Data Review And Forecasting

## Teaching Goal

Teach students to use data for decisions without turning forecasts into promises.

## Lesson Flow

| Segment | Teaching point | Instructor action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 min | Forecasts are planning ranges | Explain conservative, expected, and upside ranges |
| 5-15 min | Separate activity, response, lead signal, and conversion readiness | Build the review table |
| 15-25 min | Use assumptions explicitly | Show content volume, response rate, signal rate, risk adjustment |
| 25-35 min | Decide what to scale or stop | Use a sample weekly review |
| 35-45 min | Write the next-week operating priority | Force one clear priority |

## Instructor Demo

Use one realistic project brief and show how this module changes the operating decision. Keep the demo focused on method, worksheet completion, and review gates.

## Public/Private Boundary

Public teaching may include frameworks, worksheets, examples, and review logic. Internal execution details stay in SOPs until they pass risk review.
