# Lesson Plan - Module 07: Content System And Calendar

## Teaching Goal

Teach students to build a content system from audience pain, platform format, trust path, and CTA.

## Lesson Flow

| Segment | Teaching point | Instructor action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 min | Content calendar starts from jobs, not dates | Define content jobs: educate, prove, compare, invite, answer |
| 5-15 min | Build content pillars from pain points | Convert intake pain into five content pillars |
| 15-25 min | Match content formats to platforms | Map short post, thread, carousel, video, article, FAQ |
| 25-35 min | Build a weekly rhythm | Create a 5-day content calendar |
| 35-45 min | Add review and claim strength | Label claims as observed, estimated, or illustrative |
