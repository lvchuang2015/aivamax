# Lesson Plan - Module 11: Risk Boundaries And Human Review

## Teaching Goal

Teach students to separate automation-friendly work from human-owned decisions and sensitive communication.

## Lesson Flow

| Segment | Teaching point | Instructor action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 min | Risk boundary is part of the product | Explain why review increases quality |
| 5-15 min | Mark public, internal, client, and sales-preview versions | Use the audience versioning matrix |
| 15-25 min | Identify red flags | Review unsupported claims, impersonation, context-free messaging |
| 25-35 min | Add release gates | Define review owner, evidence, rewrite, approval |
| 35-45 min | Audit one module or client deliverable | Students mark what must be rewritten |

## Instructor Demo

Use one realistic project brief and show how this module changes the operating decision. Keep the demo focused on method, worksheet completion, and review gates.

## Public/Private Boundary

Public teaching may include frameworks, worksheets, examples, and review logic. Internal execution details stay in SOPs until they pass risk review.
