# Lesson Plan - Module 04: Platform Selection And Weighting

## Teaching Goal

Teach students to choose platforms by audience behavior, content format, trust path, and conversion path.

## Lesson Flow

| Segment | Teaching point | Instructor action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 min | Platform choice is resource allocation | Show why "post everywhere" fails |
| 5-15 min | Define each platform's job | Map platforms to awareness, trust, community, search, and conversion |
| 15-25 min | Apply industry weight patterns | Compare SaaS, ecommerce, local service, education, and AI tools |
| 25-35 min | Convert weights into content and review workload | Translate percentages into weekly effort |
| 35-45 min | Set one primary and one secondary platform | Require a decision, not a generic list |

## Instructor Demo

Use one realistic project brief and show how this module changes the operating decision. Keep the demo focused on method, worksheet completion, and review gates.
