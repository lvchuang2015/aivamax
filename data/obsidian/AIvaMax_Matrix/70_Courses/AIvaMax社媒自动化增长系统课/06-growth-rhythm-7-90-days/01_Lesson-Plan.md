# Lesson Plan - Module 06: 7/14/30/60/90 Day Growth Rhythm

## Teaching Goal

Teach students to stage growth work into cycles with clear outputs and review gates.

## Lesson Flow

| Segment | Teaching point | Instructor action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 min | Different cycles answer different questions | Explain test, validate, standardize, expand, productize |
| 5-15 min | 7-day cycle tests readiness and angles | Build a Day 1-7 plan |
| 15-25 min | 14 and 30-day cycles test repeatability | Show platform, content, and lead-signal checkpoints |
| 25-35 min | 60 and 90-day cycles build assets and SOPs | Convert winning actions into reusable assets |
| 35-45 min | Create the next review decision | Decide continue, adjust, pause, or scale |

## Instructor Demo

Use one realistic project brief and show how this module changes the operating decision. Keep the demo focused on method, worksheet completion, and review gates.

## Public/Private Boundary

Public teaching may include frameworks, worksheets, examples, and review logic. Internal execution details stay in SOPs until they pass risk review.
