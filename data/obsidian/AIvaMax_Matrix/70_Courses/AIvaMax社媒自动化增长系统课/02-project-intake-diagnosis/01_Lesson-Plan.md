# Lesson Plan - Module 02: Project Intake And Diagnosis

## Teaching Goal

Teach students to collect enough project information before proposing channels, content, or execution.

## Lesson Flow

| Segment | Teaching point | Instructor action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 min | Bad inputs create bad growth plans | Show a vague brief and identify missing fields |
| 5-15 min | Diagnose product, audience, market, proof, assets, and goal | Walk through the intake schema |
| 15-25 min | Convert customer pain into content and CTA direction | Build a pain-to-content table |
| 25-35 min | Score readiness: offer, proof, assets, compliance, tracking | Use red/yellow/green readiness labels |
| 35-45 min | Decide whether the project is ready for a 7, 14, 30, 60, or 90 day cycle | Choose a cycle based on evidence |

## Instructor Demo

Use one realistic project brief and show how this module changes the operating decision. Keep the demo focused on method, worksheet completion, and review gates.

## Public/Private Boundary

Public teaching may include frameworks, worksheets, examples, and review logic. Internal execution details stay in SOPs until they pass risk review.
