# Platform Weights

## Recommended Split

| Platform | Weight | Job | Content format | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | 35% | B2B trust, founder credibility, lead path | Founder post, carousel, case note, comment response | Book demo, download checklist |
| X | 25% | Opinion, quick education, topical reach | Short post, thread, reply, teardown | Read thread, visit demo page |
| Reddit | 15% | Pain discovery and honest discussion | Helpful answer, workflow explanation | Share checklist only when relevant |
| YouTube | 15% | Searchable proof and product education | Short demo, tutorial, workflow walkthrough | Watch demo, book call |
| Facebook | 10% | Small business groups and community presence | Group post, FAQ, case snippet | Free workflow audit |

## Why This Split

LinkedIn and X carry the main B2B discovery and trust path. Reddit is used for pain discovery and careful community contribution. YouTube supports proof and search. Facebook is a secondary channel for small business group learning.

## Adjustment Rule

After Day 14, move 10-15% effort away from channels with low-quality engagement and into the strongest lead-signal channel.
