# Strategy Plan

## Positioning

ACME-AI helps small B2B teams replace repetitive workflow handoffs with simple AI-assisted operating systems.

## Growth Hypothesis

The fastest path to qualified lead signals is not broad awareness. It is targeted education around costly manual workflows, followed by low-pressure proof and demo invitations.

## 30-Day Goals

| Goal | Success signal |
| --- | --- |
| Validate audience pain | Comments, replies, saves, and profile visits from target roles |
| Validate content angle | At least 3 content angles with above-average engagement quality |
| Validate lead path | Demo clicks, checklist requests, or qualified replies |
| Build reusable assets | Topic bank, calendar, lead review sheet, weekly forecast |

## Campaign Structure

| Stage | Days | Focus | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Setup | 1-3 | Brief, account readiness, platform weights | Approved project plan |
| Content launch | 4-10 | Educational and pain-driven content | Initial response signals |
| Engagement review | 11-20 | Contextual replies and lead signal scoring | Qualified interaction list |
| Conversion support | 21-27 | Proof, FAQ, demo bridge | Demo-ready lead list |
| Review | 28-30 | Performance review and next-cycle plan | 30-day report |

## Operating Principle

The project should optimize for signal quality first, volume second.
