# Review And Forecast

## Weekly Review Table

| Metric | Week 1 target | Week 2 target | Week 3 target | Week 4 target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Published assets | 5-7 | 6-8 | 6-8 | 5-7 |
| Helpful replies | 5-10 | 10-15 | 15-20 | 10-15 |
| Profile visits | establish baseline | +10-20% | +10-25% | maintain or improve |
| Lead signals | 1-3 | 3-6 | 5-10 | 5-12 |
| Qualified conversations | 0-1 | 1-2 | 2-4 | 2-5 |
| Risk events | 0 unresolved | 0 unresolved | 0 unresolved | 0 unresolved |

## Forecast Ranges

| Scenario | Expected condition | Lead signal range | Qualified conversation range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conservative | New profiles, limited proof, low initial response | 5-10 | 1-3 |
| Expected | Clear content angles and consistent review | 12-25 | 4-8 |
| Upside | Strong founder content, clear demo, timely follow-up | 25-45 | 8-15 |

## Decision Rules

- Scale a content angle only after it produces repeated high-quality engagement.
- Reduce platform effort if the audience is present but not responding.
- Do not count vague likes as leads.
- Treat replies, demo clicks, checklist requests, and booking intent as stronger signals.
- Lower the forecast if claim review or response review is delayed.

## 30-Day Review Output

The final report should include:

- strongest audience segment,
- strongest platform,
- top 3 content angles,
- lead signal summary,
- qualified conversation summary,
- risk events and resolutions,
- 60-day recommendation.
