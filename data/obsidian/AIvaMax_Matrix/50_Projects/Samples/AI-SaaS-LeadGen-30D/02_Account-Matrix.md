# Account Matrix

## Account Roles

| Role | Platform | Job | Content focus | Owner | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brand account | LinkedIn, X | Authority and conversion path | Product clarity, customer pain, demos | Client marketing | low |
| Founder account | LinkedIn, X | Trust and point of view | Industry insight, founder lessons, workflow opinions | Client founder | medium |
| Product education account | YouTube, LinkedIn | Teach workflow examples | Demo clips, tutorials, checklists | Client product | low |
| Community response role | Reddit, LinkedIn | Understand pain and answer questions | Helpful comments, non-promotional replies | AIvaMax operator | medium |
| Support/consultation role | LinkedIn, website | Route qualified interest | FAQ, booking, checklist delivery | Client sales | medium |

## Review Rules

- Founder posts need client approval.
- Community replies must answer the question before mentioning next steps.
- Any direct follow-up must be opt-in and reviewed.
- Case or ROI claims require evidence notes.

## Minimum Weekly Output

| Role | Weekly output |
| --- | --- |
| Brand account | 3 posts, 1 proof note, 1 CTA post |
| Founder account | 2 point-of-view posts, 3 thoughtful replies |
| Product education account | 1 demo clip, 1 tutorial post |
| Community response role | 10 reviewed helpful replies |
| Support/consultation role | Review qualified replies within 24 hours |
