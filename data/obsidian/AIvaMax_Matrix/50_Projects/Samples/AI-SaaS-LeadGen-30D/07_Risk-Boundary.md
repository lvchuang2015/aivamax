# Risk Boundary

## Client-Facing Rules

| Area | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Claims | Use observed facts, examples, or ranges. Do not promise guaranteed leads or revenue. |
| Comments | Add context and value before any CTA. Avoid repetitive templates. |
| Messages | Use opt-in, relevant, human-approved follow-up only. |
| Community posts | Respect community norms and answer the question directly. |
| Proof | Case notes must be supported by client-approved evidence. |
| Forecast | Present ranges and assumptions, not guarantees. |

## Review Owners

| Area | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Product claims | Client product owner |
| Founder posts | Client founder |
| Community replies | AIvaMax delivery owner |
| Follow-up messages | Client sales owner |
| Final report | AIvaMax delivery owner |

## Blocked Content

- Unsupported ROI claims.
- Context-free direct outreach.
- Messages that imply a prior relationship where none exists.
- Community replies that are mainly promotional.
- Any internal source trace, private path, or operator-only note.
